## 1 · India ETF → Ashoka India Equity (AIE.L) — ✅ THE WINNER (proceed-leaning, ~3.5/5)

**Why the bull edges it:** The two headline claims VERIFY against primary/independent sources — **+82.6ppt alpha since 2018 (170.7% vs 88.1%), and that alpha is 100% stock-selection** (Kepler attribution), *not* a replicable small-cap factor tilt — which is the one thing that justifies paying for active over passive. **No base management fee; base OCF ~0.2% is LOWER than the ETF's ~0.65–0.80% in every year**; the performance fee (30% of excess vs index, capped 12% NAV, **paid in shares with ≥50% locked 3yrs**) is genuine owner-aligned, mission-DNA economics. Buyable in a T212 ISA (LSE trust, FTSE 250), liquid at a 6% weight, currently at a rare ~2.5% discount (historically a premium) = neutral-to-favourable entry.

**The bear's real, must-acknowledge caveats:** (a) the lead has *narrowed* recently — FY2024 and H2-2025 actually **lagged** the index; (b) a **fresh co-manager (Hiren Dasani, ex-GS) joined Aug-2025** — ambiguous (bench-depth vs key-man dilution); (c) SPIVA base rate (~91% of EM active funds lose over 10yr) = some performance-chasing flavour; (d) you'd add active/SMID-beta/discount-volatility into the book's *only* diversifier slot.

**Net:** A genuine quality upgrade that keeps the India theme and *upgrades* the slot from a no-alignment passive basket to an owner-aligned, governance-screening active moat — for a **lower base fee**. It is the rare "active over passive" case that survives adversarial scrutiny. **Disciplined execution:** do it, or (the bear's margin-of-safety version) wait for a wider discount (≥ −10%) and require a couple of post-Dasani years confirming the process still beats the index. Not a no-brainer — but the clear best of the four.

## 2 · Eurofins → UL Solutions / Lancashire — ❌ KEEP EUROFINS

**Bear wins decisively on the swap.** The killer fact: **UL Solutions is literally buying Eurofins' own E&E testing unit (~€575m, ~14.5x EBITDA)** while *all* of Eurofins trades at **~9x EV/EBITDA**. So ERF→ULS = sell the seller, buy the buyer at **~2.5x the multiple (~22x vs ~9x)**, for the *same TIC factor* — duplication, not diversification. And the deal is itself a re-rating *tell* for Eurofins. Eurofins is cheap, mid-recovery, with the Muddy-Waters overhang defused (Sept-2025 buy-in) and a reconfirmed 2027–28 FCF inflection — selling the smallest 3% slot now is selling low into the catalyst it was bought for.

**The one thing that survives:** the bull's insurance point. Lancashire (20.9% ROE, ~1.26x book) — or **IGIC** (18.6% ROE, 41% founder-owned, less cyclical) — is a legitimate idea to **fill the book's zero-insurance blind spot**, but as a *new add funded from a marginal trim elsewhere*, NOT as an ERF swap (and it's a cyclical bought into a softening rate market — bear rated it ~85% unjustified as a swap). **Park "do we want an insurance sleeve?" as its own question.**

## 3 · Bioventix → Judges Scientific / PharmaNutra — ❌ KEEP BIOVENTIX

**Both sides converge** — even the bull rated *swapping at all* only **2.5/5**. Bioventix's ~78% op margin / ~67% ROE / net cash / **5–6% growing yield ("paid to wait")** plus the **free Alzheimer's/Tau option that is inflecting right now** (neuro royalties ~5× to ~£150k; 3 Tau antibodies into large-scale production on Beckman/Quanterix platforms) are real assets you forfeit. The 2032 troponin cliff is overstated (now ~11% of revenue, fully telegraphed; vitamin-D has no cliff).

**If the niche-monopoly slot is ever upgraded, Judges Scientific (JDG) is the candidate** (bull 4/5 JDG-over-PharmaNutra): certain AIM buyability, a proven ~25%-ROIC serial compounder at a cyclical-trough multiple (~11x normalised), clean founder succession. **But two real brakes:** (a) JDG's discount is *earned* by a live NIH/US-academic-funding shock (US orders −22%, EPS guided down ~18% for 2026) — a falling knife, not a clean compounder on sale; (b) adding JDG **increases correlated lab-/research-funding-cycle risk** the book already carries (Bachem/Sartorius/E&Z/Eurofins). **PharmaNutra:** lovely business + real WHO-2025 catalyst, but **unconfirmed T212 buyability (Euronext Growth Milan junior segment — verify in-app)** and a full ~38x multiple that removes the downside floor → a *new-money/watchlist* idea, not a BVXP replacement.
