## GEM 1: NOVANTA INC.
**Novanta Inc. | NOVT | Nasdaq | ~$5.2-5.8B market cap | Established compounder with robotics inflection**

### Wave + why under-covered
Novanta is the only public company owning the complete "motion stack" for humanoid robot joints: (a) ATI Industrial Automation — the dominant force/torque sensor maker for robot wrists/ankles (~30+ years of spec-in; listed at ati.novanta.com; "F/T specialists ATI, FUTEK, and Bota Systems bundle calibration scripts for ROS2, trimming start-up time from weeks to days"); (b) Celera Motion — IncOder inductive encoders (non-contact, humanoid-grade), frameless BLDC motors, servo drives; (c) IMS (Integrated Motion Systems) — servo drives and motion controllers.

Why under-covered: the company's dual identity (medical + industrial automation) makes it invisible to pure-play robotics investors, while its ~$1B revenue makes it too small for generalist large-cap funds. It is classified as "medical technology" in most databases, hiding the robotics exposure. Over 47% of 2025 revenue is now "advanced industrial" including robotics/automation. Analyst coverage: ~12 firms (estimated).

Q1 2026 inflection signal: Robotics and Automation bookings +50% YoY; overall bookings +37% YoY (1.10 book-to-bill ratio). Full-year 2026 guidance raised. ATI is explicitly cited by management as winning humanoid-robot F/T sensor orders.

### Moat
- ATI force/torque dominance: 30-year installed base + ROS2 integration = spec-in switching cost moat. ATI sensors are specified by name at OEM design stage for cobots, surgical robots (Intuitive, Stryker), and humanoids.
- Celera Motion IncOder: non-contact inductive angle encoder designed for harsh environments in robot joints. Spec-in at joint design stage creates multi-year supply lock-in.
- Full motion-stack integration: Novanta can supply the full motor + encoder + drive + F/T sensor as a reference design, removing integration risk for humanoid OEMs — decisive at early ramp stage when engineering resources are scarce.
- Medical stickiness: ~53% of revenue is medical (surgical robotics, radiation therapy) — providing a high-recurring-revenue base with 5-7yr device replacement cycles that buffers industrial cyclicality.

## GEM 2: VISHAY PRECISION GROUP
**VPG | NYSE | ~$1.5-1.7B market cap | Most specialist, pure-play tactile sensing**

### Wave + why under-covered
VPG makes precision foil-technology force sensors — the most accurate resistive force-measurement technology in the world (TCR ≤ 0.2 ppm/°C vs. 50-200 ppm/°C for semiconductor strain gauges). These sensors are the "tactile nervous system" of industrial and humanoid robots — measuring six-axis force and torque in robot wrists, ankles, and grippers, enabling force-compliant assembly, safe human-robot interaction (collision detection in <1 ms), and dexterous manipulation.

Why under-covered: VPG is classified as "electronic components"; it's a $1.5B micro-cap with only 2 sell-side analysts; its foil-technology moat is understood only by precision-measurement engineers; and the humanoid revenue was only $4M in 2025 — below any standard screen threshold. The Q1 2026 earnings call was the first time humanoid orders became a live public topic.

Q1 2026 inflection signal: $600K product shipped to humanoid makers in Q1; "more than double" in Q2 guidance (implying $1.2M+ Q2 2026); Q1 bookings $102.1M (3rd highest in company history, +25.5% sequential); company modelling 50% annual humanoid revenue growth from $4M 2025 base. CEO confirmed analyst math of $5-6M in 2026 and "low-teens millions" by 2027-2028.

TAM: Global torque sensor for humanoid robot market: $515M in 2024 projected to $7.9B by 2032 (~40% CAGR per Intel Market Research). VPG's addressable slice (high-precision foil-based for premium humanoids and surgical robots) is smaller but defensible.

### Moat
- Bulk Metal Foil technology (proprietary, Zandman foil): TCR ≤ 0.2 ppm/°C vs. 50-500 ppm/°C for alternatives — 250-2,500x more stable over temperature. For humanoid robots operating in varying thermal environments, this precision advantage is genuinely irreplaceable for high-specification applications.
- Multi-decade spec-in moat: VPG sensors designed into aircraft avionics, space hardware (NASA-certified), nuclear reactors — environments where recertification costs exceed switching savings by orders of magnitude. Same dynamic now applying to humanoid OEMs qualifying their F/T sensors.
- Dual-class Zandman family control: Class B shares (10 votes each) held via Zandman family trust with CEO Ziv Shoshani as co-trustee = ~28.5% aggregate voting power. Protects long-duration strategic independence.

### Founder/Insider Ownership
- Ziv Shoshani (CEO since 2010 spin-off): ~164,647 direct shares (~1.84% of ~9M shares); compensation $2.28M/yr (32.6% salary, 67.4% variable, heavily stock-linked). Co-trustee of Zandman family trust (Class B, ~28.5% voting). Alignment is genuine though not a traditional founding-family structure.

Sources: GuruFocus (Shoshani insider), Nasdaq.com (VPG insider ownership article), SEC Form 4 filings.

### Economics
- FY2025 revenue: $307.2M (+0.22% YoY — cyclical flat; precision market digesting semiconductor capex hangover)
- Q1 2026 revenue: $84.4M (+17.6% YoY, +4.7% sequential — acceleration confirmed)
- Q1 2026 bookings: $102.1M (3rd highest quarterly in VPG history; +25.5% sequential)
- Humanoid robot revenue: ~$4M in 2025 → $5-6M in 2026 → low-teens millions by 2027-2028 (per management Q1 2026 call; [source: Grafa, GlobeNewswire Q1 2026 press release, Insider Monkey])
- Foil Technology Products EBITDA margin: ~26%+ (highest segment)
- ROIC: [FLAG: unverified for current period — prior data suggests below-average blended ROIC due to Weighing segment drag; Foil Technology likely 15-20%+ standalone]
- Net cash: ~$85M+ (per Q1 2025 data; [FLAG: verify current balance])
- Stock: $25 trough (2024) to $131.68 ATH (May 2026) — 5x run already completed

Sources: SEC EDGAR VPG Form 10-Q FY2026, Form 8-K FY2026, GlobeNewswire Q1 2026 press release, Grafa.com (earnings highlights), Insider Monkey (Q1 report), Investing.com (ATH article).

### Valuation
- Market cap: ~$1.5-1.7B (13-14M shares x ~$95-120/share, approximate; [UNVERIFIED exact])
- TTM P/E: ~149-283x (cyclical trough earnings; use with extreme caution)

Total revenue $650M-$950M; at 25x EV/EBITDA on $175M EBITDA = EV $4.4B = ~3x from current $1.5B.

### Top 3 Risks
1. MEMS/semiconductor displacement: Lower-cost MEMS force sensors (STMicro, Bosch Sensortec, custom ASIC) may reach sufficient accuracy for mass-market humanoids (Unitree, Chinese producers are already using cheaper alternatives). VPG's premium TAM shrinks to surgical/precision-cobalt applications.

Hold Bioventix unless VPG pulls back to $70-90 (pre-hype zone), then reconsider a swap. Or treat VPG as a satellite/watchlist position.

**Conviction: 3/5** — Genuine foil-technology moat, dual-class family voting control, best pure-play on robot tactile sensing that is ISA-buyable. The stock has run hard; conviction increases to 4/5 on a 25-35% pullback.

## GEM 3: REGAL REXNORD
**RRX | NYSE | ~$13.8B market cap | Quality US industrial compounder with embedded humanoid option**

### Wave + why under-covered
Regal Rexnord is classified as an "industrial conglomerate" and owned primarily by generalist industrials investors focused on HVAC motors and power transmission — not humanoid robots. Yet it owns Portescap (the leading miniature BLDC/coreless DC motor maker for precision robotics, medical devices, and humanoid joints) and has already secured $30M+ in annual humanoid-related revenue with a $200M pipeline identified by JPMorgan (February 2026 analysis, per Benzinga reporting).

Portescap's coreless DC technology is the global standard for high-speed, low-inertia actuation in instruments. The coreless winding (no iron core = zero cogging, extremely smooth motion) is essential for tactile/force-controlled tasks — humanoid fingertips, wrist roll, facial expressions, and elbow actuation. Regal Rexnord also contributes Thomson linear guides (humanoid spine/waist linear axes) and Rexnord precision couplings (robot drivetrain isolation).

Why under-covered: humanoid revenue is sub-1% of $6B — below materiality threshold for analyst models. The legacy "Regal Electric + Rexnord" brand creates classification confusion. JPMorgan flagged it February 2026; the stock re-rated, but the $200M pipeline is still largely unmodeled.

### Moat
- Portescap coreless DC motors: one of only 3-4 globally capable producers of coreless DC motors at scale; coreless winding is a manufacturing process moat (cannot be replicated quickly); medical OEM spec-in (da Vinci Surgical instruments use Portescap motors in instrument drive units — switching requires full FDA re-clearance).
- Thomson linear motion: 80+ year installed base in precision linear guides and ball screws; specified into robotic drivetrains by mechanical engineers for decades.
- System-level integration advantage: RRX can provide motor + encoder + coupling + linear guide as single supplier to humanoid OEMs.

### Founder/Insider Ownership
- No founding-family concentration. CEO Louis Pinkham holds modest shares. Institutional ownership dominant. Insider alignment is the primary limitation of RRX vs. portfolio DNA. [FLAG: exact % unverified]

### Economics
- FY2025 revenue (TTM through March 2026): $5.99B (+1.54% YoY)
- Forward P/E: ~20.5x (consensus adj. earnings; approximately $10-11/share adj.) — most attractively valued of the four gems
- TTM P/E: ~52x (GAAP-distorted by Rexnord merger amortization)
- EV/EBITDA: ~15.5x; EV/FCF: ~21x
- Operating cash flow TTM: $990M
- ROIC: ~5% GAAP (depressed by $5.5B+ Rexnord merger goodwill; Portescap segment likely 15-25% ROIC standalone — [FLAG: unverified])
- Humanoid revenue: confirmed $30M+ annual (per management) with $200M pipeline (per JPMorgan; Benzinga, Sahmcapital February 2026)
- Debt: elevated from Rexnord merger; net debt/EBITDA being guided down toward 2.0x

Sources: SEC EDGAR RRX Form 8-K FY2024, stockanalysis.com (RRX statistics), Benzinga/Sahmcapital (JPMorgan humanoid analysis February 2026), Portescap product pages.

### Valuation
- Market cap: ~$13.8B (stock ~$207, ~66.7M shares)
- Fwd P/E: ~20.5x — pricing only industrial normalization, not the humanoid option
- EV/EBITDA: ~15.5x — consistent with industrial peers
- Humanoid option effectively free: $30M revenue today generates $0 valuation premium in the stock vs. industrials comps

### 10-yr bull + asymmetric math
Base case (2033): Organic growth 4-6%/yr; humanoid grows to $150-250M by 2030. Total revenue $8-9B; EBITDA ~$1.7B at 20% margin. At 14x EV/EBITDA = EV ~$23.8B, equity ~$20B = ~1.5x from today's $13.8B.

Bull case (asymmetric): Humanoid scales to $500M+ by 2030 (1M units, Portescap wins 3-4 platforms at $200-400/robot). Industrial automation cycle restarts. At 18x EV/EBITDA on $2.2B EBITDA = EV ~$40B = nearly 3x from today. Plausible on optimistic humanoid adoption.

Note: Not the "Vista 10x pattern" — this is a well-run US industrial compounder at reasonable valuation with a robotics option priced in slowly. Upside 2-3x over 7-10 years.

### Top 3 Risks
1. No founder/owner alignment — strategic priorities could shift away from humanoid investment if early ramp disappoints.
2. Merger integration risk: $5.5B+ goodwill from Rexnord merger; any impairment or integration difficulty is painful.
3. Portescap is too small to move the needle fast: $30M on $6B = 0.5%; even $300M = 5% of revenue. The humanoid lever is long; the stock may not price it in for 5+ years.

However, as a US industrial compounder at ~20x fwd P/E with an embedded humanoid option, it deserves monitoring as a potential VinaCapital Vietnam replacement (fills US industrial blind spot).

**Conviction: 2.5/5** — Solid business, genuine humanoid exposure (Portescap), reasonable valuation. Too large, too covered, insufficient insider alignment for this portfolio's DNA. Watchlist only.

## GEM 4: SCHAEFFLER AG
**SHA | Xetra (Frankfurt) | ~EUR 6.7-9B market cap (volatile post-merger surge) | Contrarian automotive-to-humanoid reframing**

### Wave + why under-covered
Schaeffler is despised by automotive investors (auto sector restructuring, ICE volume decline, EV headwinds) — which is why the humanoid optionality is being given away cheaply. Schaeffler:
- Has an "all-in-one" planetary gear actuator purpose-built for humanoid robot joints (unveiled CES 2026) combining two-stage planetary gearbox + electric motor + encoder + controller in a compact unit; won Deutsche Messe Hermes Award.
- Claims its portfolio covers ~50% of total humanoid robot BOM (bearings, precision reducers, linear actuators, gearboxes, strain-wave gears)
- Has five signed customer contracts in humanoid robotics as of Q1 2026; engaged with ~45 humanoid OEMs globally; expects mid-triple-digit EUR (EUR 100-999M) order book by 2030
- Signed strategic partnership with Hexagon Robotics (supply agreement) and Neura Robotics (joint actuator development + multi-year purchase commitment)
- Target: 10% of total revenue from new growth areas (humanoid robotics, defense, eVTOL) by 2035 — on EUR 23B+ revenue, that is EUR 2.3B+ combined

Why under-covered: universally classified as "automotive supplier"; tracked by auto-sector analysts with no domain knowledge of precision reducers or humanoid actuators. Humanoid business <1% of 2025 revenue.

Q1 2026 signal: Stock surged ~26.7% on earnings day (May 5, 2026) — market beginning to price humanoid re-rating. May be too late for lowest-price entry.

### Moat
- Precision bearing specification moat: FAG and INA bearings are the global standard in precision machine tools, wind turbines, aero engines, and robot joints. XZU double-row angular contact needle bearing is purpose-engineered for humanoid joint lightweight applications. Switching = full re-engineering and recertification.
- Vertically integrated actuator: Schaeffler integrates gearbox + motor + encoder + controller — same "full motion stack" competitive advantage as Novanta but at joint actuator (module) level.
- Strain-wave gear capability: Via Vitesco merger integration, Schaeffler now has precision strain-wave manufacturing — directly competitive with Harmonic Drive (Japan) for small/medium joint market.
- Manufacturing scale: EUR 23B revenue provides economies of scale, supply-chain leverage, and global service networks that no startup can match.

### Founder/Insider Ownership
- Schaeffler family (IHO Holding): ~79% of voting common shares. Maria-Elisabeth Schaeffler-Thumann and Georg Schaeffler control IHO.
- This is exceptional founder-family alignment — comparable to Investor AB or EXOR in structure. Family control insulates against short-term pressure to abandon multi-year humanoid investment.
- Free float: only ~21% — liquidity is limited but sufficient for a long-term investor.

Sources: Schaeffler shareholder page, MarketScreener (SHA shareholders), ad-hoc news IR releases, press releases via Schaeffler.com.

### Economics (FY2025 and 2026 outlook)

- Market cap: EUR 6.7-9B (volatile; stock ~EUR 7-9.5 in May 2026; surged ~26.7% post Q1 2026)
