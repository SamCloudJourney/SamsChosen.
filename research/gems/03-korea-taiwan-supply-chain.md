# Korea & Taiwan Hidden Supply-Chain Gems

**Lane:** niche semiconductor materials/equipment/components — picks-and-shovels into the AI/HBM/advanced-packaging supercycle, under-covered by Western investors.

## Pre-screen eliminations
- **Hanmi Semiconductor (042700.KS, ~$20B):** Brilliant TC Bonder franchise (43% op margin, 55% founder-family ownership, Kwak Dongshin buying) but no longer under-covered or a monopoly — SK Hynix diversifying to ASMPT, Micron to BESI for HBM4; Hanmi share at SK Hynix reportedly falling to 20–30% by 2026; P/B 42x, EV/EBITDA 132x on peak margins. Moat cracking at peak valuation. Not a gem at this price.
- **Chroma ATE (2360.TW, ~$31B):** NVIDIA's exclusive system-level test supplier for GB200; 61–65% GM, ROE 29%, +31% revenue 2025. But +633% in 52 weeks, P/E ~80–100x, EV/FCF ~536x. Fully discovered. Not hidden.
- **Kinsus (3189.TW, ~$11.5B):** ABF substrate AI beneficiary (+29% 2025 revenue, net profit +31x) but op margins only 6.8% (vs 22.6% 2022 peak) = severe cyclicality; Pegatron-affiliated governance; 9 buys already; fwd P/E ~27x. Covered, cyclical, thin margins.

## GEM 1: LEENO INDUSTRIAL
- **Ticker:** 058470.KQ (KOSDAQ) | **Country:** South Korea | **Mkt cap:** ~₩8.4tn (~$6.1B) | **Stage:** Mature niche monopolist, AI-driven re-acceleration.

**Structural wave & under-coverage:** Makes ultra-precision test pins (probe pins) — the spring-loaded needles contacting wafers/die during electrical test. Re-rated by: (1) AI logic complexity (more I/O points, finer pitch — Blackwell <75µm); (2) HBM test intensity (more stacking layers = more test touchpoints); (3) 3nm/2nm ramp tightening specs (pins as thin as 0.075mm). Under-covered: KOSDAQ (not KOSPI), ~$190M revenue, no ADR, files in Korean, Busan-HQ, ~zero Western coverage. Investors think test = Cohu/Teradyne/Advantest and miss the consumable pin layer.

**Moat:** >70% global share in wafer-test ultra-precision probe pins ("Leeno Pin" = generic noun). Prohibitive tolerances; qualified into 1,000+ fabs (Samsung, SK Hynix, TSMC, Qualcomm, NVIDIA, Micron); re-qual is 12–18 months. Consumable razor/blade model. >3.5% R&D (TSV probe cards, Tri-Temperature probes, 100GHz sockets).

**Ownership — GOVERNANCE FLAG:** Founder Lee Chae-yoon (b.1950) ~34.66% pre-sale; in Apr–May 2026 announced sale of ~7m shares (~9.18%) for ~₩863B (~$591M), cutting stake to ~25.5%. Children reportedly unwilling to take over; live succession risk (could trigger an M&A premium OR a prolonged overhang). Institutions ~43%.

**Economics:** FY2025 TTM revenue ~₩263bn (~$263M, +~30%). Operating margin ~37% historically; Q3 2025 cited at 49.8% (AI/HBM mix). Net margin ~33% TTM. ROIC ~15% (single source). Net cash ₩68.3bn. Trailing P/E ~279x (distorted); fwd P/E ~37–44x; P/B ~55x (asset-light); div yield ~0.6%. Stock ~₩110,800 (52-wk ₩39,050–₩129,000; +~170% from trough).

**Valuation:** 37–44x fwd on a ~40%+ margin monopoly consumable with net cash — not cheap conventionally, but a Western analogue would fetch 40–60x; Korea discount = fairly-to-modestly-cheap.

**10-yr bull case:** AI/HBM complexity flywheel; #1 supplier in 1,000+ fabs with near-zero switching. If revenue compounds 15–20% for 5 yrs at 40–45% margins, earnings ~₩400–500bn by 2030; 30x → 3–5x. Secondary: M&A premium given unclear succession.

**Risks:** (1) Succession/governance (live now). (2) Korean memory-downcycle concentration (Samsung/SK Hynix). (3) Long-term contactless-test substitution.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Bioventix** — comparable margin quality on a larger profit base, harder structural tailwind, more liquidity, Korea/Asia semi exposure (geographic gap). **Conviction: 3.5/5** (business 5/5, governance/timing 2/5).

## GEM 2: HANSOL CHEMICAL
- **Ticker:** 014680.KS (KOSPI) | **Country:** South Korea | **Mkt cap:** ~₩3.41tn (~$2.47B) | **Stage:** Compounder transitioning from commodity chemicals to spec-in semiconductor materials.

**Structural wave & under-coverage:** Pivoted from bulk hydrogen peroxide to mission-critical ALD/CVD precursors for sub-3nm fabrication. Two waves: (1) ALD precursor chemistry as the new sub-3nm bottleneck (ppt purity; qualified at Samsung, SK Hynix, TSMC, reportedly Intel/Micron); electronic materials now ~45% of profit mix and growing; (2) geopolitical diversification away from Japanese suppliers (Stella Chemifa, Kanto, Sumitomo) post-2019. Under-covered: indexed as a "chemicals company," no ADR, minimal English IR, ~$2.5B cap, ~12 Korean analysts / ~zero Western.

**Moat:** Spec-in qualification (12–24 month approval into a fab's process module, zero defect tolerance) = among the highest switching costs in materials. 20+ years of ultra-high-purity H₂O₂ know-how (99.9999%+). Local-supply preference; co-development with Samsung/SK Hynix R&D.

**Ownership — flag:** Cho family (descendants of Samsung founder via Hansol Group) ~16.4% (Cho Dong-hyeok ~15.07% + related). NPS ~14% ("active investor"). Family reportedly seeking a "white knight" — control fragility; activism plausible (could force better returns OR distract management).

**Economics:** Revenue ₩769→885→772→776→884bn (FY21–25); op income ₩198→186→124→129→156bn; op margin 25.7%→16.1%→17.7% (trough then re-expanding); net income ₩148→155→105→123→147bn; FCF FY2025 ₩117bn (recovering after FY2023 −₩15bn precursor capex); EPS FY2025 ₩13,428. ROE ~14–21%; low leverage.

**Valuation:** Share ₩280,500 (52-wk ₩120,200–346,500); P/E ~22.5x; consensus target ₩374,000 (+33%). EV/EBITDA est 10–12x. Best risk/reward in the lane — discount reflects mis-classification + governance + the flat 2021–24 period (all potentially temporary). Japanese ALD comps 15–25x.

**10-yr bull case:** Electronic materials to 50–60% of revenue by 2030; margins back to 22–25%; FCF compounds 20%+; multiple re-rates to 30–35x as Western investors discover the semi-materials story = 3–5x. Adjacent options: silicon anode (EV batteries), QD display materials.

**Risks:** (1) Governance/family-control fragility + NPS activism. (2) H₂O₂/Samsung-foundry cyclicality (~30% of H₂O₂ revenue from Samsung). (3) Capacity-ramp execution (Korea + US lines; front-loaded capex cut Q4 2025 op profit).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Sartorius Stedim** — both spec-in consumable suppliers, but Hansol has a structural upturn, better visibility, 22x vs Sartorius's recovery 50–70x. Also fills Korean semi-materials gap. **Conviction: 4/5.**
