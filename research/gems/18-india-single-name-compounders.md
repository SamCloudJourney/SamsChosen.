## 1. POLYCAB INDIA (POLYCAB.NS)
- India | ~₹1,25,000–1,43,000Cr (~$15–17B) | Established/scaling.

**Wave & under-coverage:** India infrastructure buildout (transmission, real estate, data centers, renewables, defense) consumes wires/cables. Polycab is the toll collector (every building wired, every solar farm, every DC). Gati Shakti + 100% electrification + private capex flow through its order book. Well-covered by Indian brokerages now, but still under-appreciated internationally.

**Moat:** 30–31% of India's organised wires/cables market (up from 18–19% FY19) = category captain; 28 plants, 10,600+ SKUs, 4,000+ channel partners; KEI/RR Kabel can't match distribution/breadth; default spec for electricians/contractors (Fastenal-like); FMEG (fans/switches/LED/solar) now profitable (~10% of revenue, long-duration optionality).

**Ownership:** Jaisinghani family (founded 1983) — promoter holding 61.5%; son Nikhil groomed for succession — exactly the founder-alignment archetype.

**Economics (FY22–26):** Revenue ₹12,204→28,883Cr (5-yr CAGR 27%); PAT ~₹900→2,708Cr (CAGR 25%); ROCE 34–35%; op margin 13–14%; FCF ₹853Cr→CFO ₹3,811Cr; net cash ₹4,190Cr (debt-free); FY26 revenue +29%, PAT +32%. P/E ~47x; P/B ~11x; PEG ~1.9 (reasonable for a category leader).

**10-yr bull:** India electricity infra doubles; DCs/EV/solar cable-intensive; share 30%→35–40%; FMEG to 20–25% at higher margins; revenue to ₹80,000–100,000Cr by FY2035; 15–20% CAGR.

**Risks:** (1) Commoditisation/competition (KEI, RR Kabel; Chinese dumping). (2) Copper/aluminium volatility. (3) FMEG execution vs Havells/Crompton/Bajaj.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — most "Constellation-like" in its category (founder-led, infrastructure tailwind, market-share gains, improving FCF). **Conviction: 4.5/5** (best-in-class; only missing a 5 on stretched valuation + manufacturing-thin margins).
