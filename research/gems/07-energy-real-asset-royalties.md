## PICK 1: TOPAZ ENERGY (TPZ.TO)
- Canada | TSX | ~C$5.0B (~US$3.7B) | Royalty/infrastructure hybrid, growth phase.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Sits at WCSB gas development for LNG Canada + the Clearwater oil play. Tourmaline (Canada's largest gas producer) tripling LNG-linked volumes 213,000→333,000 mmbtu/d by exit-2028 on lands where Topaz holds royalty + infrastructure interests. Infrastructure piece invisible to E&P/midstream screeners. ~17 analysts, no US index inclusion.

**Moat:** Royalty stream (~75% of revenue) — no capex/opex, 17.5% on fee lands, no abandonment liability. Infrastructure (~25%) — owns processing/water-handling that Tourmaline/Tamarack/Headwater are contractually obligated to run through (min-volume commitments; 92–96% op margins). ~90% of royalty lands operated by 3 disciplined operators. Acreage geologically defined, permanent, irreplaceable.

**Ownership:** Tourmaline ~15% (down from 21%, selling — modest flag, but it's the anchor operator); total insider/strategic ~20%; management buying in 2025.

**Economics:** FY2025 revenue C$336.5M, EBITDA C$338.7M, FCF C$307.5M (91% margin); Q1 2026 revenue C$94.6M, FCF C$78.7M; 2026 guidance ~C$380M revenue; royalty production ~23,500–23,900 boe/d; dividend C$1.40 (66% payout); ND/EBITDA 1.5x (→<1.2x exit). Share C$32.51; EV ~C$5.5B; EV/EBITDA ~16x; P/FCF ~16x; yield ~4.3%. Dividends sustainable to US$55 WTI / ~zero AECO (verified). 16x vs Franco-Nevada 35–40x.

**10-yr bull:** LNG Canada Phase 2 + Asian demand (Tourmaline TTF-linked to 333,000 mmbtu/d by 2028); Clearwater (production +50% in 2025, decade runway); 66% payout leaves ~35% FCF to buy new royalties (Franco-Nevada-style flywheel; ~23% TSR/yr since 2020 IPO); infra tariffs CPI-linked. At 6% boe growth, $70 WTI/$3 AECO → FCF ~C$500M by 2033; 15x P/FCF → ~C$7.5B vs C$5B = ~10–12%/yr incl reinvested dividends.

**Risks:** (1) AECO gas pricing (70% gas; structurally weak Canadian gas). (2) Tourmaline concentration. (3) Tourmaline ownership decline.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **VinaCapital Vietnam** (or India ETF) — passive EM → genuine moat + capital-light royalty + inflation linkage + 4%+ growing yield; fills the energy/real-asset blind spot. **Conviction: 4/5.** If only one energy slot, Topaz wins (lower valuation, LNG catalyst, infra stabiliser, higher yield).

## PICK 2: PRAIRIESKY ROYALTY (PSK.TO)
- Canada | TSX | ~C$8.1B (~US$6.0B) | "Franco-Nevada of Canadian oil."

**Wave & under-coverage:** Perpetual royalty on Canadian oil development — 18.6M acres of fee-simple mineral rights (largest independent in Canada), spun from Encana 2014 at 4x EBITDA. Clearwater + Mannville Stack light-oil renaissance on its core acreage; Trans Mountain expansion improved WCS pricing; Montney/Duvernay still early. ~11–19 Canadian analysts, ~no US coverage; should trade like Franco-Nevada/Texas Pacific Land (much higher multiples).

**Moat:** Purest royalty model anywhere — zero drilling/operating/abandonment/environmental cost; fee-simple title (operators must lease and pay 17.5% oil / 15% gas in perpetuity); lease-bonus revenue = free optionality on discovery; 18.6M acres can't be assembled by a competitor; 66 employees, ~$7.2M revenue/employee, ~90% EBITDA margin.

**Ownership:** CEO Andrew Phillips 0.45% (~C$36M), buying Feb 2026 at C$30.35; CPP/CDPQ/Ontario Teachers stable long-duration holders; institutional design self-governing.

**Economics:** FY2025 revenue C$478.2M (−7% on lower WTI); FFO C$353.0M (C$1.50/sh); ~90% EBITDA margin; dividends C$243.4M (69% payout); buybacks C$158.5M (6.2M shares); net debt C$276.5M (~0.6x); oil royalty production 13,940 bbl/d (+6%). Share C$34.69; EV ~C$8.4B; EV/EBITDA ~18.4x; EV/FCF ~24x; P/FFO ~23x; yield ~3.1% (total cash-return ~5%+ with buybacks).

**10-yr bull:** Clearwater/Mannville multi-billion-bbl plays; lease-bonus optionality on undiscovered resources; Trans Mountain pricing; 69% payout + shrinking share count compounds per-share FFO; 100% FCF deployable. At 4% production growth, $70 WTI → ~C$45–55/sh by 2033 = ~10–12%/yr.

**Risks:** (1) Oil cyclicality (FFO −7% on −14% WTI; −25–30% at $50). (2) Production plateau (PSK can't force development). (3) Already fairly valued at 18x (re-rating window largely closed).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** or **Vietnam** — perpetual mineral title + buybacks. Complementary to Topaz (oil/acreage vs gas/infra). **Conviction: 4/5.**

Income vehicle, not a compounder. **2/5.**
