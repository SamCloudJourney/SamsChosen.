## GEM 1: NABTESCO (6268.T)
- Japan | TSE / OTC NCTKF | ~¥648B (~$4.3B) | Established compounder, cyclical re-rating candidate.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Makes the RV cycloidal reduction gears forming the joints of industrial robots — invisible torque backbone of every Fanuc/ABB/KUKA/Yaskawa arm. ~60% global share in precision reducers for medium-to-large robots (~90%+ of the RV segment) — the single largest choke-point in global robot production. Under-covered: Tokyo-only, minimal English coverage; conglomerate (railway brakes, aircraft actuators, automatic doors, marine) obscures the pure robotics exposure; Western generalists miss it. Industrial robot installs +10–13%/yr; humanoid is an additive option (doubling RV capacity by 2026); reducer market $4.8B (2026) → $12.8B by 2034 (~13% CAGR).

**Moat:** Sole-source spec-in (OEM designers specify Nabtesco RV gears by name; re-engineering a joint = full re-certification); micron-precision RV cycloidal geometry (decades of metallurgy/heat-treatment/assembly); complementary duopoly with Harmonic Drive (small joints) supplying essentially all certified robots; aftermarket/parts revenue over a 10–15 yr asset life.

**Ownership:** No founding-family concentration; typical Japanese cross-holding; insider ownership not material (unverified). ROIC ~6% statutory (depressed by non-core conglomerate segments — the gear segment is likely 15–20%).

**Economics (FY2025):** Revenue ¥307.9B (+9.8%); group op income +60.3%; Component Solutions ¥100.5B revenue / ¥13.6B op profit (~13.5% margin); FY2026 group margin guidance 8.5% (Q1 9.9%, +300bps); FY2026 revenue guidance ¥327B (+6.2%); ROIC ~6.1% (group); EV/EBITDA ~14.7x; P/E ~40x TTM / ~30x fwd. Hydraulics divestiture improves quality.

**Valuation:** ~30x fwd / ~14.7x EV/EBITDA — not cheap by Western standards but prices only modest growth against capacity doubling, Component Solutions +28% in the latest quarter, and the $4.8B→$12.8B TAM. The conglomerate discount masks that the gear segment alone likely commands 25–35x pure-play.

**10-yr bull:** Robot installs +10–13%/yr; humanoids add demand (dozens of reducers/unit); Nabtesco the sole-capable Western-allied supplier (Chinese alternatives lack OEM cert for high-load); group margin 8.5%→12–15% as hydraulics drag goes; a pure-play demerger re-rates the stock. 3–5x earnings compounding + re-rating = 4–8x.

**Risks:** (1) Chinese reducer competition (Zhejiang Shuanghuan, Suzhou Green Harmonic, Beijing Chietom — already in Tesla Optimus/Unitree domestically; loss of Chinese market limits TAM). (2) Conglomerate value trap (railway/doors/marine absorbing capital/attention). (3) OEM in-sourcing (Fanuc/Yaskawa).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Vitec** — deeper moat, larger TAM (robotics > broadcast accessories), clearer secular tailwind; fills the absence of Japanese precision manufacturing. **Conviction: 4/5.**

## GEM 2: RENISHAW (RSW.L)
- UK | LSE | ~£3.86B (~$4.9B) | Re-rating candidate post-founder succession.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Makes precision encoders, touch-trigger probes, machine-tool measurement, CMM probes, metal additive-manufacturing machines — world leader in position encoders for semiconductor lithography stages, CNC feedback, CMM probing. Every high-precision process (wafer stepping, turbine-blade inspection, EV battery-tray machining) runs on Renishaw measurement. Under-covered: UK-listed, no US ADR; controlling-family ownership historically limited governance discourse; founder Sir David McMurtry died Dec 2024 (uncertainty suppressed the stock — Deltam family holdco signals stability, not sale); encoder/metrology is unglamorous.

**Moat:** Spec'd-into machine-builder designs (Heidenhain + Renishaw are the two dominant open-market encoder suppliers, designed into litho equipment globally); calibration/metrology network effect (factory QC chains built around Renishaw probes — enormous switching costs); recurring calibration / probe-tip consumption (tens of thousands of machines); metal-AM optionality.

**Ownership:** McMurtry (deceased) + John Deer families consolidated ~50.25% into Deltam Holdings (long-term commitment, ruling out near-term sale); Daniel Deer ~17%; CEO William Lee ~2.1% — owner-operated in practice (>50% family).

**Economics:** FY2025 (Jun YE) revenue £713.0M (+3.1% / +3.7% cc); adj op margin 15.7% (target 20%); net income £83.8M (−13.6%); 9M FY2026 revenue £571.6M (+9.5% / +13.5% cc); Q3 FY2026 £206M (+14%, strongest-ever); FY2026 guidance £775–805M revenue, adj PBT £145–165M; FCF ~£58–62M; ROIC ~7.6% statutory; net cash ~£199M (£218M cash, £18M debt). Trailing P/E ~52x / fwd ~30x / **~18–20x on FY2026 guidance midpoint** (materially cheaper than headline optics); EV/EBITDA ~28x; P/FCF ~62x.

**Valuation:** At 5,300p: fwd P/E ~18–20x on FY2026 guidance, net cash, ~50% family ownership; targeting 20% op margins (from 15.7%) — at 20% on ~£800M revenue, op profit ~£160M and the multiple compresses dramatically. The most attractively priced of the four gems despite headline optics.

**10-yr bull:** Semiconductor capital-equipment 2026–2030 ~$300B supercycle (TSMC/Intel/Samsung/ASML) — every litho stage/inspection tool dense with Renishaw encoders; aero/defense rises; EV/hydrogen tooling; margin 15.7%→20%+; metal AM to 10–15% of revenue. Re-rate to 25x on £1.2B revenue / 20% margins → £6B = ~55% upside (modest, low downside given net cash + Deltam anchor).

**Risks:** (1) Heidenhain competition (private German; no monopoly; IPO/pricing cycle could compress margins). (2) AM write-off risk (capital misallocation if R&D continues to AM at the expense of encoders). (3) Post-McMurtry succession/governance.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Secunet** — larger, more diversified, deeper moat, "indispensable behind-the-scenes" archetype; or complement Vitec/ATOSS as a precision-measurement niche. **Conviction: 4/5** — best combination of verified moat, reasonable forward valuation, strong insider alignment, semi/aero inflection.

## GEM 3: HARMONIC DRIVE SYSTEMS (6324.T)
- Japan | TSE / OTC HSYDF | ~$4.65B (~¥700B) | Cyclical trough/recovery.

Makes strain-wave ("harmonic") gears for small-to-medium robot joints, cobot wrists, semiconductor wafer handlers, satellite antennas, medical robotics — ~70% global share in strain-wave gears for small/medium robots. Every cobot wrist (Universal Robots, Doosan, Techman) and most surgical robots use its gears. Under-covered after a brutal 2024–25 margin collapse (semi downturn + China cobot slowdown). Waves: cobots ~15% CAGR; humanoid wrists/fingers/ankles designed for strain-wave gears; semi capital equipment recovering. **Moat:** eponymous technology (competitors license or make inferior copies); spec-in at OEM design stage (switching = full wrist redesign); unique flex-spline manufacturing (thin-walled, elliptically deformed gear cup, proprietary tooling/metallurgy); brand + know-how moats post patent expiry. **Ownership:** independent public, no dominant family; T. Rowe Price, Capital Research holders. **Economics (FY Mar 2026):** Revenue ¥59.6B (+7.0%); op profit ¥2.57B (~4.3% margin, trough); net income ¥1.61B (−53.7%); FY Mar 2027 guide ¥68.0B (+14.2%), op profit ¥6.2B (~9.1%, +141%); historical peak margins ~15–20%; EBITDA margin TTM ~16.7%; net cash; 52-wk $15.00–$53.06 (tripled from lows). **Valuation:** ~11–12x EV/Revenue (elevated, pricing recovery); recovery to 15–20% peak margins unpriced. **Risks:** (1) Chinese strain-wave competition (Suzhou Green Harmonic, Leader Harmonious Drive — government-funded; Tesla chose Chinese for Optimus) — primary existential risk. (2) Semi-cycle volatility (~30–40% of revenue; −94% op-profit drop FY2025). (3) Valuation after tripling. **Conviction: 3/5** — genuine moat, compelling trough-to-peak recovery, but China risk accelerating and the stock already recovered. Smaller position / watch.

## GEM 4: COGNEX (CGNX)
- US | NASDAQ | ~$11B (~$65.85) | Recovery / AI transition.

Machine-vision systems (industrial cameras, vision processors, deep-learning inspection) — ~30–35% global share (2–3x next competitor). Every Amazon FC, EV battery line, smartphone-camera plant uses Cognex vision. Misunderstood now: 2022–24 downturn (EV/logistics over-invested) made investors treat it as secularly declining; Q1 2026 (+24% revenue, +1,010bps EBITDA margin) is the early AI re-acceleration signal; OneVision / In-Sight 6900 (NVIDIA) / 3900 (Qualcomm) hardware→AI-platform transition under-appreciated. **Moat:** switching cost + installed base (QC lines built around camera geometry/lighting/software recipes; switching = multi-million-dollar re-engineering); 15% of revenue in R&D, 1,000+ patents; AI platform transition (training data/labeling/models = proprietary dataset moat); direct sales force; founder Dr. Shillman ~3.4% (~$370M). **Economics:** FY2025 revenue $981M (+3%); adj EBITDA $221M (~22.5%); GAAP op margin 16.3%; Q1 2026 revenue +24%, op margin 22.3%, adj EBITDA margin 26.9% (+1,010bps); Q2 guidance $280–300M (~17%), 28–31% EBITDA; $35–40M cost reductions; ROIC ~6.15% (goodwill-distorted; pre-goodwill 40–50%); ROE 21.2%. P/E ~59x TTM / ~37x fwd; EV/EBITDA ~33–41x. **Risks:** (1) Competition (ISRA/Teledyne; NVIDIA Metropolis for Industrial). (2) EV/logistics demand concentration. (3) Valuation leaves no room for error (37x fwd). **Conviction: 3/5** — dominant share + AI transition, but not a hidden-gem entry; buy on a 15–20% pullback (~$50–52). Replace **Vietnam** on weakness.
