# Lane 21 — Critical-Minerals Processing / Refining / Specialty Metals ex-China

_Researched 2026-06-01. Informational synthesis, not financial advice. Figures sourced from company filings and public sources; flagged where unverified. Re-verify before acting._

**Lane thesis:** The midstream processing and refining layer is the chokepoint China actually controls — not just mining. Gallium, germanium, tungsten, antimony, rare-earth separation, beryllium, and specialty vanadium/tantalum refining are ~80–99% China-concentrated at the processing level. The geopolitical bifurcation of 2023–2026 (China export controls on gallium, germanium, antimony, tungsten) is creating a structural, durable wave for the handful of western processors/refiners with real assets, real offtakes, and regulatory moats. This lane explicitly hunts processors over explorers — companies with operating or near-operating facilities, offtake agreements providing revenue visibility, and a real downside floor.

## 2. AMG Critical Materials N.V. — PRIMARY GEM ★★★★

**Name | Ticker+Venue | Country | ~Mktcap | Stage**
AMG Critical Materials N.V. | Euronext Amsterdam: AMG (ISIN NL0000888691) | Netherlands | ~€700-800M (June 2026) | Established operating / multi-segment diversified processor

**Wave + why under-covered/early**
AMG is a multi-decade specialty metals processor and vacuum-furnace engineer — genuinely invisible to most equity investors because it lacks a single "sexy" narrative. It processes vanadium (world leader in recycling from petroleum residues), refines tantalum and lithium (Bitterfeld battery-grade hydroxide), processes antimony (major 2025 beneficiary of China export ban), produces titanium alloys, and engineers vacuum furnaces for aerospace. It sits at the intersection of: defense rearmament (antimony for munition primers), energy storage (vanadium flow batteries), battery materials (lithium hydroxide), and aerospace (titanium + furnaces). Market cap ~€700M despite $1.7B revenue and $235M adj EBITDA in 2025 (third-highest in company history). Market perceives this as a "value trap" because of lithium exposure — that is the temporary blind spot creating the entry.

**Moat**
- Vanadium: World's market leader in recycling vanadium from oil-refinery spent catalysts — feedstock is continuous by-product; no mine needed. Unique globally.
- Antimony: Through AMG Antimony (Brazil), one of the few western non-China antimony processors. Benefited from China's Dec 2024 antimony export ban; AMG had ~$70M of low-priced inventory sold at new elevated prices (one-off in 2025; management has guided 2026 EBITDA accounts for normalization).
- Tantalum: AMG Superalloys is a major tantalum processor for aerospace/defense; feedstock partly from conflict-free coltan recycling.
- Vacuum furnace engineering (AMG Technologies): 100-year-old technology leader in vacuum metallurgy equipment for GE, Safran, Rolls-Royce, MTU — order backlog $370M, book-to-bill ~0.95x. This single segment contributed $163.8M of $235M total adjusted EBITDA in 2025 (+142% YoY) — worth more than the entire current market cap at any reasonable multiple.
- Lithium: Bitterfeld refinery (Germany) one of very few ex-China battery-grade hydroxide producers in Europe — ramp-up phase; lithium prices depressed (down 16% in 2025 = current overhang).
- Scale + geography: 40 operating units across 6 continents.

**Founder/Insider %**
CEO Dr. Heinz Schimmelbusch (appointed 2006, 19+ years tenure) directly owns 3.55% of shares (~€25M stake). Not a founder but a long-tenured owner-operator CEO. Top-10 institutional investors hold ~35% collectively. Not founder-controlled but CEO-aligned.

**Sourced Economics (FY2025 — from audited press release, GlobalNewswire Feb 2026)**

- Adjusted EBITDA: $235M (+40% YoY)
- Operating cash flow: $76.1M (+103% YoY)

The lithium trough and antimony one-off normalization create the optical discount; the underlying vanadium+engineering+tantalum combination is exceptional quality. If the Technologies segment alone (stable, aerospace-contracted) were valued at 10x its 2025 EBITDA, it would exceed AMG's current total enterprise value.

Downside floor: vanadium stays depressed + lithium stays at trough = the Technologies segment alone justifies most of today's market cap. This is an unusually safe base.

## 3. Energy Fuels Inc. — PRIMARY GEM ★★★

**Name | Ticker+Venue | Country | ~Mktcap | Stage**
Energy Fuels Inc. | NYSE American: UUUU | USA | ~$4.5B (June 2026) | Operating / transitioning to dual uranium+REE processor

**Wave + why under-covered/early**
Energy Fuels is America's largest uranium producer AND the only US company licensed to both produce uranium AND separate rare earth oxides from monazite at the same facility (White Mesa Mill, Utah). This dual-license asset is effectively irreplaceable. As of July 2025, the company became the first in the US to commercially produce separated heavy rare earth oxides — Dysprosium (99.9% purity) and Terbium — qualified by a major South Korean magnet manufacturer (December 2025). Heavy REEs face 264-276% price premiums outside China vs. within. Under-coverage: uranium analysts don't model REE separation; REE analysts don't track uranium. The dual mandate creates an analytical blind spot.

**Moat**
- White Mesa Mill: Only operating conventional uranium mill in the US + only US facility licensed for REE separation. Regulatory moat — decades to permit and build a competing facility.
- Monazite feedstock: sources monazite from heavy mineral sands processing (a by-product) — higher concentration of NdPr AND heavy REEs than Mountain Pass's bastnaesite. Structurally lower-cost feedstock.
- Heavy REE first-mover: First US producer of commercial Dy oxide (July 2025), Tb (Q4 2025/Q1 2026). No other Western facility separates these at scale.
- Uranium contract book: 6 long-term delivery contracts through 2032 with US utilities + 2 new contracts signed 2025.
- Working capital fortress: $927M working capital including $797M marketable securities at Dec 31, 2025 — debt-free, fully funded.
- Jan 2026: agreed to acquire Australian Strategic Materials (ASM) — adds Dubbo REE project (Australia) + REE metallization/alloying facility in South Korea.

**Founder/Insider %**
CEO Mark Chalmers (long-tenure, not a founder) — insider selling noted (~$20M net sales in 12 months per Simply Wall St data). This is a negative flag. Offset by the strategic/DoD dynamic and fortress balance sheet. Not a high-insider-alignment name.

**Sourced Economics**
- 2025 revenue: $65.9M (-16% YoY — timing of deliveries, not structural)
- Q1 2026 revenue: $35.8M (+112% YoY) — accelerating
- Working capital at Dec 31 2025: $927M (including $797M marketable securities) [source: company press release]
- Market cap: ~$4.5B
- 2026 uranium production guidance: 1.5-2.5M lbs U3O8 (vs ~1M lbs in 2025)

- FCF: negative (investing in REE expansion); no funding risk given cash position

**Valuation**
~$4.5B market cap vs. $66M TTM revenue = pure optionality pricing. Analyst consensus: Strong Buy (8 analysts), 12m target ~$27 (+44% from ~$18.22). This is NOT cheap on current earnings — it is an options-on-assets valuation. Conviction derives from the regulatory moat and heavy REE first-mover; not from current earnings.

**10-yr bull + asymmetric math**

Floor: uranium contract book provides ~$100-140M annual contracted revenue floor.

**Top 3 Risks**
1. Heavy REE separation at commercial tonnage has never been done outside China — execution risk is real and material
2. Uranium price volatility: spot market volatile; contracts provide cushion but re-contracting environment matters

## 4. Materion Corporation — QUALITY COMPOUNDER ★★★

**Name | Ticker+Venue | Country | ~Mktcap | Stage**
Materion Corporation | NYSE: MTRN | USA | ~$2.87B (June 2026) | Established compounder / regulatory-moat processor

**Wave + why under-covered/early**
Materion is the only US company that mines, processes, and manufactures beryllium-based precision materials. Beryllium is the lightest structural metal, with extraordinary stiffness-to-weight, thermal conductivity, and neutron reflectivity — irreplaceable in nuclear weapons, X-ray optics, space telescopes, and advanced semiconductor equipment. Beryllium sales to China, Iran, North Korea are export-controlled. Materion is a near-100-year-old specialty materials processor (founded 1931 as The Brush Beryllium Company). Under-covered because (a) beryllium is obscure, (b) it's classified as basic-materials (wrong frame — it's a precision industrial), and (c) the semiconductor/optics businesses dilute the defense narrative. With global defense spending surging, a major defense prime invested $65M specifically to expand Materion's beryllium capacity.

**Moat**
- Sole US beryllium miner: Materion Natural Resources operates the only US domestic beryllium mine (Juab County, Utah) + processing mill (Millard County, Utah). Federal land access + multi-decade permitting = essentially impossible to replicate.
- Export control barrier: Beryllium cannot be sold to China or hostile states — Materion's US production is structurally the only source for Western defense programs.
- Customer qualification stickiness: Qualified-supplier relationships for nuclear weapons, X-ray windows, space telescopes, and advanced semiconductor equipment take years to establish — switching costs extreme.
- Precision optics: Growing segment for laser defense systems, hyperspectral imaging, space optics — 26% YoY revenue growth Q4 2025.
- Electronic materials: Advanced semiconductor packaging alloys; 20% YoY revenue growth Q4 2025.

**Founder/Insider %**
No founding family. CEO Jugal Vijayvargiya (appointed 2021). Institutional ownership ~88%. Not a high-insider-alignment name — moat is asset-based. Offset by 100-year heritage and stable long-tenured management.

## 5. Almonty Industries Inc. — NEXT-VISTA CANDIDATE ★★★½

Almonty Industries Inc. | NASDAQ: ALM (also TSX: AII) | Canada/International | ~$4.5-5.6B (May 2026) | Transitioning to production / ramp phase

**Wave + why under-covered/early**
Tungsten is the forgotten critical mineral. China controls ~80-85% of global tungsten production and an even larger share of processing. Tungsten is irreplaceable in cutting tools, armor-piercing munitions, semiconductor interconnects (tungsten CVD in chips), and aircraft alloys. Almonty has revived the Sangdong Tungsten Mine in South Korea — historically one of the world's largest and highest-grade (0.51% WO3, ~3x global average) tungsten deposits, closed 30+ years, now producing as of December 2025. At full Phase 2 capacity, Sangdong is designed to supply ~40% of global non-China tungsten. This is the picks-and-shovels answer to Western tungsten. Under-covered: still in ramp phase with minimal Sangdong revenue; Canadian primary listing; and tungsten is obscure to generalist investors.

**Moat**
- Sangdong geology: Grade 0.51% WO3 (~3x global average) — lowest-cost deposit structure in the Western world; open at depth, >40-year mine life.
- Scale dominance: Phase 1 = 2,300 MT tungsten concentrate/yr; Phase 2 (2027) doubles to 4,600 MT — at that point ~40% of all non-China tungsten supply from one mine.
- Offtake agreements:
  - US defense: Tungsten Parts Wyoming (defense contractor) + Metal Tech (Israel) — binding offtake for US defense applications
  - SeAH Group (Korean steel giant, SpaceX contractor): molybdenum offtake life-of-mine
  - DoD mandate: US procurement requiring non-China tungsten post-2027
- Montana US project: Gentung Tungsten acquisition in Montana to establish US domestic supply
- Cash fortress: $268M cash at Dec 31, 2025 (from two 2025 equity raises) — fully funded to Phase 2

CEO and co-founder Lewis Black (British-American, born 1968): holds ~6.9% per Schedule 13G (19.5M shares beneficial). All insiders combined ~18% [Yahoo Finance]. Lewis Black is the true founder/entrepreneur who identified Sangdong and held through 15+ years of development. High alignment.
