## Lane thesis

France has a deep bench of family- or founder-controlled niche-global leaders that are structurally under-researched by UK and US investors: too small for the CAC 40 narrative, too local-language for Anglo investor relations, often thinly covered by sell-side. The archetype (Sartorius Stedim, Robertet, Teleperformance in its prime) is: world-dominant in an unsexy vertical, 15-25% ROIC, net-cash balance sheet, compounding quietly for decades. The portfolio already holds Sartorius Stedim and Eurofins -- this lane looks for NEW names that deepen or diversify that DNA.

Somfy eliminated immediately: taken private by the Despature family in January 2023. Not investable.
Devoteam eliminated immediately: returned to private ownership Q4 2021. Not investable.
Esker eliminated: announced acquisition by General Atlantic/Bridgepoint for ~EUR 1.58B in 2024, likely delisted. Not investable.

## Gem 1 - Robertet SA

Name | Ticker | Venue | Country | ~Mkt cap | Stage
Robertet SA | RBT.PA | Euronext Paris (Compartment B) | France | ~EUR 1.65-1.70B (EUR 830/share) | Profitable mid-stage compounder

### Structural wave + why under-covered / early
The "clean label / naturals" wave in luxury fragrance and premium food has been building for 15+ years and is now accelerating -- driven by luxury brands demanding traceable, natural, sustainable ingredients as consumers and regulators pressure synthetic chemistry. Robertet is the single most direct pure-play on this structural trend in the entire listed world. It is under-followed because: (1) it trades at ~EUR 830/share with a tiny float and low daily volume; (2) only ~3 sell-side analysts cover it; (3) it is headquartered in Grasse, has French-language dominance, and does minimal English IR; (4) its EUR 1.65B market cap puts it below the screening threshold of most institutional mandates.

### Moat
- Specialisation moat: Unlike Givaudan, IFF, or Symrise (all 10-30x Robertet's size), Robertet has stayed entirely in naturals -- the highest-ROIC segment of the F&F industry. [V - multiple sources]
- Vertical integration ("Seed to Scent"): Robertet controls its own farms and sourcing partnerships across 60+ countries -- 80% of natural volumes secured through contracts of 3+ years. This is a cost and quality moat competitors cannot replicate quickly. [V - investor report / Substack]
- Highest gross margins in the F&F industry: ~55.7% gross margin vs. Givaudan ~43%, IFF ~42%. [V - GuruFocus cross-referenced]
- ROIC ~22%: Best-in-class among all global F&F peers. [V - GuruFocus]
- Switching costs: Fragrance formulations are IP-intensive, approval-cycle-heavy, and deeply embedded in client product lines. Once a client's perfume or food flavour is certified with Robertet's natural molecules, switching is painful and risky.
- ~15% global market share in the natural sub-segment: Total F&F market ~$30B; naturals ~20% = ~$6B; Robertet's ~EUR 800M revenue implies ~13-15% share of naturals. [U - estimated]

### Founder / family alignment
Maubert family retains controlling position since Robertet's founding in 1850 (Grasse). In November 2024, Robertet reshaped its shareholder base: Fonds Strategique de Participations (ISALT) and Peugeot Invest each acquired 7.1% stakes alongside the Maubert family, which reinforced its position -- bringing in quality long-term co-investors. [V - Actusnews press release Nov 2024]

## Gem 3 - Thermador Groupe SA

Name | Ticker | Venue | Country | ~Mkt cap | Stage
Thermador Groupe SA | THEP.PA | Euronext Paris | France | ~EUR 644M (~EUR 70/share) | Cyclically depressed serial-acquirer compounder

### Structural wave + why under-covered / early
The European energy transition and building renovation super-cycle: heat pumps replacing gas boilers, solar thermal, building retrofits mandated by EU Energy Performance of Buildings Directive. Thermador distributes the products that go into these systems -- heat pumps, thermal valves, pumps, pool equipment, industrial fluid circuits. No manufacturing risk. Sits in the indispensable distribution layer, earning 36-37% gross margins by aggregating 25+ specialist product brands. Analogous to Pool Corp (US) in structural positioning. Under-covered because: EUR 644M market cap, French-only IR, neglected SMID universe. Alpha Spread estimates intrinsic value at EUR 121/share vs. current EUR 70 -- a 42% discount. [V - Alpha Spread]

### Moat
- Multi-brand specialist distribution confederation: ~10 distinct subsidiaries each focused on a specific product vertical. Specialist expertise and local relationships that a generalist cannot replicate.
- Serial acquirer discipline: Active M&A even in down cycle: Alto Metering (Jul 2024), Vena Contracta (Aug 2024), C2AI (Jun 2025), Quilinox (Sep 2025). [V - press releases via search] ROIC ~10-11% above WACC ~8.3%. [V - GuruFocus]
- Gross margin durability: 36.9% gross margin held even as revenue fell 13% in 2024. [V - stockanalysis.com]
- Net cash: ~EUR 56M net cash as of Dec 2025. [U - calculated from EUR 97M cash vs EUR 41M bank debt]

### Founder / family alignment
Important nuance: The five original founders explicitly decided their children would NOT hold operating positions. NOT a classic family-controlled stock. Institutional investors 44.3%, individual shareholders 45.4%, employees 6.9%. [V - Thermador website] Founding philosophy (decentralisation, prudence, no debt) encoded in culture. Lower alignment score but owner-operator ethos is real.

## Gem 5 - Groupe Guillin SA

Name | Ticker | Venue | Country | ~Mkt cap | Stage
Groupe Guillin SA | ALGIL.PA | Euronext Growth Paris (ALXP) | France | ~EUR 420M | Profitable niche-industrial compounder (capex trough)

### Structural wave + why under-covered / early
EU Single-Use Plastics Directive + Extended Producer Responsibility regulations forcing the entire European food industry to migrate from standard plastics to recyclable/compostable/paper-based formats. Groupe Guillin is Europe's #1 producer of food packaging for processors/bakers and fruit/vegetable packers -- the exact categories most affected. Heavy capex (EUR 62M in 2024, >7% of revenue) builds new sustainable packaging capacity. This is a transition opportunity for a scale player that can retool while smaller rivals cannot. Under-covered: Euronext Growth listing, ~EUR 420M market cap, French-only IR, 64% family block = tiny float.

### Moat
- European scale in a niche: 32 production facilities across Europe, 25,000+ product references, #1 European position in bakery packaging and fruit/veg packaging. Scale creates cost and service advantages (same-day delivery of 25,000 SKUs) rivals cannot match.
- Guillin family anchor: Francois Guillin founded in 1972; family retains 64.36% of capital. [V - MarketScreener] Strongest family alignment of the five picks in this lane.
- Customer stickiness: Managing 25,000 SKUs with just-in-time delivery to food processors creates switching friction.

### Sourced economics
FY2024: revenue EUR 869.7M [V] (-1.7%), EBIT EUR 78.2M [V] (9.0% margin), net income EUR 60.1M [V]
FY2023: revenue EUR 884.6M [V], EBIT EUR 98.6M [V] (11.1% margin), net income EUR 76.0M [V]
Operating cashflow FY2024: EUR 105.1M [V]; capex ~EUR 62M [U]; FCF est. ~EUR 43M [U]
Net debt FY2024: EUR 16.2M [V]; equity EUR 632.4M [V]; dividend EUR 1.00/share proposed [V]
Margin compression in 2024 driven by: (a) weak French consumer volumes; (b) heavy capex on sustainable packaging transition. Business remains cashflow-positive with minimal net debt.

### Valuation (as of ~June 2026)
Market cap: ~EUR 420M [V]; EV: ~EUR 488M [U]
P/E (TTM): ~8.5x [V - stockanalysis.com]; EV/EBITDA: ~4.45x [V - stockanalysis.com]
EV/FCF: ~30.6x (elevated capex phase) [V]

### 10-year bull case + asymmetric math
Base: Revenue recovers EUR 950M by 2027, EBIT margin recovers to 10-11%, then 4% CAGR -> ~EUR 1.2B by 2034; at 10% EBIT margin EUR 120M EBIT; at 10x EV/EBIT EV EUR 1.2B -> mkt cap ~EUR 1.18B vs EUR 420M entry = ~2.8x (~11% CAGR). [U]
Bull: 6% CAGR, 12% EBIT -> EUR 180M EBIT -> at 12x EV EUR 2.16B -> ~5x (~17% CAGR). [U]
Floor: At 4.45x EV/EBITDA with 64% family ownership and minimal debt, Guillin family would almost certainly pursue take-private before letting it trade below 7-8x EBITDA.

### Top 3 risks
1. Plastic regulation overhang: If EU bans certain packaging categories faster than Guillin can retool, stranded-asset risk. Capex program mitigates but does not eliminate.

3. Liquidity: Euronext Growth listing + 64% family block = ~EUR 150M tradeable free float. Position-building takes weeks.

None -- watchlist unless specifically wanting deep-value/contrarian exposure. Could displace VinaCapital Vietnam (4% slot) as a "value with a catalyst" bet if sustainable packaging cycle inflection is 1-2 years away. Different DNA from the rest of the portfolio.
