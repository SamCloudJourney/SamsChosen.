## GEM 1: LEMAITRE VASCULAR (LMAT)
- US (Burlington MA) | NASDAQ | ~$2.15B | Profitable compounder.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Peripheral arterial disease affects 200M+ globally, rising with aging; open vascular surgery is structurally underfunded by innovation capital (big medtech chases minimally-invasive cardiovascular, leaving the open peripheral niche to LeMaitre). ~7 analysts; $2B below most large-fund radar.

**Moat:** 16 ultra-niche product lines (bovine carotid grafts, biologic patches, valvulotomes, carotid shunts) where it's #1 or sole supplier; single-use disposables required in every open peripheral procedure; surgeons trained on an Artegraft won't switch mid-career; FDA/CE barriers; in-house manufacturing (Burlington/North Brunswick). Each niche too small to attract competition, large enough in aggregate to sustain a $250M business growing 12%.

**Ownership:** George LeMaitre (Chairman/CEO, son of founder) ~7.6% (~$164M); total insider ~10%; systematic selling = wealth management.

**Economics:** Revenue FY2023–25 $193.5→219.9→249.6M; gross margin 65.7%→71.5%; op margin 19.0%→27.2%; FCF $29.5→74.5M (FY2025 conversion ~129%, one-off WC release); ROIC ~21% (~43% ex-goodwill); net cash ($367M cash vs ~$168M debt Q1 2026); FY2026 guidance $280M revenue (+12%), op margin ~28%, EPS $3.00 (+26%). P/E ~37–38x / ~32x fwd; EV/EBIT ~25–27x; FCF yield ~3.5%; DCF base ~$64–66 (stock ~45% above).

**10-yr bull:** $500M+ revenue by 2035 (10% CAGR), 32%+ op margin, $160M+ FCF; serial niche acquisitions; international Artegraft ($4M→$10M 2026→$40M+ by 2030); re-rate to 35x FCF → $5.5B+.

**Risks:** (1) Valuation (32x fwd needs sustained 12%+ growth). (2) George LeMaitre succession (irreplaceable DNA). (3) Artegraft concentration / single-site.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Bioventix** — larger, more liquid, equally moaty, stronger growth, more durable installed-base dynamics than a single-product royalty. **Conviction: 4/5.**

## GEM 2: CELLAVISION (CEVI.ST)
- Sweden | Nasdaq Stockholm | ~SEK 2.9B (~$270M) | Small-cap compounder.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Digital transformation of hematology labs is multi-decade. Manual blood-cell morphology is still practiced in most of ~100,000 clinical labs; CellaVision has converted only ~22% of ~17,000 large labs and ~zero of 80,000+ mid/small. 4–5 Swedish analysts, ~zero US; $270M off most screens.

**Moat:** >80% global share in digital cell morphology (DCM); switching costs (retraining + revalidation); data flywheel (20+ yrs of classifications improving the AI model); consumables lock-in (RAL Diagnostics reagents + software). Sysmex OEM distribution (double-edged — partner and potential competitor); Roche Cobas a limited threat.

**Ownership:** Founder Christer Färhaeus ~9.6%; William Demant A/S ~20%; Grenlunden (Martin Gren of Axis, Joel Eklund of TePe) ~10% — ~40% patient industrialist capital.

**Economics (SEK):** Revenue FY21–25 565→759m (~7.6% CAGR); op margin ~24.6–28.8%; FCF 146→182m; net income 125→153m; gross margin 68–69% (stable); ROIC ~15% (~26% ex-goodwill); net cash ~SEK 102m. Q1 2025 organic +14%, Q4 2025 +12.2% (accelerating but not yet to the 15% target). Share ~SEK 120–151; P/E ~19x; EV/FCF ~24.9x; EV/EBIT ~16x.

**Caution:** Actual 5-yr CAGR ~7.6% trails the 15% target — must be weighed; 2025 acceleration encouraging but not sustained.

**10-yr bull:** Revenue ~SEK 2.5B+ (12–14% CAGR via mid/small-lab penetration), EBITDA 38–40%, FCF ~SEK 900M; 25x FCF → SEK 22B+ (~7x). Catalysts: bone-marrow CE mark, DC-1 analyzer for smaller labs.

**Risks:** (1) Sysmex dependency (could build a competing system). (2) Growth ceiling at large labs if mid/small thesis unproven. (3) Founder-era DNA diluted; new CEO limited public-company experience.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Bioventix** or **Vitec** — Nordic single-niche diagnostics, more diversified (instruments + software + reagents) at lower entry valuation. **Conviction: 3.5/5** (prove the 15% target).

## GEM 3: VIRBAC (VIRP.PA)
- France | Euronext Paris | ~€3.2B (~$3.5B) | Mature compounder, pure-play animal health.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Animal health $50B+ growing 7–10% (pet humanization, ownership, vet spend, EM protein). Pure-play, no human-health exposure. Under-covered vs Zoetis/Elanco: French-listed (smaller US following), no US blockbusters, focus on "essential products" (parasiticides, antibiotics, anti-inflammatories) keeps it off momentum screens.

**Moat:** 100+ countries with country-specific regulatory approvals (decades-long moat); essential-therapeutics repeat-purchase mix; Dick family control prevents short-termism; Vikaly (medicated renal cat food, 2025) differentiated; avoids capital-intensive biologics races.

**Ownership:** Dick family (Marie-Hélène Dick-Madelpuech, vet surgeon) 50.09% capital / 66.20% votes — minimal M&A risk, long-term orientation.

**Economics (FY2025):** Revenue €1,465M; organic +7.9% (+4.8% reported, FX); adj EBIT margin 16.3% (~€234M); net income €150.5M; FCF ~€93M (62% conversion — capex-intensive €102M); net debt €172.8M. 2026 guidance: 5.5–7.5% organic, ~17% margin, FCF ~€80M. P/E ~21.3x; EV/EBITDA 11.8x; fwd P/E ~19.8x; EV/EBIT ~14.5x; consensus target €407 (~14% upside).

**Flag:** FCF €93M vs EV ~€3.38B = 2.8% yield (high capex €102M is the culprit; improves as cycle completes).

**10-yr bull:** €2.5–3B revenue by 2035 (7% CAGR), EBIT 18–20%, FCF ~€350M; 20x FCF → €7B (~2.2x). Upside: GLP-1-for-pets analogues (Lilly canine obesity trials).

**Risks:** (1) FX (EUR strength: 7.9% organic → 4.8% reported). (2) Companion-animal softening post-COVID. (3) Capex overhang.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** or **Sofina** — direct EM (40%+ APAC/Americas) + secular pet-health, family control; fills the animal-health blind spot (zero in the book). **Conviction: 3.5/5.**
