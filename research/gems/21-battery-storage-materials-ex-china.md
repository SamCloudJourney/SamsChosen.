# Lane 21 — Battery & Energy-Storage Materials ex-China

_Researched 2026-06-01. Informational synthesis, not financial advice. All figures sourced and flagged. Verify before acting._

## Mission
Hunt Western (ex-China) picks-and-shovels into the structural wave of battery supply-chain de-risking: cathode/anode/electrolyte/separator materials, recycling, grid-scale storage components. Target: real economics, genuine moats, UK T212 S&S ISA buyable.

---

## The Structural Wave

China controls ~80%+ of global battery cell production and the vast majority of upstream cathode active materials (CAM), anode graphite, electrolyte salts, and PVDF binders. The IRA in the US and CRMA in Europe have triggered a once-in-a-generation supply-chain reshoring effort worth hundreds of billions in committed investment through 2030. Key policy levers: IRA Section 45X manufacturing credits, EU Critical Raw Materials Act local sourcing targets, and explicit de-risking strategies from the major OEMs (VW, Stellantis, BMW, Tesla). The Western-supply-chain infrastructure layer — the companies that make the enabling chemicals, materials, and systems — is largely invisible to most investors, because it sits several steps back from the sexy "EV" headline.

### GEM 1 — Cabot Corporation

**Name:** Cabot Corporation

**Country:** USA (HQ Boston, MA)
**~Market cap:** ~$4.4B USD (June 2026)
**Stage:** Established compounder with a growing, early-stage battery-materials sub-segment

#### The Wave + Why Under-Covered / Early

Cabot is the global number-one in carbon black — a commodity chemical most people associate with tire rubber. The **battery angle is almost entirely invisible** in current analyst coverage: Cabot's conductive carbon additives (product line: LITX) are a critical picks-and-shovels ingredient in lithium-ion battery electrodes. Every EV cell and every grid BESS unit requires conductive additives to allow electrons to flow through the electrode. Cabot supplies virtually all major cell manufacturers. The segment has grown battery revenue **43% year-over-year in Q2 FY2026**, and management is guiding for ~$40M EBITDA from battery materials alone in FY2026 (growing from near-zero 2-3 years ago). The PowerCo SE (Volkswagen Group) multi-year supply agreement announced January 2026 positions Cabot as a "leading conductive materials supplier in the European EV battery market." This sits buried inside the Performance Chemicals segment of a company most analysts cover as a tire-additive play.

#### Moat

1. **#1 global scale in carbon black + proprietary flame synthesis processes** — 36 global manufacturing facilities with decades of process IP. Battery-grade LITX conductive additives require exacting particle size, surface area, and purity specs that only a handful of producers globally can reliably hit.
2. **Qualified at virtually every major lithium-ion cell manufacturer** — qualification takes 1-3 years of testing; once in, switching costs are high (changing a conductive additive forces re-qualification of the entire electrode formulation).
3. **Supply chain geography** — Cabot has US and European manufacturing, directly fitting the IRA/CRMA onshoring mandate. Most Chinese competitors cannot easily penetrate Western OEM supply chains post-tariff.
4. **Technology centre network** — Cabot has battery application technology centres in Europe, Asia, and North America, building deep co-development relationships with cell makers.

The core reinforcement materials (rubber carbon black) remains a steady-but-cyclical cash engine, currently in a cyclical trough (Asian tire imports pressuring North American margins). This is what the market is pricing — the battery inflection is not yet in estimates.

Syensqo signed multi-year contracts in Q1 2025 totalling **>EUR 150M in cumulative net sales** for Solef PVDF with automotive OEMs and battery makers — a signal that demand is contracted and real. - **Cabot (CBT) + Syensqo (SYENS)** are the two most compelling "hidden in plain sight" battery-materials compounders accessible in a UK ISA — both discounted relative to the long-term battery structural wave potential.
