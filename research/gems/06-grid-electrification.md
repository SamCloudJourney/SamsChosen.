## GEM 1: POWELL INDUSTRIES (POWL)
- US (Houston) | Nasdaq | ~$3.2B (down from ~$6B peak early 2026) | Mid-cap, inflection underway.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Custom-engineered medium-voltage switchgear, switchboards, power-control for utilities, oil & gas, data centers, LNG, industrial. Pure play on grid modernization + industrial electrification + AI data-center power. Nearly tripled revenue in 4 yrs, ROIC ~33%, yet only 8 analysts — a small-cap profile for a $3B company sitting in a classification blind spot.

**Moat:** Fully custom-engineered per order (specialty engineering + recurring hardware); mission-critical placement (2–3 yr qualification); dominant NA maker of arc-flash/explosion-proof switchgear for petrochem/LNG (3–6 month lead-time premiums); service flywheel; backlog $1.8B (Mar 2026) vs $1.1B LTM revenue = 18 months; electric-utility segment +50% in FY2025.

**Ownership:** Founded 1947 by William Powell; son Thomas Powell long-time CEO, ~17% (post-2025 unverified). Institutions ~52%.

**Economics (FY ends Sep):** Revenue FY21–25: $471→533→699→1,012→1,104M; net income $1→14→55→150→181M; gross margin 16%→29%; EBITDA $11→225M; EPS $0.02→$4.95. 4-yr CAGR ~24%; ROIC 33–35%; net cash $476M (zero debt, >15% of cap); FCF ~$155M FY2025 (~14% yield); backlog $1.8B (3x from $600M FY2022).

**Valuation:** ~$3.2B (−40% from peak). P/E ~17–18x; EV/EBITDA ~12–13x (discount to Eaton 34x, ABB, Schneider); P/FCF ~20x. Defensible with $1.8B backlog floor.

**10-yr bull:** Grid supercycle 2024–2035+; AI DCs need purpose-built switchgear; 6+ NA LNG terminals need explosion-proof gear; Remsdaq acquisition extends services. 15–20%/yr for 3–4 yrs then 8–10%; revenue $2.5–3B, EPS $10–15 → stock $300–450 vs ~$250.

**Risks:** (1) Margin cyclicality (low-teens pre-2022; could revert 800–1000bps if ABB/Eaton flood custom segment). (2) Order lumpiness/concentration (LNG/utility).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — US/NA real-economy infrastructure absent from the book. **Conviction: 4/5.**

## GEM 2: ARTECHE (ART.MC)
- Spain (Basque) | BME Main Market since Feb 2026 | ~€1.83B (+~339%/1yr) | Small-mid cap, just re-rated.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Top-3 global maker of instrument transformers (CT/VT/digital/optical) for substations up to 800kV — the sensing/measurement layer of every grid connection. Every new substation/HVDC converter/offshore-wind collector needs them. Virtually unknown outside Spain until early 2025; only moved to Main Market Feb 2026 (institutional discovery just beginning).

**Moat:** Per-regulator type-testing/approval = 3–5 yr barrier per product/market (certs in 80+ countries); top-3 globally with ABB and Trench/Siemens (>300kV ~3 suppliers); 51% Hitachi Energy GIS JV (AGIT) = captive demand; optical/digital transformers (ECO-5 600kV DC, SDO-OCT) = next-gen platform; 70-yr installed base.

**Ownership:** Arteche family retains control post-listing (BME Growth 2021 → Main 2026); exact % unverified (Spanish CNMV).

**Economics:** Revenue 2022–25 ~€360→401→450→508M; EBITDA ~€40→80.5M (margin ~11%→15.8%); net profit ~€10→45.3M (2024→25 +93%); EPS €0.33→0.79; net debt €22.7M (0.3x); orders 2025 €590M (>1x revenue), Network Reliability +26.2%; div €22.6M (50% payout). ROIC est 15–20% (unverified).

**Valuation:** ~€1.83B. P/E ~34–40x; EV/EBITDA ~22–23x; ~3.6x sales. Not cheap after +339%; PEG arguably <0.5x. Analyst FV ~€20–29 (below ~€32 now) — much of the re-rating priced.

**10-yr bull:** Substation build-out (offshore wind, HVDC, solar, EV, DCs) needs hundreds of thousands of installs; optical/digital is the platform for digital substations. If digital → 25–30% of new builds by 2035 at higher margins, revenue €1.5–2B at 18–20% EBITDA = 3–5x.

**Risks:** (1) Valuation after +339%. (2) ABB/Siemens/Trench capacity expansion.

**Portfolio fit:** The most "Bachem-like" pick — niche global top-3, certification-gated, invisible to generalists. Replace **Secunet** or watch-list/starter. **Conviction: 3.5/5** (4/5 on a 20–30% pullback to ~€22–25).

## GEM 3: VOLTAMP TRANSFORMERS (VOLTAMP.NS)
- India (Vadodara) | NSE | ~₹9,600Cr (~$1.15B, post-20% Q4 sell-off) | Small-mid, unknown to global investors.

**Wave & under-coverage:** India electrifying fast (PM-KUSUM ₹3.03tn, RDSS, data centers, industrial capex); transformer market +12–18%/yr. Voltamp leads premium dry-type (air-cooled, confined urban/industrial spaces). Barely followed beyond a few India analysts.

**Moat:** ~40% of India's organised dry-type market (HTT Germany tech transfer 1990s; 2 decades cast-resin know-how); premium positioning (avoids low-margin tenders; serves L&T, Tata Projects, Technip, Petrofac, Toyo, EIL); 80,000+ installations / 62 yrs; highest realisation per MVA; new power-transformer factory (Jul 2026); net cash ₹74Cr.

**Ownership — flag:** Promoter 30% (down from 50% in FY2023 — investigate intent). FII 22.8%, DII 31.7% (institutions now own more than promoters).

**Economics (FY ends Mar):** Revenue FY22–26 ₹1,127→2,154Cr (~17% CAGR); EBITDA margin ~18%→13–14% (Q4 weak); ROCE ~22%→27.6% (peak ~32% FY2024); net cash ₹74–77Cr. FY2026 revenue ₹2,154Cr (+11.3%) but Q4 net profit −50.5% (rupee/raw-material costs, vendor diversion to exports, fixed-cost deleverage, bond MTM). Order book ~₹1,500Cr (~7 months).

**Valuation:** ~₹9,600Cr. P/E ~31x (on depressed FY2026 — Q4 anomalous); EV/EBITDA ~20x on normalized margins; P/B ~5x. Normalized 17–18% margins imply higher earnings power.

**10-yr bull:** Industrial/DC capex needs dry-type everywhere; deepening manufacturing (semis, defense, chemicals); new power-transformer factory adds a stream. 15–18% for 5 yrs then 10% → ₹7,000–8,000Cr revenue; at 17% EBITDA, 20x → ₹25,000–30,000Cr = ~2.5–3x.

**Risks:** (1) Promoter stake reduction 50%→30% (biggest concern — investigate). (2) Margin cyclicality / copper-CRGO / INR sensitivity.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — concentrated active India infrastructure compounder, price just reset. **Conviction: 3.5/5** (starter now; build on promoter-intent clarity + margin recovery).

## GEM 4: NKT A/S (NKT.CO)
- Denmark (Brøndby) | Nasdaq Copenhagen | ~€7.9B (~$8.7B) | Above the $10B "hidden" threshold — borderline, included for completeness.

**Why it matters:** One of ~4–5 firms globally able to make/install 525kV HVDC submarine cables (needs specialized extrusion tower, cable-lay vessel, proprietary insulation, offshore expertise — NKT owns all four). €10.2B high-voltage backlog ≈ 4 yrs of HV revenue. Drivers: European offshore wind + HVDC interconnectors (Eastern Green Link 3, LanWin7, NordOstLink). Well-covered in Denmark (14 Nordic analysts) but absent from most global portfolios.

**Economics (2025):** Revenue €2,722M at SMP; op EBITDA €390M (14.3% margin, record); net €275M; +6% organic; ROIC >15% (capex years 2025–28 ~€2B total suppress FCF); FCF 2025 −€244M (€750M capex); 2028 targets: revenue CAGR >14% from 2021, op EBITDA >€700M, RoCE >20%; EV/EBITDA ~20–22x.

**Risks:** (1) €2B capex execution (new vessel NKT Eleonora, Karlskrona). (2) Capacity wave (Nexans, Prysmian, LS, Sumitomo) eroding oligopoly pricing 2028–30. (3) No family alignment. (4) Softer 2026 guidance.

**Portfolio fit:** Fills European offshore-wind/energy-transition slot; could partly replace **Vietnam**. Least "gem-like" of the four. **Conviction: 3/5.**
