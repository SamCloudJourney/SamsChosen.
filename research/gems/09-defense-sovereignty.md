## GEM 1: RENK GROUP (R3NK.DE)
- Germany | Xetra | ~€5.7B | Post-IPO (Feb 2024), scaling.

**Wave & under-coverage:** The drivetrain of European land warfare — powerpack transmissions for the Leopard 2 (NATO's armored backbone), marine gearboxes for naval vessels, industrial slide bearings. Every new/upgraded Leopard 2 or frigate runs through RENK — the obscure mechanical heart inside the weapon system. Thinly covered post-IPO (Triton PE exited Aug 2025 at a 7x return); KNDS holds 15.8% (strategic anchor); analysts only now building coverage.

**Moat:** Military powerpacks need decades of per-platform qualification — no newcomer displaces a certified RENK transmission without multi-year NATO-wide re-qual; ~55% aftermarket/MRO (recurring, high-margin, grows with the fleet); dual moat (land + naval); €6.68bn backlog (Dec 2025) = 4.9x FY2025 revenue.

**Ownership:** KNDS N.V. 15.8% (needs RENK for its own products — won't sell); management (Rebecca Mgmt) 4.2%; Triton fully exited (overhang cleared); BlackRock ~3.5%, Norges ~2.1%.

**Economics (FY2025):** Revenue €1.37bn (+19.8%); adj EBIT €230m (16.9% margin; VMS segment 20.4%); backlog €6.68bn (record); book-to-bill 1.2x; FCF €67m (~47% conversion — low, capacity ramp); net debt €391m (1.5x). 2026 guidance: revenue >€1.5bn, adj EBIT €255–285m; 2030 target €2.8–3.2bn at >20% margin.

**Valuation:** EV ~€6.1bn; EV/EBITDA ~24x; P/E ~50x; P/S ~4.1x; EV/2026E EBIT ~22x. Not cheap, but 4.9x backlog = minimal revenue-visibility risk; off ~45% from €90 peak to ~€49 (some compression done).

**10-yr bull:** Zeitenwende structural; Bundeswehr to 203,000 soldiers + more Leopard 2s through 2032; Poland 1,000 tanks; Baltic naval build-out. At €3bn revenue / 20% EBIT (€600m) and 20x EV/EBIT → €12bn EV ≈ 2x current.

**Risks:** (1) Cycle reversal / political risk (backlog insulates several years). (2) Margin-target execution. (3) Valuation compression if rates rise (50x P/E).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — German-defense growth optionality + a real moat + asset-specific upside; fills the absence of European industrial/hardware defense (Secunet is software). **Conviction: 4/5** — clearest blind spot; accumulate on weakness.

## GEM 2: EXAIL TECHNOLOGIES (EXA.PA)
- France | Euronext Paris (Compartment B) | ~€2.4B | Family-controlled, post-restructuring growth.

**Wave & under-coverage:** World leader in fiber-optic-gyroscope (FOG) inertial navigation systems and autonomous underwater vehicles — the navigational brains of submarines, surface vessels, torpedoes, autonomous mine-countermeasure drones, precision artillery. Navies protecting undersea cables / deploying autonomous mine-clearance need Exail. Invisible: was Groupe Gorgé until late 2022 (data aggregation broken); €2.4B Compartment-B cap; loss-making until 2025 (screened out by quality filters); ECA + iXblue combination only closed 2022.

**Moat:** iXblue = global #2 in FOG INS behind Northrop Litef, far more export-accessible — NATO navies wanting a non-US system (France, UK, Germany, India, Australia) come to Exail (sovereignty in its purest form); ECA AUVs dominate French Navy SLAMF; naval simulation; FOG photonic-assembly know-how built over decades.

**Ownership:** Gorgé family (Pelican Venture + Gorgé SA) 41.4% capital / 56.3% votes; Bpifrance minority; free float ~66% after Mar-2026 placement. **Flag:** Gorgé SA sold shares Mar 2026 — monitor disposal pace.

**Economics (FY2025):** Revenue €479m (+28%); "current EBITDA" €103m (+40%, ~21.5% margin, H2 23%); net income €6m (first profit; FY2024 −€5m); order intake €844m (+87%, record); backlog €1.1bn (+52%); net debt €114m (1.1x, excl ICG bonds); op cash flow >€100m; 2026+ guidance double-digit growth, 25% EBITDA target. EV ~€2.35bn; EV/2025 EBITDA ~23x; +~370% since early 2025.

**Flags:** "current EBITDA" is non-IFRS; GAAP net income depressed for years by iXblue amortization. ICG bonds excluded from stated net debt.

**10-yr bull:** FOG INS becomes sovereignty-critical for every NATO navy reducing US-GPS dependence; AUVs standard for EU mine-countermeasures. At €1bn revenue / 25% EBITDA (€250m) and 18x → €4.5bn EV vs €2.35bn = ~90% over 4 yrs.

**Risks:** (1) Family overhang/governance. (2) GAAP losses for years (index ineligibility). (3) French-Navy concentration (SLAMF).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **VinaCapital Vietnam** — family-controlled specialist (mirrors EXOR/Investor/Vitec/Topicus owner-operator pattern) with a deep naval-sovereignty moat. **Conviction: 3.5/5** (window may be closing post-+370%).

## GEM 3: CHEMRING GROUP (CHG.L)
- UK | LSE Main | ~£1.5B | Margin step-up.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Two segments — Countermeasures & Energetics (flares, decoys, chaff for aircraft/naval self-protection) and Sensors & Information (EW, CBRN detection, cyber). Largest air-countermeasures supplier in Europe; near-monopoly with UK/US/European air forces. Under-covered: history of operational problems (Tennessee, now resolved); "boring munitions"; ESG selling pressure. Countermeasures are consumables — fired, lost, replenished (recurring demand pool; Ukraine/Middle East de-stocking now being replenished).

**Moat:** UK's only qualified flare/decoy maker; US consolidated; consumable replenishment; CBRN/sensors growing; explosive-manufacturing facilities need 5–10 yr permitting/workforce/safety builds.

**Ownership — weakness:** No founder/family; insiders <1% (CEO 0.21%); institutional (Merian 7.9%, AXA 7.3%, Royal London 5.3%). Weakest ownership of the lane.

**Economics (FY2025, Oct YE):** Revenue £498m (+2%, +3% cc); underlying op margin 19.1% (+280bps); order book £1.3bn (+32%); underlying op cash £112.2m (114% of underlying EBITDA); net debt £89m. 2030 target ~£1bn. Mkt cap ~£1.5bn; P/E ~27x (sector avg 37–44x — cheapest in the lane). FCF currently negative (−£2.3m TTM; capex £93m capacity build; 3-yr conversion 101%).

**Adversarial:** Revenue only +2% despite the order book (capacity/operational lag — Tennessee now "delivering the plan" but still constraining); £1.3bn = 2.6x coverage (less than RENK's 4.9x), ~£413m earmarked for FY2026.

**10-yr bull:** Countermeasures resupply emergency; UK 2.5%→3% of GDP; Tennessee enables 20%+ US growth. At £1bn / 20% margins (£200m op) at 20x → £4bn = ~2.7x.

**Risks:** (1) Tennessee re-lapse (single biggest). (2) No owner-operator. (3) UK political/FX.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Bioventix** (marginal — Bioventix's near-monopoly + 50%+ margins make it the hardest to replace) or **Sartorius**. **Conviction: 3/5.**
