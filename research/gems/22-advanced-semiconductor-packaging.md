## WHY THIS LANE NOW

Advanced packaging has become the **primary battleground for AI-era chip performance**. The laws of physics have broken Moore's Law scaling in the traditional sense — but chiplet disaggregation, hybrid bonding at sub-5µm pitch, 2.5D/3D stacking, and HBM3e/HBM4 memory architectures are doing what new process nodes cannot. Every AI accelerator (Nvidia H200/B200, AMD MI300, Intel Gaudi) relies on sophisticated packaging, not just front-end silicon.

**Market size signals:**
- Back-end equipment market: ~$6.9B in 2025 → ~$9.2B by 2030 (5.8% CAGR). [Yole Group]
- Hybrid bonding equipment sub-segment: ~$152M in 2025 → ~$397M by 2030 (21.1% CAGR). [Yole Group]
- ABF substrate market: ~$4.9B in 2025 → ~$9.5B by 2033 (10.6% CAGR). [MarketReportAnalytics]
- Chiplet interconnect market: $2.17B in 2025 → $41.2B by 2035 (34.4% CAGR). [WorldEcoMag]

**Why under-covered:**
Most investor attention flows to front-end semicap giants (ASML, AMAT, Lam, KLA) and the obvious OSAT duopoly (ASE, Amkor). The **enabling picks-and-shovels** into advanced packaging — the bonders, the inspection/metrology systems, the photomask makers — are systematically overlooked. These names are smaller, less covered by sell-side, and often more defensible because their technology is spec'd in deeply and customer-qualified for years.
