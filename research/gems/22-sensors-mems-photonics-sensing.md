## GEM 1: SOITEC (SOI.PA -- EPA / Euronext Paris)

France | ~EUR 6.3B mkt cap | Stage: compounder in a cyclical trough with structural inflection in Photonics-SOI

### Wave and why under-covered / early

Every silicon-photonics transceiver and co-packaged optics module in every AI data centre is fabricated on a Silicon-on-Insulator (SOI) wafer. Soitec holds greater than 95% market share in photonics-grade SOI (Bank of America, March 2026 optical interconnects primer) -- protected by an exclusive Smart Cut technology licence from CEA-Leti and 3,500+ related patents. GlobalWafers attempted to compete; their Smart Cut licence was terminated October 2023, with a settlement granting an exit runway through 2027, after which they leave the market entirely. Soitec's photonics monopoly gets MORE concentrated by 2027, not less.

The wave: optical interconnects are replacing copper between GPUs as AI clusters scale past 1,000kW/rack. The optical interconnect market is projected from USD 14B (2025) to USD 73B by 2030 (+39% CAGR). Every incremental transceiver sold embeds a Soitec Photonics-SOI wafer -- the substrate at the absolute bottom of the value chain that nobody else can provide.

Why under-covered: the stock was reclassified by most indices as general semiconductor materials and carries almost no photonics-specific buy-side research. Its primary revenue (RF-SOI for smartphones) is deep in an inventory correction -- FY2026 total revenue fell 34% YoY to EUR 592M, creating maximum pessimism that obscures the Photonics-SOI inflection. Stock was down ~75% from 2022 peak before recovering 200%+ off December 2025 lows.

### Moat

- Exclusive Smart Cut licence from CEA-Leti (the inventors of the technology)
- Greater than 95% photonics-SOI market share -- no qualified alternative at volume; GlobalWafers exiting 2027 further concentrates the monopoly
- 3,500+ patents covering process steps and wafer specifications
- Co-packaged optics and 1.6T+ transceiver design-win across all major transceiver OEMs (Coherent, NVIDIA/Cisco supply chains, and Chinese fabs)
- Every new co-packaged optics generation requires new Photonics-SOI specifications -- creating ongoing re-qualification switching costs

### Founder / insider ownership

Soitec was founded by two CEA researchers. Today's major shareholders: National Silicon Industry Group (China, ~11%), Bpifrance (~11%), CEA Investissement (~11%), Fonds Strategique de Participations (~8%). Top 12 shareholders hold ~51% combined. French state-adjacent consortium provides stability and strategic sovereignty protection. No single founder family; state institutional backstop replaces that function. CEO Laurent Remont appointed April 2026 (ex-Infineon MEMS/Magnetics GM -- directly relevant experience).

### Sourced economics

- FY2025 (year to March 2025) revenue: EUR 891M
- FY2026 (year to March 2026) revenue: EUR 592M (-34% YoY -- inventory correction in RF-SOI and automotive)
- Photonics-SOI revenue FY2026: crossed USD 100M; 60-70% YoY growth implied by Morgan Stanley (earlier than guidance)
- FY2026 EBITDA margin: held at 25.4%; net debt/EBITDA improved to 0.4x (near net-cash)
- FY2026 FCF: EUR 63M vs consensus EUR 6M -- massive beat; shares +14% on results day (27 May 2026)
- FY2027 Q1 guidance: +15% YoY at constant currency
- Photonics-SOI management targets 20-30% annual growth from ~EUR 100M base; Morgan Stanley models 35%+ for FY2027
- ROIC unverified at trough; pre-correction ROIC was ~20%+ on capital-light photonics process (flag -- unverified)
- Net debt/EBITDA: 0.4x -- essentially unlevered

### Valuation

- Price ~EUR 178 (post +14% surge 27 May 2026)
- Market cap ~EUR 6.3B
- P/E TTM: negative (trough net income)
- EV/Revenue ~9x on trough EUR 592M
- If Photonics-SOI hits EUR 300-350M by FY2028 and RF-SOI/automotive recover, combined revenue EUR 900M-1.1B plausible. At 25% EBITDA margin and 20x EV/EBITDA -> EUR 4.5-5.5B EBITDA-based EV (already embedded in EUR 6.3B cap). The photonics super-bull is the incremental upside.
- Entry discipline: cheapest window was EUR 23-50 (Dec 2025); at EUR 178 the recovery is partially priced; position-sizing and entry below EUR 150 improve risk/reward

### 10-yr bull + asymmetric math

Base: Photonics-SOI grows to EUR 500M by FY2031 (38% CAGR from EUR 100M); RF-SOI recovers to EUR 600M; total FY2031 revenue EUR 1.1B; 28% EBITDA = EUR 308M; 20x EV/EBITDA -> EUR 6.2B, approximately flat from here. ## GEM 2: SENSIRION (SENS.CH -- SIX Swiss Exchange)

### Wave and why under-covered / early

Structural waves:

1. HVAC A2L refrigerant leak detection -- new EPA regulations mandate A2L (mildly flammable) leak sensors in every US air-conditioning unit; Sensirion established itself as the global market leader within one year (50%+ share of new A2L market by 2025; SGD4x reference design adopted by major US HVAC OEMs).

3. Methane monitoring and industrial gas analysis -- acquisition of Kuva Systems (Cambridge, MA) June 2025 for satellite-grade methane emissions monitoring; industrial segment +48% YoY FY2025.

## GEM 3: MELEXIS (MELE.BE -- Euronext Brussels)

Belgium | ~EUR 2.4B mkt cap | Stage: quality automotive sensor IC compounder at a cyclical trough

### Wave and why under-covered / early

Melexis designs fabless semiconductor sensor ICs for automotive applications -- Hall-effect magnetic position sensors, current sensors (battery management), temperature sensors, smart motor drivers. Every EV has dramatically more sensors than an ICE vehicle: each battery module needs current + temperature sensors; each motor inverter needs Hall-effect position sensing; ADAS needs multiple blind-spot + parking sensors. Global vehicle fleet electrification multiplies sensor content per vehicle 3-5x.

Melexis's patented Triaxis 3D magnetic field sensing technology (measures Bx, By, Bz simultaneously in one automotive-grade CMOS chip) has no disclosed equivalent at the same precision/integration level. Tier 1 suppliers (Bosch, Continental, Aptiv) and OEMs spec Melexis ICs by family name -- the invisible mandatory ingredient.

Why under-covered: Belgian-listed, fewer than 10 analyst followers, lumped with general automotive semis alongside Infineon and ST; the Triaxis monopoly in 3D magnetic sensing rarely highlighted.

### Moat

- Triaxis 3D Hall-effect monopoly -- Melexis holds primary patents; automotive qualification of a competing part takes 18-36 months minimum
- Design-win stickiness: automotive ICs go through IATF 16949 / AEC-Q100 qualification embedded in powertrain ECU designs with 5-10 year platform lifetimes; once designed in, the revenue is annuity-like
- Fabless model (outsources wafer production mostly to X-FAB, whose founding family is the same Duchatelet network -- vertical alignment): R&D/IP-intensive, capital-light, 45%+ gross margins targeted
- Long-term revenue target EUR 1.1B by 2028 (from ~EUR 840M 2025; ~10% CAGR): driven by sensor content per vehicle multiplying with EV/ADAS penetration

### Founder / insider ownership

Duchatelet family (via Elex) and De Winter-Chombar family (via Sensinnovat) collectively hold the founding stake -- previously held jointly via Xtrion NV; restructured November 2023 into separate vehicles but combined economic ownership remains substantial (~40%+ estimated, exact post-restructuring split unverified -- flag). Both founding families confirmed no plans to reduce shareholdings. Roland Duchatelet remains a board director.

### Sourced economics

- FY2025 revenue: EUR 839.6M (-10% vs FY2024; automotive sector inventory correction)
- FY2025 EBITDA: ~EUR 172M (~20.5% margin -- compressed from peak 25%+)
- Q1 2026 revenue: EUR 182.1M (+3% YoY); EBITDA EUR 88.2M (+5% YoY); net profit EUR 67.4M
- FY2026 guidance: EUR 750-800M revenue + gross margin 52-54%
- Long-term target: high single-digit revenue growth, ~25% EBIT margin
- ROIC: 10.03% TTM (depressed; historical peak 20%+ -- unverified, flag)
- Net margin TTM: 13.2%; ROE: 20.2%

### Valuation

- Price ~EUR 68 (June 2026 estimate); market cap ~EUR 2.4B
- P/E TTM: ~22.6x; forward P/E: ~18x
- EV/EBITDA: ~14.7x
- Long-term scenario: EUR 1.1B revenue at 25% EBIT = EUR 275M EBIT; 18x -> EUR 4.95B EV vs current EUR 2.4B cap = ~2x compounding return on the base case alone
- Cheapest on forward multiples in 5+ years

### 10-yr bull + asymmetric math

Base: EV penetration drives sensor content/vehicle up 3x; ADAS standard on 70%+ new vehicles by 2030; Melexis grows at 8% CAGR to EUR 1.8B by FY2033; 25% EBIT = EUR 450M; 18x -> EUR 8.1B EV (~3.4x from EUR 2.4B).
