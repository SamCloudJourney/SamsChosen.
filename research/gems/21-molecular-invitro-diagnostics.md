## PICK 1: DiaSorin (DIA.MI | Borsa Italiana)

**Italy | Borsa Italiana (Milan) | ~EUR 3.2-3.6B market cap (as of May 2026) | Profitable compounder, post-acquisition integration trough**

### Wave & why under-covered / early

Two structural waves: (1) Infectious-disease molecular testing becoming standard of care globally, and (2) specialist immunoassay platforms displacing general chemistry in niche "specialty" tests (vitamin D, hormone panels, transplant monitoring, TB screening). DiaSorin occupies the specialist IVD tier below Roche/Abbott's commodity battleground — 140+ proprietary assays mostly with no viable substitute on LIAISON. The Luminex acquisition (2021, $1.8B) added ~16,000 xMAP multiplex instruments and 70+ IVD partners, creating a second installed base. Market cap has de-rated ~50% from peak as COVID tailwinds unwound and Luminex integration costs weighed on margins. The business is now being re-rated by the street as a slower-growth story; but the underlying reagent pull-through from 45,000+ LIAISON instruments and 16,000+ xMAP instruments is durable. ~15 analysts cover it; not undiscovered by name but misunderstood on the long-cycle installed-base economics.

### Moat

- Installed base lock-in: LIAISON instruments are fully closed (LIAISON reagents only); switching requires revalidation, FDA/CE resubmission, staff retraining — 18-24 month friction minimum.
- 140+ proprietary assays: Many are sole-source (infectious disease serology, transplant panel, vitamin D method). No mass-market equivalent.
- Luminex xMAP open platform: 70+ IVD partners develop assays ON Luminex hardware, giving DiaSorin reagent economics without needing to own every assay.
- Regulatory dossiers: Each assay carries CE-IVD / FDA 510(k) approval; competitor cannot enter niche without 3-5 year regulatory investment.
- QuantiFeron-TB Gold (QFT): DiaSorin is the worldwide partner behind this TB test — near-monopoly in IGRA-based TB screening.

### Founder / insider alignment

Denegri family controls via IP Investimenti e Partecipazioni S.r.l. — owns ~46% of capital; loyalty shares give ~60% of voting rights. Chairman: Michele Denegri; CEO: Carlo Rosa (~4.6% direct stake, bought into the 2000 MBO alongside Denegri family). Combined insiders/family ~50%+ economic interest. Structure originated from a 2000 management buyout.

### Sourced economics (FY2025)

Revenue: EUR 1,195M (+4% constant FX) [DiaSorin FY2025 press release]
Adj. EBITDA: EUR 394M (33% margin) [DiaSorin FY2025 press release]
Adj. EBIT: ~EUR 233M (26% of revenue, 9M 2025 annualised) [Q3 2025 results]
FCF: EUR 209M (FY2025) [DiaSorin FY2025 press release]
Net debt: EUR 580M (improved EUR 38M YoY) [DiaSorin FY2025 press release]
ROIC (TTM): ~7-8% (includes Luminex goodwill) [GuruFocus/Macroaxis]
Installed base: 45,000+ LIAISON + 16,000+ xMAP instruments [Company presentations]
Reagent mix: ~70% of revenue recurring [flagged: estimate, not explicitly disclosed]

2026 guidance (confirmed Q1 2026): Revenue growth 5-6%; EBITDA margin 32-33% (H2 back-loaded — Q1 weak flu season, QuantiFeron stocking correction). 2027 target: high single-digit to low double-digit CAGR, EBITDA 36-37%.

### Valuation

Market cap: ~EUR 3.2-3.6B (Milan, May 2026). EV: ~EUR 3.75B. EV/EBITDA (TTM): ~10x — significant discount to comparable niche IVD peers. Trailing P/E: ~22x. FCF yield: ~6% on current market cap. The stock is pricing in the trough, not the mid-cycle.

### 10-year bull case / asymmetric math

Base case: Revenue grows 7-8% CAGR (2025-2035). EBITDA reaches 37-38% margin by 2030. By 2035 — revenue ~EUR 2.3B, EBITDA ~EUR 870M; at 14-16x EV/EBITDA = EUR 12-14B EV. Less net debt (~0), approximately EUR 12-14B equity value vs current ~EUR 3.6B = 3-4x over 10 years (18-20% CAGR including dividends).

Bull case: Luminex synergies overshoot, BIOFIRE-style syndromic PCR expansion drives 10%+ CAGR, EBITDA recovers to 37%+ by 2028: 20x EV/EBITDA on EUR 1B EBITDA = EUR 20B EV = 5x+ on today.

Asymmetric floor: Denegri family would never sell below intrinsic value; EUR 209M FCF means <20 years payback at zero growth.

### Top 3 risks

1. Luminex integration underperformance: Margins still 33% vs pre-Luminex 38-40%. If xMAP never recovers to target margins, DiaSorin could be permanently de-rated.
2. QuantiFeron structural decline: TB screening mandates can change (US immigration policy shift 2024-25 already hurt). QFT is ~10-15% of revenue [flagged: estimate].
3. Carlo Rosa succession risk: CEO is architect of both franchise and Luminex deal. No publicly named successor.

### UK ISA buyability

Borsa Italiana (Euronext Milan); ISIN IT0003492391. Euronext Milan is an HMRC-recognised EEA exchange. CLAUDE.md confirms Borsa Italiana as a supported T212 venue. ASSESS: BUYABLE in UK T212 S&S ISA. Verify DIA is not view-only before trading.

### Portfolio fit

Replaces none directly (adds differentiated IVD position). Most natural to fund from India ETF (passive) or as a watchlist addition pending Luminex margin recovery demonstration in H2 2026. Blind spot filled: physician-diagnostics consumables, Italy/EU exposure, non-software recurring moat.

Conviction: 3.5/5. Moat scores 4.5/5; entry conviction reduced by Luminex margin overhang and Q1 2026 revenue miss.

## PICK 2: bioMérieux (BIM.PA | Euronext Paris)

**France | Euronext Paris | ~EUR 8.6-9.9B market cap (April/May 2026) | Profitable large-cap compounder; infectious disease diagnostics**

### Wave & why under-covered / early

The wave is antimicrobial resistance (AMR) + syndromic PCR at point-of-care. BIOFIRE FilmArray is the global market-leading syndromic multiplex PCR platform — one cartridge, 45 minutes, identifies the exact pathogen and resistance gene from blood, CSF, respiratory, GI specimens. As AMR rises and "identify-the-right-antibiotic-fast" becomes a hospital survival metric, BIOFIRE becomes mandatory infrastructure. While bioMérieux is not an unknown, its re-rating potential from a structurally depressed ~17.9% CEBIT margin to 23%+ potential by 2028 (GO•28 plan) is not priced in. The stock has de-rated ~25% in 6 months on Q1 2026 respiratory demand weakness + China headwinds + currency. Family controlled at >50% capital / ~65% voting rights through Institut Mérieux — management will not be pressured into short-term dilutive actions.

### Moat

- BIOFIRE FilmArray: 2,500+ installed systems in US hospitals; cartridge-based closed system; 170+ targets; switching requires complete workflow overhaul.
- BACT/ALERT blood culture: Dominant automated blood culture monitoring system globally for sepsis diagnosis.
- VITEK 2 + VITEK MS: Automated ID/AST (antibiotic susceptibility testing) in essentially every major hospital microbiology lab globally.
- Reagent pull-through model: >95% of revenue from consumables/reagents/services post-instrument placement.
- Institut Merieux / family governance: Long-termism prevents dilutive M&A or financial engineering.

### Founder / insider alignment

Institut Merieux (controlled by Merieux family via CMA holding company + Fondation Christophe et Rodolphe Merieux) holds >50% of capital and ~65% of voting rights [bioMerieux governance pages, finanzwire.com 2025]. The Merieux family has controlled this business since its founding. EXOR (Agnelli family, already in current portfolio at 8%) also holds a stake in Institut Merieux — connected to the current book.

### Sourced economics (FY2025)

Revenue: EUR 4,070M (+6.2% like-for-like) [bioMerieux FY2025 press release]
Gross profit: EUR 2,309M (56.7% margin) [bioMerieux FY2025 press release]
CEBIT (adj. EBIT): EUR 728M (+16% constant FX; 17.9% margin) [bioMerieux FY2025 press release]
FCF H1 2025: EUR 170M (>3x H1 2024) [bioMerieux H1 2025 results]
Net cash position: EUR 108M (vs EUR 41M net debt at end-2024) [bioMerieux FY2025 press release]
Debt/EBITDA: 0.52x [GuruFocus]

2026 guidance (revised down Q1 2026): Sales growth 3-5% (originally 5-7%); CEBIT growth 0-10%. Headwinds: weak respiratory season, China geopolitical tensions. GO•28 strategic plan ambition (to 2028): +7% CAGR sales; CEBIT +10%/yr; ~500bps margin expansion.

### Valuation

Market cap: ~EUR 8.6-9.9B (118M shares; volatile May 2026 after Q1 disappointment + guidance cut).
EV/EBITDA: ~10-11x [flagged: bioMerieux reports CEBIT not EBITDA; this estimate uses analyst-consensus EBITDA approximation — verify before acting].
P/E TTM: ~22x.
FCF yield: ~3.5-4% at current market cap (improving rapidly).

bioMerieux historically traded at 25-35x P/E during 2020-2022. Current ~22x represents a trough re-rating. Ex-COVID core business growing 5-6% and accelerating.

### 10-year bull case / asymmetric math

Base case (GO•28 + beyond): 7% revenue CAGR to ~EUR 8.5B by 2035; CEBIT margin expands to 23-25% as operating leverage kicks in. At 20x EV/CEBIT = EUR 38-42B EV. Less net cash = equity value ~EUR 38-42B vs current ~EUR 9B = 4-5x over 10 years (~16-20% CAGR).

Bull case: AMR becomes a formal healthcare crisis driving global government mandates for syndromic PCR in all emergency departments; BIOFIRE becomes the GeneXpert of AMR; revenue inflects to 10%+ CAGR. 6-7x return by 2035.

Asymmetric floor: Merieux family will not sell at distressed prices. Installed base of BACT/ALERT, VITEK, and FilmArray generates EUR 2.5-3B in annuity reagent revenue that would attract any strategic buyer at 5-6x revenue = EUR 12.5-18B, more than 1.5-2x current market cap.

### Top 3 risks

1. China geopolitical/procurement risk: ~15-20% of revenue from China; domestic alternative redirection risk.
2. Respiratory seasonality: A significant portion of BIOFIRE volume from annual respiratory season. Mild seasons (like Q1 2026) create real but transient headwinds.
3. Premium valuation normalisation: Even at current trough, not obviously cheap on P/FCF. If re-rating to industrial multiples (P/E 15x) continues, stock could drift lower before recovering.

### UK ISA buyability

Euronext Paris; BIM.PA; ISIN FR0013280286. Euronext Paris is an HMRC-recognised EEA exchange. France is a T212-supported market. ASSESS: BUYABLE in UK T212 S&S ISA. Verify BIM is not view-only before trading.

### Portfolio fit

Replaces India ETF (passive to active diagnostics compounder with family control) or complements Eurofins in the European quality cluster. Could also partially replace Sartorius Stedim (different businesses; both EUR 4-10B European life-science infrastructure). Blind spot filled: infection diagnostics, antimicrobial resistance, global reagent recurring revenue. French family-controlled compounder at IVD scale.

Conviction: 3.5/5. Business quality 4.5/5; entry timing 3/5 (China headwind, guidance cut, EUR 8.6B market cap is not micro-cap). Best framed as "buy the Merieux franchise at a cyclical trough."

## PICK 3: Veracyte (VCYT | Nasdaq)

**US | Nasdaq | ~$2.7-2.8B market cap (May 2026) | Precision oncology genomic diagnostics — profitability inflection**

### Wave & why under-covered / early

Cancer genomics companion diagnostics is the fastest-growing diagnostics sub-segment. The model: submit a proprietary test for NCCN guideline inclusion — once included, payers are compelled to reimburse — the test becomes the clinical standard — switching costs rise to near-zero as oncologists rely on the result. Veracyte has executed this model twice (Afirma for thyroid, Decipher for prostate), is executing for bladder, and is building MRD / liquid biopsy pipeline. The "hidden" value is the GRID database — 800,000+ patient genomic profiles — which functions as a proprietary moat: no competitor can replicate this data without repeating 10+ years of clinical validation.

### Moat

- NCCN-only status for Decipher Prostate: Decipher is the ONLY gene expression test recommended in NCCN prostate cancer guidelines (Category 1 evidence). Clinical mandate; any urologist ordering to NCCN guidelines must use Decipher.
- Afirma thyroid: ~70% of thyroid FNA biopsies that are "indeterminate" are now resolved by Afirma.
- GRID database (800,000+ profiles): ML algorithms trained on this dataset outperform any new entrant who lacks the clinical validation dataset. Network effect moat.
- New product pipeline: Prosigna LDT (breast), TrueMRD (MRD for cancer surveillance), Decipher Bladder.
- Net cash $439M (Q1 2026): No existential financing risk.

### Founder / insider alignment — WEAKNESS

NOT founder-led. CEO Marc Stapley owns ~357,000 shares (~0.45% of 80M shares outstanding; flagged as low). CEO has been selling shares on a 10b5-1 plan. Business quality compensates for absence of founder alignment; NCCN anchor and GRID database are institutional moats that persist regardless.

### Sourced economics (FY2025 / Q1 2026)

Revenue FY2025: $517.1M (+16%; testing $493.2M +18%) [Veracyte FY2025 8-K]
Gross margin: 70.1% (GAAP) [Veracyte FY2025 8-K]
Adj. EBITDA: $142.5M (27.6% margin) [Veracyte FY2025 8-K]
GAAP net income: $66.4M (+175%) [Veracyte FY2025 8-K]
FCF: ~$126.6M [Veracyte FY2025 8-K]
Cash/investments: $439.1M (Q1 2026; net cash) [Veracyte Q1 2026 8-K]
Q1 2026 revenue: $139.1M (+21%; testing +26%) [Veracyte Q1 2026 8-K]
Q1 2026 adj EBITDA margin: 30.8% [Veracyte Q1 2026 8-K]
2026 guidance (raised Q1 2026): Total revenue $582-592M (+13-14%); adj. EBITDA margin >26%

### Valuation

Market cap: ~$2.7-2.8B (80M shares; ~$33-35/share, May 2026).
EV/adj. EBITDA (FY2026E): ~14-16x (EV net of $439M cash ~$2.3B; EBITDA 2026E ~$150-155M).
P/FCF: ~21-22x on FY2025 FCF; lower on 2026E FCF as margin expands.
GAAP P/E: ~94x TTM (elevated; forward P/E much lower as profitability accelerates).

### 10-year bull case / asymmetric math

Base case: Revenue grows 15% CAGR (2025-2030), moderating to 10% CAGR (2030-2035) = FY2035 revenue ~$2B. EBITDA margins reach 40% = EBITDA $800M. At 25x EV/EBITDA = $20B EV + cash = $21B+ equity. Vs current ~$2.7B = ~8x in 10 years.

Asymmetric upside: If TrueMRD achieves FDA breakthrough + NCCN inclusion, addressable market expands by $5B+. Revenue could inflect to $5B by 2035. 15-20x scenario.

Downside floor: $439M cash + FCF generation = 16% cash-to-mktcap buffer. NCCN-embedded tests do not disappear — payers face clinical/legal challenge if they stopped reimbursing a Category 1 guideline test. Floor: $1.5B+ (>50% haircut required to break).

### Top 3 risks

1. Reimbursement policy reversal: If CMS redefines coverage policy or a competitor achieves equivalent NCCN Category 1 status, moat narrows. Low probability but existential if it occurs.
2. No founder alignment: Management execution risk without a controlling owner. CEO selling shares under 10b5-1 is a yellow flag.
3. Valuation demands execution: Stock priced for continued 15-20% growth. Any guide-down or payer setback would cause significant de-rating.

### UK ISA buyability

Nasdaq common stock (not ADR). Per CLAUDE.md, individual common stocks on Nasdaq are generally buyable in T212 S&S ISA. PRIIPs/KID rule applies to packaged products, NOT individual common stocks listed directly on US exchanges. ASSESS: BUYABLE in UK T212 S&S ISA.

### Portfolio fit

Closest replacement: VinaCapital Vietnam (4%) — passive frontier fund vs precision-oncology compounder with structural guideline moat and 15%+ revenue growth. Or replaces iShares MSCI India ETF. Blind spot filled: US precision diagnostics, genomic companion diagnostics, cancer-decision-support software moat. Distinct from Bioventix (antibody royalty) and all other current holdings.

Conviction: 3.5/5. Strong moat quality and profitability inflection; reduced by absence of founder alignment and valuation demand. Best entry on pullbacks (<$28).

## PICK 4 (Watchlist / "Next Vista" asymmetric): Rapid Micro Biosystems (RPID | Nasdaq)

**US | Nasdaq | Pre-EBITDA early-stage; "Next Vista" candidate only**

Growth Direct is the first automated, image-based rapid sterility-testing system for pharmaceutical manufacturing — FDA-mandated quality control for every batch of biologics, injectables, vaccines, and cell/gene therapies. Current gold standard: 14-day manual agar-plate incubation. Growth Direct: 5 days, automated, image-based. Genuine "indispensable picks-and-shovels" for biopharma manufacturing. FDA validation creates 18-24 month lock-in once validated (pharma batch-release filing built on Growth Direct result).

FY2025 revenue: $33.6M (+20%); recurring revenue $17.8M (+15%); 190 Growth Direct placements (155 validated); gross margin ~20%; 2026 guidance $37-41M. Q1 2026 recurring revenue +30% YoY (consumables inflecting).

NOT a core pick: pre-EBITDA; 20% gross margin; dilution risk. Track: quarterly placement data, gross margin trend. Re-evaluate at 400+ placements and 35%+ gross margin.

Conviction: 2.5/5 now. ISA buyable (Nasdaq common stock; verify).
