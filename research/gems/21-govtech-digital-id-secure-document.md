# Lane 21 — Govtech / Digital-ID / Secure-Document / E-Government Infrastructure

_Research date: 2026-06-01. Informational synthesis only — not financial advice._

## Lane thesis

Governments worldwide are replacing paper credentials with tamper-proof, biometrically linked digital identities — ePassports, national eIDs, digital driver's licences, EU Digital Identity Wallets (EUDI Wallet, deadline 2026), UK Digital ID (target 2029). The wave is structural and multi-decade.

**ICAO mandates** embed biometrics in all new passports globally; billions of documents need re-issuance on 5-10-year cycles. EU EES/ETIAS border control (biometric matching system live 2025) creates new infrastructure demand. UK Gov One Login + Digital ID scheme creating multi-year systems integration spend. Developing-world biometric enrollment (Africa, LatAm, SE Asia) is years 1-5 of a 20-year rollout. AI-deepfake threat makes tamper-proof physical + digital credentialing MORE necessary, not less.

**ISA buyability note:** De La Rue (DLAR.L) delisted from LSE on 3 July 2025 — acquired by ACR Bidco. IDEX Biometrics (Oslo) has going-concern qualification Dec 2025. FPC/Precise merger in-process, tiny. NEC, Idemia, Veridos, G+D — all Tokyo-only or private. This narrows the buyable shortlist significantly.

## GEM 1 — Muehlbauer Holding AG

**Ticker + venue:** MUB.DE — Xetra (Frankfurt), ISIN DE0006627201
**Country:** Germany
**~Market cap:** ~EUR 570-660M (stock ~EUR 37-42, ~13.7M shares)
**Stage:** Mature compounder at earnings trough — classic entry window
**UK ISA buyability:** CONFIRMED. Xetra common stock, AJ Bell explicitly lists as ISA-eligible. T212 supports Xetra EU common stocks. No KID/PRIIPs issue.

### Structural wave + why under-covered

Muehlbauer is THE ONLY true one-stop-shop for the full lifecycle of passport and smart-card production globally: it designs, builds, and installs the machines that physically produce and personalise ePassports, national eID cards, driving licences, biometric voter-registration systems, and RFID/contactless credential systems — then supplies consumables and maintenance. Competitors (Idemia, Veridos, De La Rue) are mostly integrators that BUY Muehlbauer's machines.

Why under-covered: (a) Xetra small-cap with minimal sell-side coverage and very low free float — Josef Muehlbauer family regularly buys shares on-market, further reducing float. (b) Financials are lumpy — large government contracts cause revenue/profit swings that scare generalist investors. (c) Sits at the unsexy intersection of mechanical engineering and geopolitics. Most investors miss it entirely.

**Recent contract wins confirming live pipeline:** Nepal biometric passport contract (Package I, ~USD 11M for nationwide enrollment system, awarded mid-2025, beating Idemia in legal challenge); Uganda 30M biometric registrations; Mozambique; Congo biometric tax-ID cards; Fiji.

### Moat

1. **Regulatory/certification moat:** ICAO, ISO, and national government approval processes take years; Muehlbauer systems are pre-qualified for dozens of nations' identity programmes.
2. **One-stop-shop lock-in:** Governments sign 10-20 year supplier relationships covering equipment, software, consumables, and maintenance. Switching mid-contract is logistically and politically near-impossible.
3. **Know-how:** 40+ years of proprietary process knowledge in secure document production (RFID inlay embedding, chip-module integration, laser personalisation, optical security features). No realistic fast-follow — capital equipment for ePassport production requires sovereign-grade security clearances.
4. **Installed base flywheel:** After equipment is installed, Muehlbauer earns an annuity stream of consumables, spare parts, and service contracts.

### Founder / insider ownership

Founded 1981 by Josef Muehlbauer (HQ at "Josef-Muehlbauer-Platz 1", Roding). Josef Muehlbauer serves on supervisory board; Josef Markus Muehlbauer is Co-CEO/Co-Chairman Management Board. Family is controlling shareholder and REGULARLY BUYS SHARES ON-MARKET, further reducing already-low free float. Exact family stake not confirmed in searches [UV — check latest annual report, likely >50%].

### Sourced economics

FY2023: Revenue EUR 464.4M | EBIT EUR 66.3M (14.3% margin) | Net income ~EUR 38M [UV]
FY2024: Revenue EUR 522.5M (+12.5%, record) | EBIT EUR 15.1M (-77%) | Net income ~EUR 11.5M | Dividend EUR 1.50/share
Balance sheet: Net cash EUR 83.7M, ZERO DEBT throughout.

FY2024 profitability collapse is a CYCLICAL TROUGH, not structural — large contracts in execution (revenue recognition) but with front-loaded costs. Revenue was record. Zero debt. Dividend maintained. Nepal contract won mid-2025 confirms pipeline is live and moat intact. [UV: verify whether trough is cost mix vs revenue recognition vs one-off project losses in 2024 annual report]

### Valuation

Market cap ~EUR 600M [UV midpoint]. At normalised EUR 50-65M EBIT (mid-cycle between FY2022/23 peak and FY2024 trough), EV/EBIT = ~10x — deeply cheap for a near-monopoly, founder-aligned, net-cash, global-critical-infrastructure supplier. P/S = ~1.1x. Dividend yield ~4% at EUR 37.

### 10-year bull case

Base case: Revenue normalises to EUR 600-700M by 2028 (EU wallet rollout, Africa/Asia new issuances). EBIT margin returns to 12-14% = EUR 72-98M. At 15x EBIT -> EV ~EUR 1.1-1.5B -> stock ~EUR 80-110. ~2-3x from EUR 37 plus dividends.

Bull case (10yr): Revenue compounds 8% p.a. to ~EUR 1.1B by 2034. At 18x EBIT EUR 165M -> EV ~EUR 3B -> stock ~EUR 220. ~6x total return.

Downside floor: Net cash EUR 83.7M + zero debt + essential government infrastructure supplier = floor ~EUR 25-30 (below book value). Muehlbauer has survived every cycle since 1981.

Asymmetric structure: Trough earnings create temporary optical cheapness. The moat is not impaired — Nepal contract win 2025 proves pipeline is live.

### Top 3 risks

1. Earnings lumpiness / project execution risk — a single large contract going wrong devastates a year's earnings. Inherent to model, not structural.
2. Geopolitical/sanctions risk — if a major client country becomes sanctioned, that revenue is lost. Partially mitigated by geographic diversification.
3. Low float / liquidity risk — family buybacks further compress free float, making it hard to build a large position.

### Portfolio fit

Best fit: Secunet (4%) or India ETF (6%). Muehlbauer is complementary to Secunet (hardware/machines vs software/cybersecurity, not duplicative). Both are German so adds EUR-Germany concentration. If India ETF is swapped, Muehlbauer is a strong candidate for that slot. Conviction: 4/5.

## GEM 2 — Sopra Steria Group SA

**Ticker + venue:** SOP.PA — Euronext Paris (Compartment A), ISIN FR0000050809
**Country:** France (operations across EU, UK, Scandinavia)
**~Market cap:** ~EUR 2.76B
**Stage:** Large-cap quality compounder at cyclically depressed valuation (8-9x P/E)
**UK ISA buyability:** CONFIRMED. Trading212 explicitly lists SOP.FR (Euronext Paris) as available for ISA accounts. AJ Bell also lists it.

### Structural wave + why under-covered

Sopra Steria is systematically misclassified as a generic IT services company. In reality it is one of Europe's two or three deepest sovereign-government-trusted digital infrastructure partners:
- Operates the UK Home Office UKVCAS (UK Visa and Citizenship Application Service, GBP 91M+ contract, 4M+ customers processed)
- Runs NHS Shared Business Services (JV with Department of Health since 2004 — 20+ year partnership)
- Systems integrator for the EU sBMS (Shared Biometric Matching System — Europe's central fingerprint/facial recognition database for border control, declared live 2025)
- Built EES (Entry/Exit System) biometric matching system for EU border control

Valuation at decade lows: 8-9x P/E vs Capgemini at 18x, Accenture at 25x. The EU govtech wave (EUDI Wallet 2026, NHS digital transformation, EES/ETIAS, UK One Login) creates a multi-year contract pipeline that Sopra Steria is uniquely positioned to capture via pre-existing sovereign trust built over decades.

### Moat

1. **Sovereign trust and security clearances:** UK national security clearances and EU-level certifications required to operate biometric/sensitive government data systems take years to obtain.
2. **Long-duration contract lock-in:** NHS SBS 20+ year partnership; UKVCAS multi-year with extensions; EU sBMS is critical infrastructure with multi-decade requirements.
3. **Switching costs are prohibitive:** Governments cannot switch biometric-matching or visa-processing infrastructure mid-operation.
4. **Founder/family alignment:** Pasquier and Odin families (co-founders 1968) hold ~23.15% share capital and ~33.78% voting rights as of March 2026.

### Founder / insider ownership

Founded 1968 by Pierre Pasquier, Francois Odin, Léo Gantelet. Concert party (Sopra GMT + Odin family + Pasquier family + individual manager signatories + OEP SGMT BV) = 23.15% share capital, 33.78% theoretical voting rights as of March 31, 2026. Exceptional founding-family alignment for a EUR 2.76B-cap company.

### Sourced economics

FY2025: Revenue ~EUR 5.63B | Operating margin 9.5% | Operating profit EUR 534.3M | Net profit EUR 296.8M (+18.3% YoY) | Net debt EUR 246.7M (0.45x EBITDA, down 35.4% YoY) | ROCE 20.4% (above 20% medium-term target) | New EUR 40M buyback launched May 2026.

### Valuation

P/E ~9.3x (trailing), ~8.2x (forward). ROCE 20.4%. Net debt/EBITDA 0.45x. At 14x P/E (still peer discount) on EUR 296M net profit -> market cap EUR 4.1B -> ~50% upside. Buyback from founder concert provides downside support.

### 10-year bull case

Base: EU digital government wave drives 4-5% organic growth for 5 years, margin expansion to 11-12% -> revenue EUR 7B by 2030, net profit EUR 400-450M -> at 14x P/E -> EUR 5.6-6.3B market cap. Stock roughly doubles.

Bull (10yr): Sopra Steria becomes recognised as EU's sovereign digital infrastructure champion, re-rating to 18-20x P/E on EUR 600M net profit by 2034 -> EUR 10.8-12B market cap -> ~4x return.

Downside floor: At 8x P/E on a bad year, stock already nearly at floor. With 34% founder voting control and active buyback, there is a real put from founders at current prices. Sopra Steria has been profitable in every year since 1968.

### Top 3 risks

1. European IT services sector de-rating — macro slowdown, government austerity could drag stock even if fundamentals sound.
2. UK government contract dependency — NHS SBS and UKVCAS are large UK revenue contributors; in-sourcing push could reduce pipeline.
3. AI disruption to services model — generative AI could compress revenue-per-consultant if clients take more work in-house.

### Portfolio fit

Best fit: iShares MSCI India UCITS ETF (6% weight). The India ETF is the portfolio's only passive exposure with no moat, no insider alignment — structural "replace me" target from 14/20 research lanes. Sopra Steria at 9x P/E with founder alignment and structural EU govtech demand is a materially higher-quality holding. Conviction: 3.5/5.
