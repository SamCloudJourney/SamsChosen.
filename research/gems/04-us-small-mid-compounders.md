# US Small/Mid-Cap Under-Covered Quality Compounders

_For a 15-stock concentrated quality-compounder book; light on US/USD; seeking founder-aligned picks-and-shovels into structural waves._

## Executive Summary
Four names clear the bar to displace an incumbent. Comfort Systems (FIX) disqualified — at ~$35B it is no longer under-covered. The four gems:
1. **OTC Markets Group (OTCM)** — monopoly financial-infrastructure tollgate
2. **InterDigital (IDCC)** — recurring patent-royalty machine into 5G/6G/AI video
3. **Installed Building Products (IBP)** — founder-led insulation installer into AI data centers + housing
4. **Kinsale Capital (KNSL)** — founder-led technology-native E&S insurer

## Gem 1: OTC MARKETS GROUP (OTCM)
- US | OTC Pink | ~$640M | Mature compounder entering a new growth phase.

**Structural wave & under-coverage:** Operates the infrastructure beneath the entire non-exchange US securities ecosystem — 12,000+ securities, $445B+ annual dollar volume, three tiers (OTCQX, OTCQB, Pink). Waves: (1) global capital-market democratization (foreign ADRs without full SEC registration = 75% of securities, 85% of volume); (2) tokenization/digital-asset securities (federal approval to trade on OTC Link); (3) 24-hour/overnight trading (MOON ATS +52% revenue Q1 2026). Under-covered: trades OTC (most desks structurally can't own it), ~$640M below most mandates, no sell-side, CEO doesn't court Wall Street.

**Moat:** Tollbridge/network-effect monopoly — any broker quoting an OTC security must use OTC Link ATS; no competing platform at scale/standing. Corporate-services listing fees (recurring, switching costs); market-data licensing (pure software margin). 131 employees, ~$48M FCF on $125M revenue (38%+ FCF margin).

**Ownership:** Cromwell Coulson (CEO since acquiring the predecessor in 1997) ~27–34% (sources vary; 27.7% personal + 7.4% family trust per one; 34.48% direct per another). Insiders/officers/directors >34%.

**Economics (2025):** Gross revenue $125.3M (+13%); operating income $38.2M (30.5%); net income $31.1M (+14%); adj EBITDA $47.6M (+15%); FCF ~$48.4M (~38% margin); 13-yr revenue CAGR ~12%; ROIC 47–58% (5-yr avg ~58%); zero debt; Q1 2026 revenue $34.8M (+14%).

**Valuation:** ~$53–55 share; ~$640M cap; P/E ~20x (14% below 10-yr median 23x); FCF yield ~7.5%; consensus target $65 (+18%). For 58% avg ROIC, zero debt, 12% CAGR, structural monopoly — anomalously cheap.

**10-yr bull:** Preferred on-ramp for tokenized securities/digital assets; MOON ATS captures 24-hr trading share; ADR/foreign-listing volumes expand. To 28–30x on $80–90M net income → $2.4–2.7B cap = 3.5–4x + dividends. Coulson likely never sells.

**Risks:** (1) Regulatory restructuring of OTC rules / competing ATSs (existential moat risk; but adapted in 2014 Pink cleanup). (2) Growth ceiling — core has limited pricing power; new products unproven at scale (one source projects only 5% 5-yr CAGR).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — structural US financial-infrastructure alpha, founder-operator 30%+, near-zero cyclicality, idiosyncratic upside. **Conviction: 5/5** — closest thing to a hidden gem in US markets today.

## Gem 2: INTERDIGITAL (IDCC)
- US | NASDAQ | ~$6.5B | Established compounder, re-rating phase.

**Structural wave & under-coverage:** Invents/licenses foundational wireless + video-compression tech — every 3G/4G/5G device and modern video codec potentially owes a royalty. Waves: 5G (80% of global smartphones licensed; ARR +24% to $582M); AI video (Deep Render acquisition for AI-native codecs); 6G (world's-first 6G sensing architecture with Türk Telekom at MWC 2026); IoT/fintech licensing. Under-covered: misperceived as a "patent troll"; unusual model (near-zero COGS, high litigation overhead, lumpy settlements); ~26M shares, heavy institutional ownership, low retail visibility.

**Moat:** ~38,000-patent portfolio (7 new/day, doubled 2017–2025) embedded in global wireless/video standards — legally mandated infrastructure. FRAND licensing = court-backed recurring revenue. Won vs Apple, Huawei, ZTE. ARR $582M (+24%) = contracted future cash.

**Ownership:** Not founder-led (CEO Liren Chen since 2023); insiders ~1.9% (weakness), but aggressive buybacks ($169M in 2025; share count >50M→~26M over a decade; >$1B authorized).

**Economics (2025):** Revenue $834M (−4% YoY, 2024 was a record; 3-yr CAGR ~6%); ARR $582M (+24%); adj EBITDA $589M (71% margin, record); net income $407M (+13%, record); FCF $474M (~57% margin); ROIC 25–30%; net cash ~$780M; operating margin 49.7%.

**Valuation:** ~$6.5–8.4B cap; EV/EBIT ~14x; EV/EBITDA ~12x; P/FCF ~11x; FCF yield ~8.9%; 9.5% short interest (squeeze fuel). Materially cheap for a 57%-FCF-margin monopoly-adjacent IP engine.

**10-yr bull:** ARR to $1B+ by 2030 (mgmt target); AI-video licensing +$200–300M ARR by 2030; 6G cycle ~2030–2032; with ~25M shares and $1B+ net income by 2032 → $80–120/share at 15x = 2–3x.

**Risks:** (1) Court/arbitration ruling against FRAND rates (China most sensitive). (2) 6G standardization delay to 2035+ compresses the case. (Plus low insider alignment.)

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **VinaCapital Vietnam** — asset-light recurring IP royalty engine vs pure EM macro; fills US/USD gap. **Conviction: 4/5** (docked for low insider ownership + lumpy litigation).

## Gem 3: INSTALLED BUILDING PRODUCTS (IBP)
- US | NYSE | ~$5.6B | Quality consolidator, mid-cycle.

**Structural wave & under-coverage:** Installs insulation (+ complementary products) across residential, commercial, and — increasingly — data centers. Waves: US housing shortage (installs 25%+ of all new US residential insulation); tightening energy codes; AI data-center buildout ($710B+ NA infra spend 2026, specialized thermal insulation); renovation/retrofit of aging stock. Under-covered: classified as "homebuilder services," seen as cyclically peaking; data-center/commercial opportunity under-appreciated; ~$5.6B too small for big funds.

**Moat:** Hyperlocal, labor-intensive install — can't ship across state lines; needs local crews/relationships/trucks. 250+ branches, 10,000+ employees = scale in local labor, insurance, training, builder relationships. Builders value scheduling reliability. Efficient scale (3–4 regional players + an independent; a fifth destroys economics). ROIC ex-goodwill ~41%; FCF conversion ~90%+.

**Ownership:** Jeff Edwards (CEO/Chairman) owns 14.4% (3.9M shares, Dec 2025), 30+ yrs in the industry, co-founder.

**Economics (2025):** Revenue ~$2.97–3.0B (record, +~1%, weather/demand headwinds H1); adj EBITDA $518.5M (17.5%); GAAP EBITDA $496M; net income $265M (diluted EPS $9.71); op CF $371M, capex ~$34M → FCF ~$340–370M (~5.6% yield); ROIC ex-goodwill ~41%; net leverage 1.1x (target <2x); cash $321.9M; insider 13.86%.

**Valuation:** ~$5.6B cap; P/E ~22x; EV/EBIT ~17x; EV/EBITDA ~13x; P/FCF ~18x. Not cheap absolutely but reasonable for quality + tailwinds; down from $300+ highs on housing-start concerns (entry window).

**10-yr bull:** Starts recover to 1.5M+/yr; commercial/data-center demand compounds separately; disciplined M&A (50+ deals/10 yrs) adds adjacent services. To $5–6B revenue by 2034 at maintained margins, 20x earnings → $10–12B cap = ~2x + dividends.

**Risks:** (1) Prolonged housing downcycle (~70% residential). (2) Labor-cost inflation compressing margins.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Veralto** — comparable picks-and-shovels quality at lower multiple, 14% founder ownership, 41% ROIC, data-center tailwind, fills US gap. **Conviction: 4/5.**

## Gem 4: KINSALE CAPITAL (KNSL)
- US | NYSE | ~$7.0B | Quality compounder, growth normalizing.

**Structural wave & under-coverage:** Purest play on Excess & Surplus (E&S) lines — risks too unusual/novel for standard admitted carriers. E&S grew ~$75B (2022) → $100B+ (2024) on climate, cyber, construction liability, supply-chain disruption. E&S carriers price freely and customize terms — structurally superior for a disciplined underwriter. Secular, irreversible migration as the world gets riskier (AI liability, weather). Under-covered: niche/obscure; insurance under-owned by quality-growth investors; $7B in small/large no-man's-land.

**Moat:** Technology-driven underwriting discipline — proprietary in-house IT platform (faster broker quotes, ~20.8% expense ratio vs industry ~32%); single business segment (refuses to diversify into easier admitted lines); combined ratio 71.7% Q4 2025 (industry ~95–100%); AI in underwriting/claims; broker loyalty from fast reliable quotes.

**Ownership:** Michael Kehoe (founder/CEO, founded 2009) ~9.15% (2.13M shares, ~$950M); deeply aligned.

**Economics (2025):** GWP ~$2.0B; net income (9M) $365M (FY implied ~$480–500M); operating ROE 26%; combined ratio FY avg ~76% (Q4 71.7%); book value/share $84.66 (+33%); op CF ~$1.0B; ROIC 21.4%; float $3.1B (rising-rate beneficiary); insider 5.39% (Kehoe ~9.15%).

**Valuation:** ~$7.0B cap; P/E ~13.4x; EV/EBIT ~10.5x; P/FCF ~6.9x; FCF yield ~14.4%. Strikingly cheap for a 76% combined ratio, 26% ROE, founder-led name. Pulled back 40%+ from $512 high to ~$305 on E&S-growth deceleration (30%+→low-double-digits) — real, but the business remains exceptional.

**10-yr bull:** E&S market $100B→$200B+ by 2035; Kinsale holds ~2% share while expanding into cyber/AI/climate; 26% ROE compounds book 20%+/yr → BVPS $600–700 by 2034; at 2x book → $14–15B = ~2x + dividends.

**Risks:** (1) E&S deceleration as admitted carriers reclaim share in a soft cycle; cat losses / AI-liability tort cycle could spike combined ratio to 95%+. (2) Re-rating risk — peaked at 40–50x earnings; multiple may compress before growth re-accelerates.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Secunet** — adds US/USD, different wave (E&S insurance), 9% founder stake, FCF yield 14% vs Secunet's lower; near recession-resistant. **Conviction: 4/5** (edges past ideal cap; growth deceleration to monitor; insurance is binary-risk).
