## GEM 1: KASPI.KZ (KSPI)
- Kazakhstan | Nasdaq GS | ~$13.7B | Late-growth, cash-generating.

**Wave & under-coverage:** ~19M population (small enough for near-total dominance — 75%+ adults are monthly active users), now exporting the playbook to Turkey's 85M. Permanent "Kazakhstan discount" + a 2024 Culper short report (Russia-sanctions allegations → 19% one-day drop + securities class action) + Turkey integration difficulty keep most institutions away despite exceptional metrics — precisely why the opportunity exists.

**Moat:** Most complete digital-financial infrastructure of any company its size — single super-app: Kaspi Pay (450,000+ merchants), Kaspi Market (primary e-commerce + BNPL), Kaspi Bank, plus Travel/Grocery/Government Services. Textbook flywheel (more consumers → more merchants → more GMV → more lending → more data → lower NPLs → reinvest). Extreme switching costs (how Kazakhs pay rent, shop, transfer, access government). Parallels Topicus/Constellation switching-cost model.

**Ownership:** Co-founders Mikhail Lomtadze (CEO) + Vyacheslav Kim (Chairman) combined ~44% (de facto control); Tencent ~3.2% ($518M, 2025) strategic anchor; Lomtadze bought after the Culper report; top 25 holders ~84%.

**Economics (FY2025):** Consolidated revenue ~$8B+ (KZT 4.05T); core (ex-Turkey) revenue +19%, underlying net income +18%; consolidated net income ~$2.1B; op margin ~75% (KZ segment); ROIC ~87% (model est); ROE ~67%; dividend yield ~9% ($6.84/ADS at ~$86); Turkey net loss −$185M; fwd P/E ~6–7x; EV/EBITDA ~4.5x; KZ base rate 18% (headwind); class action pending. 2026 guidance ~5% consolidated EBITDA growth (Turkey drag; KZ volumes ~20%).

**Valuation:** ~$86/ADS (~7x trailing) — among the cheapest high-quality fintech compounders globally (Indian IT 20–30x, Brazilian fintechs 15–25x). 7x reflects country risk + Turkey losses + class action. Re-rate to 12–14x = 70–100% upside; 9% dividend pays you to wait.

**10-yr bull:** Turkey profitable by 2027 (Rabobank Türkiye lending + HepsiPay scaling); combined KZ+TR ~105M people; FY2030 earnings $4–5B (15–18% CAGR); even 10x → $40–50B vs ~$14B = 3–4x + 9% dividends. The Constellation "one country → platform" model going to a second country.

**Risks (honest):** (1) **Sanctions/regulatory (HIGH, unresolved)** — class action pending; Kazakhstan ~40% of trade via Russia; OFAC/DOJ designation would be existential (management denies; KZ regulator cleared). (2) Turkey execution (Hepsiburada cost $1.127B, lost ~$185M; lira ~44% inflation). (3) Kazakhstan saturation (75%+ penetration; future = ARPU + fintech volume) + new bank taxes/reserve requirements compress 2026 bottom-line.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — sharper, contrarian EM exposure with a real moat + aligned founders + dirt-cheap valuation; fills the frontier-fintech-infrastructure slot. Size for the tail risk (~4–5%, not 8%). **Conviction: 4/5.**

## GEM 2: FPT CORPORATION (FPT.VN)
- Vietnam | HOSE | ~$4.8B | Mid-cap growth compounder.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Vietnam's largest IT-services/tech group — the Infosys/TCS of Vietnam, at ~$4.8B vs TCS's $160B+. Threefold wave: China+1 nearshoring; global digital transformation (lower-cost Vietnam engineers competitive with India); Vietnam's FTSE EM upgrade Sept-2026 ($5–10B passive inflows, FPT eligible). Under-covered: HOSE-listed (Western custody friction), minimal English coverage, frontier-until-Sept-2026; Aberdeen Asia Focus one of few Western holders.

**Moat:** Talent supply chain (FPT University ~35,000 students/yr feed FPT Software — unique education-to-workforce vertical integration); deep enterprise relationships ($225M largest-ever US contract 2024; $256M AI deal 2025; 26 contracts >$10M in 2025); automotive/SDV specialization (FPT Automotive Texas; targeting Toyota/BMW; $1B auto-revenue target by 2030); AI services (NVIDIA AI-factory JV; 25% net-profit uplift 2025–26); telecom + education stabilizers.

**Ownership:** Founder/chairman Truong Gia Binh (building since 1988; high-single-digit % estimated, unverified); inside shareholders ~17.8%; Aberdeen ~3.44%.

**Economics:** Revenue FY2025 ~$2.76B (+11.6%); 9M2025 PBT VND 9.54T (+17.6%); FY2024 PBT $435M (+20.3%); global IT services signed revenue 2025 $1.5B+; net margin ~14.6%; op margin ~15.7%; gross margin ~35.8%; ROIC 33.4%; ROE 28.5%; FCF yield ~5.5%; net cash ~$420M. P/E ~13.3x trailing (from 33x in 2024; stock −30% YTD) / fwd ~11.2x; EV/EBITDA ~7.95x; P/BV ~3.04x.

**Valuation:** 11–13x for 18–21% earnings growth, 33% ROIC, net cash, $1.5B+ signed pipeline, FTSE tailwind — materially mispriced vs Indian IT (Infosys/Wipro 20–25x on slower growth).

**10-yr bull:** $5B revenue by 2030 (IT services $2–3B, auto $1B, telecom $750M, education $500M+); 18–20% earnings CAGR for 7 yrs from ~$390M → ~$1.2–1.4B; 20x → $24–28B = ~5–6x. FTSE upgrade Sept-2026 = near-term catalyst.

**Risks (honest):** (1) US tariff risk (MEDIUM — 46% proposed on goods, but software services exempt; indirect if US IT spending slows). (2) Vietnam geopolitical/regulatory (49% foreign-ownership ceiling limits Western participation). (3) Talent competition/wage inflation (mitigated by the university hedge; FPT turnover 11.9% vs India 20.3%).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **VinaCapital Vietnam (VOF)** — upgrades Vietnam exposure from a country basket to the best-in-class franchise (NB: FPT is likely already a large holding inside VOF — check for double-counting). Or replace **India ETF** (India's IT story 20 years ago, a fraction of the valuation). **Conviction: 4/5.**

## GEM 3: GRUPO AEROPORTUARIO DEL PACÍFICO (PAC/GAPB)
- Mexico | NYSE/BMV | ~$12B | Mature cash compounder, CAPEX cycle.

12 Mexican airports (Guadalajara, Tijuana, Los Cabos, Puerto Vallarta) + 2 Jamaica. **Wave:** Mexico nearshoring — industrial freight/business travel through north/west Mexico airports (esp. Tijuana) surged ("nearshoring and fleet expansions" cited as 2025 drivers). Trades at a discount to global airports (European 20–30x EV/EBITDA vs Mexico ~8–10x). **Moat:** 50-yr exclusive concession for 12 airports (no competing airport buildable in catchment areas); government raised concession fee 5%→9% (painful but within the framework); non-aero revenue target 30% by 2026; 64–67% EBITDA margins; 58% expansion plan 2025–30. Cintra (government-linked) ~46.5% (aligns government interest with concession stability but limits flexibility). **Economics:** FY2025 revenue MXN 41.4B (+23.2%); H1 EBITDA MXN 11.1B (67.1%, compressing to 64%); P/E ~20–23x; div yield ~4.6%; Q1 2026 EPS $3.78 (+23.5% vs forecast); cash MXN 11.7B; Debt/EBITDA ~1x; CAPEX MXN 52B 2025–30 (suppresses FCF to 2029–30). **Risks:** (1) Concession-fee/regulatory risk (HIGH precedent — unilateral 2023 tariff-base change + fee hike; #1 material risk). (2) MXN/political (Sheinbaum, US-Mexico trade). (3) FCF compression during capex. **Conviction: 3/5** — the most marginal pick (real-asset, government-dependent moat, doesn't fit the tech/software DNA as cleanly as Kaspi/FPT). Replace **India ETF** if LatAm infrastructure is wanted.
