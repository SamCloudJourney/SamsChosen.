## GEM 1: CAMS (CAMS.NS)
- India | NSE | ~₹19,600Cr (~$2.35B) | Cash-generative growth compounder.

**Wave & under-coverage:** India MF AUM ~₹81 lakh crore (~$970B), on a path to ₹150 lakh crore by 2030 (~1.85x). SIP AUM projected to triple ₹13.4L→41–44L Cr by FY30. CAMS is the tollbooth — every MF transaction/SIP/statement flows through CAMS or KFin. 68% market share of MF RTA (₹55 lakh crore serviced). Under-covered: India-listed, B2B (no consumer brand), "back-office" concept; $2.35B with no Western sell-side.

**Moat:** Regulated duopoly (only two depositories/RTAs licensed; brokers/AMCs/insurers legally must interface); embedded middleware (69+ AMC clients' investor databases, KYC, transaction histories, DP linkages on CAMS); regulatory hooks (account aggregator, KYC, FATCA/CRS); network effects (largest RTA, unmatched folio data); new AMCs choose CAMS first (reinforcing). NSDL dominates by value; CAMS dominates by retail volume (where India's growth is).

**Ownership — flag:** Promoter 0% (NSE Investments divested 37.4% at the 2020 IPO per the "no exchange >24%" rule); FII 44.4%, DII 22%, public 33.5%. No founder/promoter discipline (structural negative).

**Economics (FY22–25):** Revenue ₹551→1,082Cr (~25% 3-yr CAGR); PAT ₹312→526Cr (~19%); ROCE ~32%; op margin ~58%; FCF ₹387Cr; debt-free, ~50% payout. Q3/Q4 FY26 profit moderated ~20% QoQ (margin compression from tech/product spend; revenue still growing). P/E ~57x (fwd ~40x if PAT recovers to ₹600–650Cr FY27); FCF yield ~1.5%.

**10-yr bull:** Demat/MF accounts to 400–500M by 2035; recurring AMC + transaction fees; larger share of depository-tech (e-voting, insurance repository, KYC); PAT 18–22% CAGR; 15–20% stock CAGR.

**Risks:** (1) SEBI fee intervention (margins already 58%). (2) Demat-addition slowdown (six-year low in 2025). (3) No promoter/founder.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — purest play on India's financialization; compounds faster than the index in a bull market, sticky in a flat one; wait for a 20–25% drawdown to size up. **Conviction: 3.5/5** (44x P/E, near-term margin pressure, no founder).

## GEM 2: QUÁLITAS CONTROLADORA (Q*.MX / QUCOF)
- Mexico | BMV / OTC QUCOF | ~MXN 67–69B (~$3.9–4.1B) | Owner-operated compounder, long runway.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Mexican auto-insurance penetration ~3% of GDP, only 30% of the vehicle fleet insured (~half of Brazil/Colombia). Auto written premiums +9.9% (2025), industry projecting 11.5% CAGR. Quálitas is the premier insurer; nearshoring-driven fleet growth. Under-covered: Mexican small-mid cap, BMV-only, unglamorous, thin OTC liquidity, Mexican equities out of favor — yet compounded at ~20%+ ROE for a decade.

**Moat:** 230 franchise offices (vs ~40 for the nearest competitor) — a 30-year physical moat embedding claims service/recovery/sales; recovered 43.5% of stolen vehicles in 2025 (operational excellence improving loss ratio); holds 80.2% of the entire Mexican auto-insurance sector's underwriting profits (disciplined pricing/vertical integration while peers earn minimal/negative underwriting profit); brand trust in a low-trust market; bancassurance channel.

**Ownership:** ~48% insider ownership; Risoul family control (Bernardo Risoul Salas CEO from Jan 2026, ex-Chairman) — the highest-quality ownership in this lane. ~5% dividend yield (willing to return capital, not empire-build).

**Economics:** FY2025 written premiums MXN 75.8B; H1 EBITDA margin 67.1%; combined ratio ~94.1% (90.6% ex-VAT) — world-class; ROE >20% (24.1% history); solvency 362%; net cash MXN 11.7B; Debt/EBITDA ~1x; CAPEX-light. P/E ~15x (10-yr avg 24x — near relative lows); P/B 3.83x for a 24% ROE bank-like; FCF yield (cash gen) high. Q1 2026 net income −27% YoY (VAT regulatory change + higher loss frequency — "transition year"; 12-mo ROE to 16.8% from 24%+ — monitor).

**10-yr bull:** Nearshoring + insured-rate 30%→50% (still below peers) doubles the addressable market; ~33%+ share; international optionality (US, Peru, Costa Rica, Colombia, El Salvador). At 20–22% ROE + 60–70% payout → ~11–13% total return in MXN (7–9% USD after FX drag) + 5% dividend; re-rate to 18–20x (peers) on normalized EPS → ~2x in MXN.

**Risks:** (1) MXN depreciation (record lows ~17,800–17,900/USD). (2) Loss-ratio creep (theft/weather; Q1-2026 tick-up). (3) VAT/regulatory surprises in Mexico.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** (with CAMS too = swap passive India + add a new geography) or **Eckert & Ziegler** (judgment call — E&Z has its own moat). Fills the LatAm + owner-operated-insurer blind spot. **Conviction: 3.5/5** (Q1 VAT + FX warrant sizing discipline).
