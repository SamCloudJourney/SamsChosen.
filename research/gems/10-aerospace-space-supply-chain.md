## GEM 1: FILTRONIC (FTC.L)
- UK | AIM | ~£858M (~$1.1B) | Small-cap, earnings ramping.

**Wave & under-coverage:** The LEO satellite revolution (Starlink v3+, Kuiper, OneWeb) needs tens of thousands of high-frequency mmWave transceivers, shifting from Ka-band (26 GHz) to E-band (71–86 GHz)/V-band. Filtronic's E-band GaN solid-state power amplifiers deliver >2x the output power of GaAs solutions at these frequencies. Invisible: AIM-listed; unimpressive 15-yr revenue history (~£15–25m pre-2024); no bulge-bracket coverage. £56m FY2025 revenue understates the £47.3m ($62.5m) SpaceX order (first deliveries FY2027).

**Moat:** Vertically integrated GaN design-to-manufacture (rare at this scale for mmWave space hardware); decade of E-band heritage; SpaceX de-risked it via a qualification ladder (£2m→£3.4m→£4.8m→£7.8m→£15.8m→£47.3m over 3 yrs); SpaceX holds warrants for up to ~15% (5% vested) — a co-dependency, not a customer; no obvious substitute (Qorvo/MACOM/Wolfspeed make chips, not space-qualified integrated subsystems); requalification of in-orbit units is prohibitive.

**Ownership:** Insiders ~43% (early 2025, likely incl founding management on restricted stock — precise breakdown unverified); SpaceX 5% (vested warrants) with board-observer-level interest.

**Economics:** FY2025 (May YE) revenue £56.3m (+121%); adj EBITDA £17.0m (30.2% margin); op profit £13.4m; cash £14.5m (May 2025) / £10.5m + £10m RCF (Nov 2025); H1 FY2026 revenue £25.3m (flat), op profit £2.6m (vs £6.8m — capacity investment phase), EBITDA £5.1m; ~90% of FY2026 covered by contract; net cash ex-leases £8.2m; P/E ~93x (FY2025, stock 390p); EV ~£851m. ROIC high but not reported (asset-light). FCF modest; op cash £3.4m H1 FY2026.

**Valuation:** P/E 93x elevated; relevant metric is fwd EV/revenue on the FY2027 ramp (£100m+ from SpaceX alone). FY2026 is a "valley" between legacy revenue and the ramp.

**10-yr bull:** $3bn LEO E-band hardware TAM (conservative if Kuiper/others also qualify); 5–10% share = $150–300m vs ~£56m; V-band, military satcom, 6G backhaul extend the runway; SpaceX warrants align both sides.

**Risks:** (1) Single-customer concentration (Americas 89.6% of H1 FY2026; SpaceX dominates; in-sourcing/MACOM risk — warrants mitigate not eliminate). (2) FY2026 earnings valley / delivery execution. (3) Already re-rated (3x in 12 months; 15.3x P/S prices a successful FY2027 ramp).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — pure space-economy exposure (absent from the book); or complement E&Z. **Conviction: 4/5.**

## GEM 2: ASTRONICS (ATRO)
- US | NASDAQ | ~$3.1B | Multi-year recovery.

**Wave & under-coverage:** (1) 17,000+ aircraft order backlog globally, deliveries at a 7-yr high; (2) in-seat power spreading from premium to economy. Chronically under-followed (sits between "avionics" and "cabin interiors"; too small at $3.1B; 2022–24 supply-chain trough reputation).

**Moat:** EmPower in-seat power ~90% market share across 280+ airlines, 2M+ seats — seated under an STC, so replacement requires re-certification of every seat/aircraft type (certification moat + razor-blade aftermarket over 20–25 yr life); platform-qualified lighting (Airbus/Boeing/Embraer/Lockheed/Textron — 3–5 yr qualification locks supplier for program life); FAA ODA (via Envoy Aerospace 2025 acquisition — can self-certify STCs; very few small/mid-cap suppliers hold one); military avionics (ground-test, radio test, power distribution).

**Ownership:** Dual-class (Class B 10x votes); insider ~6.21% economic; Robert Keane (Cimpress CEO) ~20% of Class B, active chairman; CEO Peter Gundermann since 2014.

**Economics:** Revenue FY2025 ~$861.5M (vs ~$812M); Q1 2026 $230.6M (+12%); Q4 2025 op margin 14.8%; adj EBITDA Q1 2026 $37.9M (16.4%); FY2025 op CF $74.8M, capex $31.7M → FCF ~$43M; net debt ~$324M (Q3 2025); backlog $734.3M (record); FY2026 guidance $970M–$1B (raised); P/E ~70.6x; EV/EBIT 34.3x; EV/EBITDA 28.2x; P/FCF ~130x (depressed during ramp); ROIC ~19.2%; ROE ~21.2%.

**10-yr bull:** $1.5–2B revenue by 2032–34 (narrowbody in-seat-power retrofit, aftermarket leverage on 5,000+ equipped aircraft, ODA-enabled STC wins, military). At 18–20% EBITDA margins → FCF $150–200M on $2B → significant re-rating from 130x P/FCF.

**Risks:** (1) Boeing dependency (~11%; MAX/777X delays). (2) Leverage ($335M net debt + $225M 0%-convertible Sep 2025 = dilution). (3) Premium prices a smooth ramp.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Secunet** (smaller cap, limited runway) or **Sartorius** — US aerospace-aftermarket blind spot; TransDigm-like certification economics at a fraction of TransDigm's multiple. **Conviction: 4/5.**

## GEM 3: FREQUENCY ELECTRONICS (FEIM)
- US | NASDAQ | ~$747M | Micro/small-cap space heritage.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Precision timing/frequency generation — the backbone of every satellite, GPS, and military comms system. 1,000+ satellites since Voyager (1977). LEO constellations to 50,000+ by 2030 + DoD modernization (GPS IIIF, JADC2, quantum-referenced clocks). Only 1–2 analysts; 226 employees, $68m TTM revenue.

**Moat:** 60+ yr flight heritage (5,000+ assemblies on 1,000+ satellites — primes require flight heritage); ITAR + US production (excludes foreign Safran-Orelia/Oscilloquartz from DoD); sole-source on specific GPS/military programs (Lockheed GPS IIIF Digital Rubidium $5.9m 2024; $45m combined satellite contracts Mar 2026); emerging quantum sensing.

**Ownership:** Founded 1961 by Martin Bloch; largest holder Edenbrook Capital ~20%; top-5 ~54%; reasonable but not "family-run."

**Economics (FY ends Apr):** Revenue FY2025 $69.8M (+26.3%); Q3 FY2026 $16.9M (down YoY); funded backlog $83M (record, Jan 2026); gross margin ~44–45%; net income FY2025 $23.8M (+325%, outlier on mix); TTM net income $7.2M (−69%, mix shift); net cash ~$32M, debt-free; mgmt target $150M by FY2029; P/E ~104x TTM / ~64x fwd.

**Flags:** FY2025 net income was a mix-driven outlier; at $747M on $68m revenue and $7m current earnings, priced for the $150M target on schedule — minimal margin of safety.

**Risks:** (1) Order air-pockets (Q3 fell YoY despite record backlog). (2) Valuation (104x). (3) Competition from Microchip/Safran-Orelia/Hexagon in cost-sensitive commercial LEO.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Bioventix** (low priority). **Conviction: 3/5** — irreplaceable heritage/ITAR moat but no room for order volatility at the price.
