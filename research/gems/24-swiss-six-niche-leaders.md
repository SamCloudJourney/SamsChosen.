### Wave + Why Under-Covered / Early
Ems-Chemie is the world's premier supplier of high-performance specialty polymers — the invisible ingredients inside automotive connectors, EV battery housings, 5G antenna housings, medical devices, electronics, and aerospace components. The structural wave is the electrification and lightweighting super-cycle: every EV requires 3-4x more specialty polymer content than an ICE vehicle (heat-resistant, flame-retardant connectors; battery-module housings; e-motor insulation). Simultaneously, miniaturisation of electronics and medical devices drives demand for precisely engineered polymer compounds. Despite a CHF 17B market cap it remains under-analysed relative to the Roche/Novartis giants — only ~12 sell-side analysts cover it, and it trades on SIX without a US-listing, making it invisible to most US-centric screens.

Why early-ish: Revenue was temporarily down ~6% in 2025 due to the strong CHF and weak automotive cycle — masking underlying volume resilience. The market is pricing a near-term trough, not the structural EV/electrification ramp that makes Ems the "picks-and-shovels into electrification."

### Moat
- Proprietary formulations: decades of polymer chemistry R&D create thousands of patented/trade-secret compounds; not replicable without years of qualification testing.
- Customer qualification lock-in: automotive OEM qualification cycles are 3-5 years; once a Grilamid or Grivory compound is designed in, displacement risk is very low.
- Premium pricing power: Ems sells on performance, not price — its EBIT margin (29% in 2025) exceeds every major chemical peer by 10-20pp.
- Scale in specialty niches: Ems is global #1 or #2 in most compound families — a scale moat in markets too small for BASF/Solvay to compete at the same margin.

### Founder / Family Ownership

### Wave + Why Under-Covered / Early
Huber+Suhner is a 160-year-old Swiss precision connectivity manufacturer that quietly owns POLATIS, the world's leading all-optical circuit switch used in hyperscale AI data centers. For most of its history it was "boring cables and connectors for railways, defense, and telcos" — the POLATIS business was invisible to the market until mid-2025.

POLATIS: All-optical switches allow hyperscale AI training clusters to patch fiber connections without converting to electrical signals — lower latency, lower power, higher density. As AI training scales to hundreds of thousands of GPUs, optical circuit switching is a critical and scarce capability. HUBN is the only vendor with high-port-count (up to 384x384) all-optical switches at commercial scale.

Simultaneously, European defense rearming is a structural tailwind for Radio Frequency and Low Frequency segments (coaxial cables, connectors, EMC solutions for military systems).

The stock went CHF 58 -> CHF 195 in ~6 months during 2025 as these catalysts were priced. Key question: is POLATIS a 10-year secular winner or a 1-year AI-capex trade?
