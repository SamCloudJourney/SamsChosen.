# Lane 21 — GLP-1 / Obesity Drug Supply Chain (Ex-API)
### Auto-injectors · Fill-finish · Specialty excipients · Elastomers · Contract device manufacturing

_Research wave: 2026-06-01. Informational synthesis only — not financial advice. All figures sourced; uncertainties flagged._

---

## Mandate framing

Bachem (current holding, 9%) already covers the GLP-1 peptide API / solid-phase peptide synthesis angle. This lane hunts the **downstream picks-and-shovels** that are indispensable regardless of which specific GLP-1 molecule wins: the injection devices, primary packaging, elastomeric closures, and CDMO services that every GLP-1 drug must pass through before it reaches a patient's abdomen.

The structural logic is tight:
- Global GLP-1 sales estimated at $53B in 2025 → $157-200B by 2030-31 (IQVIA, Grand View Research). Every vial, pen, auto-injector, plunger, and prefilled syringe is a toll on this wave.
- "100x dosing inefficiency" of oral GLP-1 formulations vs injectable: regulators certify specific combination products (drug + device); changing the device combination triggers FDA prior-approval re-submission. That creates a multi-year lock-in for approved suppliers.
- Fill-finish capacity ran near-full across the industry in 2024-25; structural undersupply of injectable capacity is the defining constraint in pharma manufacturing through 2028.

**Why under-covered / early:** Device and packaging companies are not classified as biopharma — they sit in "industrials," "specialty materials," or "medical equipment" — so GLP-1 thematic investors miss them. Many are Swiss or Italian-listed, further limiting analyst coverage relative to US-listed biotech.

## GEM 1: Ypsomed Holding AG

**Name:** Ypsomed Holding AG
**Ticker + venue:** YPSN · SIX Swiss Exchange
**Country:** Switzerland (Burgdorf)
**~Market cap:** CHF 4.5B (~$5B USD) as of late May 2026 (stock ~CHF 340)
**Stage:** Mid-cap profitable growth; transitioning to pure-play post-diabetes-division sale

### The wave + why under-covered / early

Ypsomed is the world's leading independent manufacturer of subcutaneous self-injection systems — the auto-injectors, pen injectors, and infusion devices that physically deliver GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Zepbound into patients. They are a strategic component supplier to Novo Nordisk, Eli Lilly, AstraZeneca and >100 pharma/biotech customers.

Why it's still under-covered: (1) Swiss-listed, SIX exchange — excluded from US thematic GLP-1 ETFs; (2) Until May 2025 the company was mixed with its legacy Diabetes Care retail business (insulin pens sold to patients), masking the margin profile of the high-margin Device Systems segment; (3) Post-divestiture it is now a cleaner, simpler, higher-margin compounder but the re-rating is incomplete.

**Key structural insight:** Ypsomed is mission-critical AND switching-cost-protected. When Novo Nordisk or Lilly file an NDA for a new GLP-1 drug using a specific Ypsomed autoinjector platform, the device becomes part of the approved drug product. Changing supplier requires an FDA Prior Approval Supplement — a 4-10 month process — and full comparability studies. Once a Ypsomed device is in a filing, it is locked in for the life of the drug.

### Moat

- **Regulatory lock-in:** Combination-product FDA filings embed Ypsomed's specific injection platforms (YpsoMate 1.0ml, 2.25ml autoinjector; UnoPen) into drug approvals. Switching = re-filing.
- **Platform standardization:** Ypsomed's YpsoMate platforms are used across dozens of customers with different drugs — economies of scale in tooling, validation, and FDA familiarity that a competitor replicating one platform cannot match.
- **Capacity advantage:** New auto-injector production lines require ~EUR 100M+ capital, 18-36 months lead time, and FDA/GMP certification. Ypsomed has pre-committed capacity expansions in Switzerland, and new plants opening in China (June 2025) and USA (2027). This capacity is being contracted before it is built.
- **Local-for-local strategy:** Post-tariff world: US drugs need US-made devices; China drugs need China-made devices. Ypsomed is building exactly this geographic redundancy, giving customers supply-chain sovereignty.
- **Qualitative moat score:** 4.5/5 — lock-in via regulatory filings is the highest-quality moat archetype in pharma services.

## GEM 4: Datwyler Holding AG

### The wave + why under-covered / early

Datwyler makes **elastomeric closures, plungers, and sealing components** for injectable drug vials and prefilled syringes — the rubber stoppers, plungers, and film-coated seals that physically close GLP-1 drug containers. These components must be validated alongside the drug product; once validated in an NDA/BLA, they cannot be changed without regulatory re-approval.

Datwyler started serial production supply of elastomeric components for GLP-1 therapies (plungers for prefilled syringes) in 2024-25 and is ramping volumes as GLP-1 prescriptions scale. Healthcare is 42% of revenue and growing.

The stock has declined despite GLP-1 ramp because automotive headwinds masked healthcare strength.

- **Dual fluoropolymer coating:** Datwyler's FirstLine production standard with both film and spray fluoropolymer coating is a unique capability vs West Pharmaceutical and Nipro.
