## GEM 1: RATIONAL AG (RAA.DE)

**Name:** RATIONAL Aktiengesellschaft
**Ticker + venue:** RAA.DE (Xetra, MDAX) | ISIN: DE0007010803
**Country:** Germany (Landsberg am Lech, Bavaria)
**Market cap:** ~7.8B EUR (share ~EUR 688, ~11.35M shares, June 2026)
**Stage:** Mature global compounder, pre-saturated in EM

### Wave + Why Under-Covered

**Structural wave:** Global foodservice labor crisis meets smart-kitchen automation. Company estimate: 4.8 million potential combi-steamer customers worldwide; ~75% still use traditional appliances. US penetration already tripled since 2015; Asia-Pacific is fastest-growing (China, India, SE Asia). As per-capita income crosses $8,000 in SE Asia, commercial kitchens systematically upgrade to professional equipment - a 10-20yr secular wave.

**Why under-covered:** Classified as "industrial machinery" (boring). No US sell-side coverage. MDAX, not DAX. The Meister founding family (~63%) suppresses investor relations hype deliberately. Classified "boring" despite 45%+ ROIC and 26% EBIT margins consistently.

### Moat

1. **600+ patents** on combination steam-convection cooking technology
2. **Network effect via service ecosystem:** 5,000+ trained chefs, ChefLine, ConnectedCooking app - not available from any competitor
3. **~50% global combi-steamer market share** (company figure) vs. Electrolux Professional at low single digits, private Unox at ~280M EUR revenue
4. **Switching costs via workflow integration:** Large kitchens build HACCP documentation, staff training, and menu standardization around Rational programming
5. **Limited product range:** Only iCombi Pro + iVario Pro. Focused R&D keeps capex light.

### Founder / Family

- Meister family total **~63%:** Estate of founder Siegfried Meister (died 2017) ~32%; daughters Gabriella Meister + Franziska Wurbser ~31.8% combined
- Supervisory Board chaired by family
- Dividend: 18+ consecutive years of increases; 2026 payout EUR 16 regular + EUR 4 special = EUR 20/share total (announced March 2026). 68% payout ratio.

### Sourced Economics

**FY 2025 (confirmed):**
- Revenue: EUR 1,260M (+6.0% reported, +8% currency-adjusted)
- EBIT: EUR 333M (+6%) - EBIT margin: **26.4%** (FY24: 26.3%)
- Net income: ~EUR 254M
- Equity ratio: **80%** (total assets EUR 1,183M; equity EUR 941M; near-zero financial debt)
- FCF H1 2025: EUR 70.3M (-35% YoY on working capital/tax; normalized H2)
- ROIC: **~45-47%** (GuruFocus TTM; multiple sources corroborate)
- ROCE: **~34%** vs. industry ~8.7% [UNVERIFIED - secondary source]
- Unit sales: ~85,000 devices (+9%)

**FY 2026 Guidance:**
- Revenue growth: mid- to high single-digit
- EBIT margin: 25-26%

**Valuation (June 2026):**
- P/E (TTM): ~28-30x
- Share price ~EUR 688 - down ~30% from 2024 peak ~EUR 990
- Analyst consensus: Buy; average target ~EUR 950 (+38%)
- Historical context: Traded 35-50x P/E in 2017-2022 growth years; current 28-30x is cheapest since 2018

### 10-Year Bull Case + Asymmetric Math

**Base case (7% CAGR):**
- Revenue EUR 2.5B by 2036, EBIT EUR 625M
- At 25x EBIT: ~EUR 15.6B = **2.0x from today**

**Bull case (EM acceleration, 10% CAGR):**
- Revenue EUR 3.3B, EBIT EUR 924M (28% margin)
- At 30x EBIT: ~EUR 27.7B = **3.5x from today** (~13% CAGR)
- Plus 2.9% dividend yield annually

**Downside floor:** 80% equity ratio + net cash + 63% family (won't sell at distress) + 50% market share = very slow structural decline. Floor P/E ~18-20x = ~EUR 450-500, ~30% downside.
**Asymmetry: ~12:1** (3.5x upside / 0.3x downside)

### Top 3 Risks

1. **Unox (private Italian) competitive creep:** Revenue grew from EUR 125M to EUR 280M by 2022 at ~24% margin. If Unox (or Chinese OEM) reaches 15-20% market share, Rational's pricing power compresses. 5-10yr timeline.
2. **EM execution delay:** Asian penetration is long-dated; could underdeliver vs. 10yr bull case.
3. **Tariff/currency:** EUR 13M US tariff hit in 2025; USD/EUR drag on international revenues.

### UK ISA Buyability

**BUYABLE.** RAA.DE is an ordinary German common share on Xetra - same exchange as ATOSS, Secunet, E&Z (already held). No ADR/KID/PRIIP complications. Standard ISA-eligible equity.

### Portfolio Fit

**Replace iShares MSCI India UCITS ETF (6%):** Rational's EM penetration story is more efficiently captured via the world's dominant kitchen-automation brand than via a passive ETF. Adds: zero-debt ballast, real earnings, 45% ROIC, family alignment - everything the ETF lacks.

**Secondary:** Replace VinaCapital Vietnam (4%) - smaller swap but clean upgrade.

**Conviction: 4.5/5** - the most straightforward "quality compounder hiding in MDAX" in the DACH universe. Not 5/5 because: EUR 7.8B = no moonshot multiple; Unox competition creeping.

## GEM 2: NEMETSCHEK SE (NEM.DE)

**Name:** Nemetschek SE (Nemetschek Group)
**Ticker + venue:** NEM.DE (Xetra, MDAX) | ISIN: DE0006452907
**Country:** Germany (Munich, Bavaria)
**Market cap:** ~8.0B EUR (share ~EUR 69, ~115.5M shares, June 2026)
**Stage:** High-growth SaaS transition - ARR crossing EUR 1.2B, 95% recurring

### Wave + Why Under-Covered

**Structural wave:** BIM (Building Information Modelling) is the digital backbone of the global construction industry. EU Construction Products Regulation and national mandates increasingly require BIM for public procurement (UK, Germany, France, Netherlands already mandate for government contracts). Only ~30-40% of commercial projects use full BIM today - aspirational 90%+ by 2035.

**Why under-covered/re-rated as early:** Stock has fallen ~55% from its 2024 peak (52-week high ~EUR 138.50 to current ~EUR 69). Market is pricing AI disruption risk (UBS downgrade) + construction slowdown + SaaS multiple compression. But the SaaS transition is *accelerating*: Q1 2026 revenue +17% reported, +22.6% FX-adjusted. ARR "almost EUR 1.2B" in Q1 2026, +21% FX-adj. EBITDA margin inflecting toward 32-33%.

**The AI risk is overpriced:** CEO Yves Padrines adopted "AI-first" strategy; Nemetschek is embedding AI across its portfolio (Bluebeam Max, ALLPLAN AI). AEC software is technically deep - structural calculations, regulatory code compliance, multi-discipline coordination cannot be commoditized by LLMs on a 2-3yr horizon. More likely AI is additive (agents run on Nemetschek workflows) than disruptive.

**Hidden angle:** Despite being Top-3 global AEC software vendor (alongside Autodesk and Bentley Systems), essentially unknown outside Germany and AEC professionals. US investors confuse it with generic CAD. Autodesk revenue is ~6x larger but Nemetschek's openBIM positioning creates an architectural moat Autodesk cannot replicate without destroying its Revit lock-in.

### Moat

1. **openBIM ecosystem:** Champions open IFC standards - wins European market share vs Autodesk's closed Revit; EU procurement favors open standards
2. **Multi-brand portfolio lock-in:** Allplan (structural), ArchiCAD (architecture, ~18% global architectural BIM share [UNVERIFIED]), Vectorworks (US/UK), Bluebeam (collaboration - dominant in US construction documents)
3. **95% recurring revenue (FY2025):** SaaS/subscription transition essentially complete; ARR EUR 1.2B = highly visible compounding base
4. **Media segment:** Cinema4D (3D animation for Hollywood) - small but incredibly sticky, high-margin niche
5. **Switching cost physics:** Architectural firm's BIM library, templates, family files, and project history all in Nemetschek tools - migration takes 1-2 years and millions of EUR

### Founder / Family

- Nemetschek founding family total **~51%:** Prof. Georg Nemetschek directly ~2.4%; Nemetschek foundations ~38.9%; family members ~5.5%; other family entities ~4.2%
- Family foundations = multi-generational patient capital
- CEO Yves Padrines (since 2022, professional manager) - driving AI-first strategy under family board oversight

### Sourced Economics

**FY 2025 (confirmed):**
- Revenue: ~EUR 1.19B (+22.6% FX-adjusted, ~+19% reported)
- EBITDA: EUR 371.1M (+23.3%) - margin **31.2%** (2024: 30.2%)
- ARR: "Almost EUR 1.2B" (Q1 2026, +21% FX-adj)
- Recurring revenue share: **~92-95%** (record)

**FY 2026 Guidance:**
- Revenue growth: **+14-15%** (FX-adjusted organic)
- EBITDA margin: **32-33%**

**Valuation (June 2026):**
- Share price: ~EUR 69 (down ~55% from 2024 peak ~EUR 138)
- Market cap: ~EUR 8.0B
- EV/EBITDA: **~20-25x** (varies by source; current likely ~22-24x given share price decline from Dec-2025 source of 30.14x)
- P/E TTM: ~30x; forward P/E ~21-22x at 15% earnings growth
- Analysts: Majority Buy; average target ~EUR 110 (+59% from current)
- Historical peak: 50-80x EV/EBITDA in 2021-2022; current 22-25x is arguably fair for 15% grower with 31-33% EBITDA margin and 95% recurring

### 10-Year Bull Case + Asymmetric Math

**Base case (ARR CAGR 13-15%):**
- BIM mandate tailwind, US/Asia penetration
- By 2036: Revenue ~EUR 4-5B, EBITDA ~EUR 1.4-1.75B
- At 25x EV/EBITDA: Market cap ~EUR 35-44B = **4-5x from today**

**Bull case (AI-augmented ARPU uplift, 18-20% ARR CAGR):**

- EBITDA margin 38% (SaaS leverage)
- By 2036: Revenue ~EUR 7B, EBITDA ~EUR 2.7B

**Downside floor:** 95% recurring = revenue near-protected in construction recessions. At 15x EV/EBITDA (distressed for 90%+ recurring-revenue business): ~EUR 5.5B = ~EUR 47/share (-32% from current).
**Asymmetry: 5-10x upside / 0.3x downside.**

### Top 3 Risks

1. **AI disruption (genuine but mispriced):** If LLM-based architectural design tools commoditize the workflow, Nemetschek's stickiness could erode. 5-7yr risk horizon (not 2-3yr).
2. **Autodesk counter-response:** Autodesk dominant in North America; could discount Revit aggressively or acquire BIM collaboration tools.
3. **Construction cycle sensitivity:** While recurring revenue is protected, new-customer ARR growth linked to construction starts. Prolonged EU downturn slows growth rate.

## GEM 3: MENSCH UND MASCHINE SOFTWARE SE (MUM.DE)

**Name:** Mensch und Maschine Software SE
**Ticker + venue:** MUM.DE (Xetra) | ISIN: DE0006580806
**Country:** Germany (Wessling, Bavaria)
**Market cap:** ~EUR 627-650M (share ~EUR 36-40, ~17.3M shares, June 2026)

### Wave + Why Under-Covered

**Structural wave:** Manufacturing digitization. CNC machining (aerospace, medical, toolmaking, automotive) is being automated at scale - 5-axis machining, robotic cells, additive manufacturing. Open Mind's **hyperMILL** CAM software is the programming brain. Also: BIM construction digitization (same wave as Nemetschek, but from the CAM/machining side).

**Why under-covered:** Market cap EUR 0.63B. Company name opaque ("Man and Machine"). Only 4-5 analysts cover it. The Autodesk reseller transition (phasing out resale for commission model) created a reported revenue collapse that confuses screeners: FY2025 reported revenue -27%. But gross profit rose +4.2% and EBIT margin hit 28.3% - the quality signal is the opposite of the revenue headline.

### Moat

1. **Open Mind / hyperMILL:** Top-5 global CAM software (CIMdata 2020 [UNVERIFIED]). Strongest in 5-axis simultaneous machining - switching cost is extreme (machining programs, post-processors, operator training takes 12-24 months to rebuild)
2. **Own IP:** Unlike pure resellers, MuM develops proprietary software (Open Mind CAM, MuM BIM tools). Own-IP generates 28%+ EBIT margins vs 3-5% in reselling
3. **Founder-CEO alignment:** Adi Drotleff (founder, CEO, Chairman) holds **~45.7% of shares** - one of the largest founder stakes in any German-listed software company. Total insider ~53%.
4. **Subscription transition:** Autodesk model shift mechanically compressed reported revenue but increased recurring gross profit. By 2026-2027 the revenue line normalizes and the margin picture becomes visible.

### Founder / Family

- **Adi Drotleff (founder + CEO):** ~45.7% directly
