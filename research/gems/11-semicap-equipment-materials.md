## GEM 1: DISCO (6146.T)
_(Full profile also in Japan lane — cross-confirmed as a top pick by both lanes.)_
- Japan | ~¥7.9tn (~$52B) | Mature compounder, secular re-acceleration.

Dicing saws, grinding wheels, laser saws — the final mechanical step cutting/thinning wafers. Three tailwinds: advanced packaging (chiplets, HBM, 2.5D/3D — every innovation adds dicing/thinning steps; HBM ground <50µm); SiC/GaN power (mechanically harder, needs Disco's grinding — "near lockdown on power semis"); consumables flywheel (~30–35% of revenue, riskless, scales with installed base). Under-covered: Japanese-domestic coverage; "Will" internal-currency culture misread; consumables undervalued as "hardware."

**Moat:** ~70–80% global dicing/grinding share; blade-machine co-engineering (re-qualify all recipes at TSMC-scale = months of downtime, prohibitive switching).

**Ownership:** Sekiya family stake (exact current % unverified vs DART; foreign institutions ~54% but family alignment intact).

**Economics:** FY2025 revenue ¥393.3bn (+27.9%); FY2026 ¥436.9bn (+11.1%); Q2 FY2026 op margin 42.4%; gross margin ~70%; net margin ~31%; ROIC 35.8–54%; net cash; strong FCF conversion. P/E ~57x TTM / ~43x fwd; EV/EBITDA ~30x. Not cheap — quality compounding, not value.

**10-yr bull:** Packaging complexity multiplies dicing/thinning steps 2–4x; SiC volumes 10–20x; consumables compound with the installed base; revenue 3–5x over a decade; FCF/share 15–20%/yr at 35–42% margins. Bear floor: consumables hold 60–70% of revenue even in a severe cycle.

**Risks:** (1) China substitution (~35% sales; CETC etc. government-promoted — #1 risk). (2) Capex cycle cuts (down years 2019, 2023). (3) New dicing paradigm (laser/plasma — Disco has its own laser products).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — specific high-quality monopoly with pricing power and secular tailwinds, keeping Asian semi-supply-chain exposure the ETF only captures indirectly. **Conviction: 4/5** (entry matters at 43x fwd).

## GEM 2: LASERTEC (6920.T)
- Japan | TSE | ~¥3.36tn (~$22B) | Growth/transition — near-term air pocket, long-cycle winner.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Makes actinic EUV photomask inspection systems — the only tools that inspect photomasks at the actual EUV wavelength (13.5nm). The single most critical QC step in advanced chip manufacturing: every EUV mask defect must be detected before exposing thousands of wafers. Every new EUV layer (Intel/TSMC/Samsung add layers per generation) and High-NA EUV (ASML EXE:5000) demands more mask qualification. Under-covered: Japanese sell-side only; Western analysts focus on ASML (scanner) and KLA (in-fab) and miss that Lasertec is the sole gatekeeper for the mask feeding every EUV scanner.

**Moat:** ~90–100% global share in actinic EUV mask inspection — the only commercial 13.5nm-wavelength system; KLA's DUV alternative can't detect all EUV-relevant defects; TSMC/Samsung/SK Hynix/Intel Foundry all require Lasertec qualification; High-NA expands the TAM (ACTIS A200HiT launched Oct 2025).

**Ownership:** No controlling family identified; insider data not found (unverified).

**Economics (FY ends Jun):** Revenue FY2025 ¥251.5bn (+17.8%); op income ¥122.8bn (48.8% margin); net margin ~33.7%; FY2026 guidance ¥220bn (−12.5%), op income ¥100bn (~45.5%); orders FY2025 ¥105.2bn (−61.4% — critical near-term fact); backlog ¥315.9bn (−31.6%); net cash; ROIC unverified (asset-light, implied high). Stock corrected ~65% from the 2024 ~¥71,000 peak to ~¥31,000–33,000. P/E ~46–47x (FY2025) / ~55–60x on trough FY2026 earnings.

**Adversarial:** The order decline is the central fact — orders −61% FY2025, revenue guided −12.5% FY2026; mgmt expects recovery from CY2026 as chipmakers resume EUV capex after digestion. The structural monopoly is intact; the pain is cyclical capex timing.

**10-yr bull:** Every EUV scanner sold permanently increases mask-inspection demand; ASML guided 90+ High-NA units through 2030; orders recover CY2026–27 (TSMC N2/N1.4, Intel 18A/14A); revenue re-accelerates to ¥300–400bn by FY2028–30; at 45%+ margins, op income ¥135–180M; re-rate to 35–40x → 3–5x from trough-priced levels.

**Risks:** (1) Order-timing concentration (single TSMC/Samsung orders dominate — high earnings volatility). (2) Customer capex cyclicality (if AI capex cools, trough lasts longer). (3) Export-control expansion (METI; catastrophic if extended to Korea/Taiwan, unlikely).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Secunet** — Lasertec's TAM and moat durability are substantially stronger; gain the EUV-inspection chokepoint, an unhedgeable necessity. **Conviction: 4/5** — order trough = the entry; lumpy but durable monopoly.

## GEM 3: PHOTRONICS (PLAB)
- US (mfg US/Taiwan/China/Singapore) | NASDAQ | ~$2.2B | Mature niche with CHIPS-Act catalyst.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Photomasks = templates printing circuit patterns onto wafers; every new chip/node needs new masks. Photronics is the largest merchant (independent) mask maker — the only alternative to captive shops at TSMC/Samsung/Intel. Merchant mask market = Photronics + Toppan + DNP oligopoly. Only high-end-capable US commercial mask maker (critical for CHIPS-Act domestic fabs) and largest commercial maker in Taiwan. Under-covered: sub-$2.5B, few analysts, ~10x earnings, "masks sound low-tech." CHIPS Act + Intel/TSMC Arizona/Ohio = direct catalysts.

**Moat:** Geographic oligopoly (Intel/TSMC US fabs need a US-qualified supplier for national-security compliance — Photronics is the only option); Taiwan JV PDMC (50.01%, co-located with TSMC; DNP non-compete in JV territory); multi-month qualification switching costs; one of only four EUV-mask-capable firms globally. Moat = "very strong oligopoly," not sole-supplier monopoly (more contestable than Disco/Lasertec).

**Ownership:** Insider ~3.7% (~$72M); recent net insider selling (~$12M/3 months — yellow flag); no founder concentration.

**Economics:** Revenue FY2024 $866.9M (−3%); FY2025 ~$862M (flat); op margin ~24.5%; gross margin ~35%; net margin ~15.8%; op CF FY2025 $267.7M; buybacks $97.4M; ROIC 18–22%; cash-positive. P/E ~10x; EV/EBITDA ~6–7x; P/OCF ~8x; OCF yield ~12%. The value entry in the lane.

**10-yr bull:** CHIPS Act → 5–10 new leading-edge US fabs by 2030; each needs a US mask supplier (Photronics the only one); 20% of incremental US demand → revenues +40–60% over 5 yrs with margin expansion; display/FPD (27% of revenue) recovery a second leg. Revenue $862M → $1.3–1.5B by 2030; margins 24%→28–30%; buybacks ~4.5%/yr; EPS +12–15%; re-rate 10x→15–18x = 2–3x + EPS growth.

**Risks:** (1) Revenue growth (FY2024/25 flat; bull case needs CHIPS-Act fab ramps to accelerate). (2) Tech displacement (EUV-mask complexity concentrating at Toppan/DNP). (3) Insider selling.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — only US-capable merchant photomask maker at 10x, 12% OCF yield, US-manufacturing renaissance catalyst. **Conviction: 3/5** (strong oligopoly not monopoly; growth stagnant; 10x = margin of safety).

## Final
Replace **India ETF** with **DISCO** (deepest moat + consumables flywheel + SiC/advanced-packaging) and consider **Lasertec** for **Secunet** (sole EUV-inspection monopoly at a cyclical trough). **Photronics** = the value dark horse (10x, 12% OCF yield, CHIPS-Act optionality).
