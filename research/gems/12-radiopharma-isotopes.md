## GEM 1: IBA — ION BEAM APPLICATIONS (IBAB.BR)
- Belgium | Euronext Brussels / OTC IBAB | ~€458M (~$500M) | Profitable, scaling.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Dominant cyclotron manufacturer for radiopharmaceutical production (~32% global medical-cyclotron share). RLT creates new structural cyclotron demand: Telix ordered multiple Cyclone KIUBE units for its US radiopharmacy network; Jubilant contracted 5; IBA + Framatome announced (Feb 2025) an industrial At-211 cyclotron network across Europe/US (the next short-half-life alpha emitter after Ac-225); IBA acquired ORA (radiochemistry) Dec 2025. Under-covered: Euronext Brussels, ~6 analysts; perceived as a "proton therapy company" (~60–65% of revenue); European small-cap healthcare falls between desks.

**Moat:** 32% share + 1,600+ installed machines over 35+ yrs (locked-in, high-margin recurring service); cyclotrons are 10–20 yr assets with proprietary software/parts/service (no switching); the At-211/Framatome network is a regulated, capital-intensive build only IBA + a major nuclear player can do; NRC/FDA/Euratom relationships non-replicable; founder-aligned ownership.

**Ownership:** Sustainable Anchorage SA (IBA employees/founder vehicle) 20.49% equity / 30.42% voting; founder Yves Jongen still an internal director.

**Economics (FY2025):** Revenue €620M (+24.5%); gross margin 32.2%; adj EBIT €27.4M (+58%, ~4.4% margin); net income €12.7M (+38%); backlog €1.6B (all-time high); net debt €58M; FCF −€35M (capex-heavy ORA + investment); ROIC 13.2%; FY2026 adj EBIT guidance ≥€32M (+17%); 2028 REBIT target ~10% of revenue.

**Valuation:** Mkt cap €458M; EV €510M; EV/Revenue 0.82x; EV/EBIT 18.6x (trough margins); fwd P/E 9.5x (recovery); consensus target €16.67 vs ~€15.72. Anomalously cheap for 32% cyclotron share in a secular-growth market — the proton-therapy legacy discount does the work and should fade.

**10-yr bull:** RLT drives 5–10x cyclotron demand for therapeutic-isotope production (At-211, Cu-67, Sc-47 networks); 1,600+ machines become a recurring services+software platform worth €100M+/yr by 2030; At-211/Framatome adds a new recurring stream; REBIT 10%+ of €900M → €90M+ EBIT → €1–1.5B cap on 15–17x = ~3x.

**Risks:** (1) Proton therapy margin-dilutive longer than expected (chronic large-contract execution risk; €1.6B backlog but negative-margin legacy keeps burning cash). (2) Cyclotron commoditization (GE post-NMP, Siemens, Sumitomo, ACSI); At-211 network unproven at commercial scale. (3) Belgian small-cap illiquidity (~€2M/day).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Secunet** — "ASML of the radiopharma supply chain" (infrastructure every new radiopharmacy must buy); complementary to E&Z (E&Z = isotope supplier; IBA = hardware/network layer). **Conviction: 4/5.**

## GEM 2: ASP ISOTOPES (ASPI)
- US (ops South Africa) | NASDAQ | ~$980M (ranged $490M–$1.8B/12mo) | Early commercial, pre-profit.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Lu-177 needs Yb-176 precursor; until 2023 the only commercial source of highly enriched Yb-176 was Russia — a geopolitical single-point-of-failure for the entire Lu-177/RLT chain. ASP commercialized Quantum Enrichment (laser-based; enrichment factors up to 678x vs <20x older methods) enabling low-cost ex-Russia Yb-176. Apr-2025 commissioned its first Yb-176 QE facility in Pretoria. Also makes Si-28 (quantum computing), C-14, with HALEU longer-term. Under-covered: South Africa ops; straddles nuclear/isotopes/quantum (no clean category); retail "uranium/AI hype" masks the radiopharma moat.

**Moat:** Only ex-Russia commercial Yb-176 enrichment facility in OECD chains — a strategic chokepoint as Lu-177 demand grows 3–4x by 2030; patent-protected QE process; strategic necessity for Novartis/SHINE/ITM de-risking Russian dependency. Founder Paul Mann ~6% (returned as CEO Jan 2026); insiders ~17%.

**Economics:** FY2025 revenue $23.85M (+476%); net loss −$175M; 9mo loss −$96.5M; ~125.9M shares (+75% YoY — severe dilution); stock $7.78 (52-wk $3.92–$14.49); analyst target $13; FY2031 EBITDA target >$300M (aspirational). At ~$980M on $24M revenue = ~41x sales (venture-stage multiple on a listed company).

**10-yr bull:** Geopolitical + RLT demand forces full Yb-176 de-risking; ASP becomes the "ASML of radiopharma precursors" with near-100% ex-Russia share; $200–400M revenue by 2030 at 40%+ margins; re-rate to 15–20x EBITDA → $2–4B.

**Risks:** (1) Dilution kills equity (burned ~$150M+ vs $24M revenue; 75% annual share growth — uninvestable unless samples convert to long-term anchor contracts fast). (2) Tech scale risk (QE works at pilot scale; SHINE/ITM may integrate own enrichment). (3) South Africa jurisdiction.

**Portfolio fit:** Hard at current size/risk; if anything, replace **India ETF** as a high-conviction speculative slot. **Conviction: 2.5/5** (→3/5 if a Novartis/SHINE supply agreement is announced).
