# Lane 21 — Datacenter Physical Infrastructure
### Liquid Cooling · Thermal Management · Power Distribution · Busbars · Backup Power

_Research date: 2026-06-01. Informational synthesis; not financial advice. All figures sourced and flagged where unverified._

---

## Mandate for this lane

AI datacenter buildout is accelerating rack-power densities from ~10 kW/rack (air-cooled era) to 100–200 kW/rack (Blackwell/MI300X clusters). That shift makes liquid cooling mandatory, not optional, and creates massive demand for every physical layer underneath the GPU: thermal management units, CDUs (coolant distribution units), direct-liquid-cooling loops, intelligent PDUs, 48 V DC busbars, and reserve-power (UPS/battery) infrastructure. These are the literal picks and shovels of the AI supercycle — boring, B2B, infrastructure-grade, invisible to retail. Avoided the already-obvious large-caps (Vertiv ~$40B, Eaton, Schneider). Hunted the one bracket below.

**Market context (sourced):**
- AI datacenter liquid cooling market: $3.7B in 2026 → projected $17.8B by 2036 at ~17% CAGR (Morningstar/AccessWire, May 2026).
- Liquid cooling adoption: <10% of datacenters today; new NVIDIA Blackwell/AMD MI400 deployments mandating it → step-change adoption not gradual.
- Broader datacenter power & thermal market: $27B liquid cooling market estimated by 2035 at 18.2% CAGR (multiple industry sources).

### The wave + why under-covered / early
Modine was a boring auto-parts thermal supplier. CEO Neil Brinker began restructuring ~2022, shedding auto exposure for the "Climate Solutions" segment. The datacenter cooling (Airedale by Modine brand) business went from ~$300M to $1.1 billion in FY2026 (ended March 2026), growing 73% YoY (sourced: Modine 10-K/8-K, May 2026). Most analysts still categorise it as an "industrial/auto" name — the re-rating to datacenter infrastructure compounder is underway but not complete. The decisive signal: in May 2026 Modine announced a $4 billion Long-Term Capacity Agreement (LTA) through 2029 with an undisclosed "strategic data center customer," receiving a $165M upfront deposit — essentially a hyperscaler locking up Modine's cooling capacity for three years (sourced: Modine IR/PR Newswire, May 2026, confirmed DataCenter Knowledge and multiple financial outlets). This is not a revenue figure; it is a committed-supply agreement that guarantees demand visibility through 2029.

### Moat
- **Brand + engineering heritage**: Airedale by Modine is a 50+ year British CRAC/CRAH brand with deep datacenter credentials. Not a startup.
- **Manufacturing scale**: Opened dedicated facilities in UK, US (Franklin WI — 155,000 sq ft), and India in 2025 specifically for datacenter products. The $165M deposit funds further capacity expansion.
- **Integrated solutions**: Air-side economisers, rear-door heat exchangers, CDUs, in-row cooling — full portfolio rather than a single product.
- **Switching costs**: Datacenter cooling is designed in at build-phase; mid-cycle vendor replacement is very rare.
- **Contract lock-in**: LTA structure with minimum commitments creates revenue floor.

### Founder / insider ownership
- CEO Neil Brinker holds ~238K shares (~0.45% of 52.8M shares outstanding). He sold ~32K shares in Dec 2025 via pre-planned 10b5-1 (sourced: GuruFocus, Investing.com). No controlling family block. Broadly institutionally owned.
- Not founder-led. Conviction penalty applied.

### 10-year bull case + asymmetric math
- $4B LTA 2027–2029 = avg ~$1.33B/yr guaranteed minimum revenue. FY2027 guide: +20–35% sales growth.

- Downside floor: legacy Performance Technologies ($2.1B revenue, profitable) plus the $165M LTA deposit provides cash buffer.

### The wave + why under-covered / early
Carel makes precision electronic control systems (controllers, humidity systems, IoT software) for HVAC/R. Datacenter precision cooling is a fast-growing segment of its HVAC business. Almost zero English-language analyst coverage outside Italy. As liquid cooling shifts to sophisticated precision-temperature-controlled loops, Carel's controllers become the "nervous system" of the thermal management stack — indispensable, embedded, invisible. Revenue inflected: Q3 2025 organic HVAC growth +14%, driven by North America datacenter demand (sourced: Carel 9M 2025 results).

### Moat
- **Deep OEM embedding**: Controllers specified into Stulz, Aermec, Rittal HVAC products. Near-impossible to swap mid-product-generation.
- **IoT/software growing moat**: Carel tGO and BOSS2 cloud platforms create data-led switching costs.
- **R&D intensity**: ~9–10% of revenue (flag: unverified — check annual report). Iterative product leadership.
- **Natural refrigerant transition**: CO2/propane/ammonia require specialist control architectures. Carel leads; competitors cannot catch up quickly.
- **Global channel**: 75+ country distribution via OEM + specialist distributors.

### The wave + why under-covered / early
AAON is a Tulsa, Oklahoma custom HVAC manufacturer founded by Norman Asbjornson in 1987. In 2021 it acquired BASX Labs (liquid cooling and precision datacenter cooling specialist). That acquisition is now going parabolic: BASX Q4 2025 revenue +138.8% YoY to $181.4M (sourced: AAON Q4 2025 press release). Liquid cooling = 40% of BASX datacenter sales. Yet AAON sits in the "commercial HVAC" screener bucket and receives almost no datacenter coverage. It is arguably the most "hidden" of the four gems in this lane.

### Moat
- **Custom-to-order manufacturing**: Made-to-spec commercial HVAC with structurally higher margins than catalogue competitors (Trane, Carrier). Direct price comparison is harder.
- **BASX proprietary liquid cooling IP**: CDUs, rear-door heat exchangers, immersion-cooling R&D — engineered internally rather than assembled.
- **Founder culture / lean overhead**: AAON has run with minimal bloat for 35+ years. Operating margins structurally above peer set.
- **Backlog visibility**: $1.32B total backlog (+104% YoY at year-end 2025), with a $174.5M single-customer liquid cooling order confirmed (sourced).

### Founder / insider ownership (sourced)
- **Norman H. Asbjornson** (founder): ~13.1M shares, ~16% of company (sourced: GuruFocus / WallStreetZen, 2026).
- Total insider ownership: ~17% (sourced: Simply Wall St).
- Exceptional alignment for a company this size — one of the highest founder stakes of any mid-to-large HVAC manufacturer.
- Gary Fields (BASX integration driver) transitioned to advisor role in May 2025; new CEO leadership is untested. Flag as transition risk.

### Moat
- Thermal integration across dry-side (enclosures, busbars) and wet-side (liquid cooling) — end-to-end.

- Scale + certifications (UL/CE) = genuine barrier to small entrants.
