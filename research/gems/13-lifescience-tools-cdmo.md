## PICK 1: MEDPACE (MEDP)
- US (Cincinnati) | NASDAQ | ~$12.9B (~$448/share) | Mature compounder, cyclical trough.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Secular outsourcing of clinical trials by small/mid-cap biotech (which lacks internal trial infrastructure and MUST outsource). The only fully-integrated, pure-play small-biotech CRO at scale. 29 analysts (not undiscovered by name) but the market is mispricing cyclical softness as structural deterioration.

**Moat:** Vertically owned central labs (Cincinnati/Leuven/Singapore/Shanghai, 30–40% EBITDA); single-vendor lock-in (can't switch CROs mid-trial); net retention >115%; 1,400+ in-house MDs/PhDs; proprietary ClinTrak platform; 96% small/mid-biotech revenue (wins on complexity/speed not price).

**Ownership:** Dr. August Troendle (founder/CEO/Chairman 33 yrs) ~20.9% (5.57M shares); total insider ~21.5%; just bought $10m in the open market.

**Economics:** FY2025 revenue $2.530bn (+20%); EBITDA margin 22.0%; net margin 17.8%; op CF $713m; FCF ~$682m (~27% margin, capex only 1.7% of sales); ROIC 22–40%; backlog $3.03bn (+4.3% only); minimal net debt. 2026 guidance $2.755–2.855bn (9–13%), EBITDA $605–635m. P/E ~28x; EV/EBITDA ~20x; P/FCF ~19x; ~32% off the 52-wk high.

**Key risk alert:** Levi & Korsinsky securities investigation (Feb 2026) after Q4 2025 disclosed "highest cancellations in over a year" (metabolic/GLP-1 trials). Book-to-bill: Q1-25 0.90x → Q2 1.03 → Q3 1.20 → Q4 1.04 → **Q1-26 0.88x** (genuinely concerning, not fully explained). Cancellations concentrated in metabolic/GLP-1 (boom-bust sub-segment); core oncology holding. Cyclical, but could suppress growth to single digits through 2026–27.

**10-yr bull:** Biotech VC funding normalizes (2027+), book-to-bill recovers >1.15x; 15–18% revenue CAGR as CRO-of-choice for 12,000+ small biotechs; founder buys the trough; 20x FCF in 2033 on $2.5bn FCF → $50bn+ (~4x).

**Risks:** (1) Sustained biotech-funding drought keeps book-to-bill <1.0x for 2+ yrs. (2) Securities litigation headline/settlement. (3) Troendle succession (33-yr tenure, no named successor).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** or **Vietnam** — US life-science infrastructure compounder, nothing like Bachem/Sartorius/Bioventix (clinical execution, not bioprocessing). **Conviction: 4/5** — buy the dip, not the distress.

## PICK 2: CHEMOMETEC (CHEMM.CO)
- Denmark | Nasdaq Copenhagen | ~DKK 5.4bn (~€725m/$790m; down ~29% in a week — verify) | High-growth CGT-infrastructure monopoly. FY ends June.

**Wave & under-coverage:** NucleoCounter is the de-facto gold standard for CAR-T / cell & gene therapy (CGT) QC — every FDA-approved CAR-T uses it in manufacturing. As CGT industrializes (~doubling every 3–4 yrs), each new suite installs NucleoCounters and then consumes proprietary Via-Cassette consumables perpetually (razor-blade). 1–5 analysts on Nasdaq Copenhagen, ~zero Anglophone coverage; $5bn-cap Danish CGT-QC company off everyone's radar.

**Moat:** FDA-filing lock-in (CGT manufacturers specify ChemoMetec in BLA/IND filings; switching = 18–24 month revalidation — the strongest moat mechanism in small-cap life-science tools); Via-Cassette IP; gold-standard brand (40%+ of cell-therapy NCI trials reference it); publication network effect; no viable GMP cell-counting substitute at the price/accuracy point.

**Ownership:** Co-founder Hans Martin Glensbjerg (Vice-Chairman) ~3.0–3.5% (single source); new CEO Martin Helbo Behrens (31, internal). KKR does NOT own it (separate from Biotage).

**Economics:** FY2024/25 revenue DKK 495.6m (+21.5%); EBITDA margin ~54–55%; recurring ~70%; FY2025/26 guidance DKK 565–580m (~17%), EBITDA DKK 320–335m (~57%); net cash DKK 257m. ROIC implied >40% (estimate). Fwd EV/EBITDA ~15–16x (down from 35.7x Sep 2025); ~9x fwd sales (vs 19x six months ago).

**10-yr bull:** CGT approvals ~30 → 150+ by 2033; each suite adds NucleoCounters + cassette consumption; expansion into bioprocessing QC (Roche partnership) and pharma batch testing; 15–20% revenue CAGR; EBITDA to 58–60%; 25x EBITDA on DKK 2bn+ → 5–8x.

**Risks:** (1) Allogeneic ("off-the-shelf") therapies could reduce per-treatment cell-counting events vs autologous CAR-T. (2) CGT funding winter pauses placements (consumables resilient, growth slows). (3) The ~29% one-week decline may signal a near-term miss — verify before acting.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — pure CGT picks-and-shovels, different end-market from bioprocessing/GLP-1. **Conviction: 4/5** (verify the recent drop).

## PICK 3: RAYSEARCH LABORATORIES (RAY-B.ST)
- Sweden | Nasdaq Stockholm | ~SEK 6–7bn (~$560–650m) | Founder-owned software monopoly; radiotherapy treatment planning.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Cancer incidence +1–2%/yr; radiotherapy treats ~50% of patients, underutilized in EM. RayStation = world's leading independent radiotherapy treatment-planning system (TPS) in 1,200+ clinics, 51 countries — hardware-agnostic (runs on Varian, Elekta, Accuray), the layer connecting disparate hardware. As clinics mix vendors and adopt proton/MR-Linac/adaptive therapy, a vendor-neutral best-in-class TPS becomes mandatory. 1–5 analysts, ~invisible outside Sweden.

**Moat:** Embedded in daily cancer treatment workflows — switching means revalidating all protocols (months, regulatory filings, patient risk); near-zero churn; best-in-class Monte Carlo dose calc + AI auto-planning; growing maintenance/support recurring revenue (support +13.9% 2025); proton-therapy partnerships (Mevion, IBA); RayCare OIS expansion.

**Ownership:** Founder Johan Löf (CEO, largest holder) — after Mar-2025 placement, 40.5% of votes / 10.2% of capital (dual-class A-shares); "personal liquidity buffer" sale (not a red flag).

**Economics (FY2025):** Net sales SEK 1,344m (+13%, +19% organic); EBIT SEK 292m (+12%, 22% margin); net income SEK 228m; EPS SEK 6.65; FCF SEK 82m (low vs earnings on WC build); dividend SEK 4.00 (60% payout). CEO 2026 guidance: "at least 25% operating margin." Share ~SEK 180; cap ~SEK 6.2bn; P/E ~27–28x; EV/EBIT ~20x; sole active analyst target SEK 360 (~100% upside).

**10-yr bull:** RT access expands to EM (each new Linac needs TPS); adaptive RT (MR-Linac) needs best-in-class algorithms bundled systems can't match; RayCare penetrates the base; revenue 15% CAGR to SEK 5–6bn by 2033; EBIT to 30%+; 25x EBIT on SEK 1.5bn → SEK 37bn (~6x).

**Risks:** (1) Varian (Siemens Healthineers) or Elekta bundling an improved subsidized TPS. (2) Hospital capital-budget freezes. (3) Löf succession (40.5% votes, no named successor).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Secunet** or an **ETF** — founder-led monopoly in a critical clinical workflow, ~1 analyst, fills "software infrastructure for cancer treatment." **Conviction: 4/5** — the archetype of what this book hunts.

## PICK 4: VERACYTE (VCYT)
- US | NASDAQ | ~$3.55B | Growth-to-profitability inflection; cancer-genomics diagnostics.

**Wave:** Molecular oncology + payer-guideline inclusion. Tests avoid unnecessary surgery (thyroid) / over-treatment (prostate) — they save the system money (reimbursement tailwind). GAAP net income $66m in 2025 (+175%). Orthogonal to bioprocessing/GLP-1/peptides. **Moat:** NCCN guideline inclusion (Afirma thyroid, Decipher prostate/bladder); GRID database (100,000+ genomic profiles = data flywheel); 800,000+ patients tested; Medicare (34%) + private payer (47%) breadth; patents 2027–2040.

**Ownership — weakness:** Not founder-led; CEO Marc Stapley ~0.17%; institutional-heavy.

**Economics (FY2025):** Total revenue $517.1M (+16%); testing $493.2M (+18%); GAAP gross margin 70.1%; adj EBITDA $142.5M (27.6%); GAAP net income $66.4M (+175%); op CF $136.3M; FCF ~$126.6M; cash+investments $412.9M (net cash). Q1-2026 +21%, EPS beat 57%, guidance raised. 2026 guidance $570–582M (+10–13%), adj EBITDA ~25%. P/E ~41x TTM / ~25.8x fwd; P/FCF ~25–28x.

**10-yr bull:** Decipher Bladder broad coverage (+$50–100M); AdvanceAD-Tx; MRD ($5bn+); revenue 15% CAGR to ~$2bn by 2033; EBITDA to 35%; 30x EBITDA on $700M → $21bn (~6x). **Risks:** (1) Reimbursement-policy reversal (existential for diagnostics). (2) No founder alignment. (3) Foundation Medicine/Myriad competition; proximity to Bioventix (adjacent, different).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **VinaCapital Vietnam** or **Bioventix** (most contested — adjacent diagnostics). **Conviction: 3/5** — smaller position/watchlist.
