## Gem 1: GOOSEHEAD INSURANCE (GSHD)

**Name | Ticker | Venue | Country | ~Mkt cap | Stage**
Goosehead Insurance Inc. | GSHD | Nasdaq | USA | ~$1.25B | Growth-to-maturity compounder

### Structural wave & why under-covered / early

Goosehead is turning the $320B+ US personal-lines insurance distribution market into a technology-enabled, franchise-royalty machine. The structural wave is three-layered: (1) independent insurance agents are gaining share from captive agents (State Farm, Allstate) because technology platforms let independents shop 150+ carriers in real time — Goosehead's Digital Agent 2.0 platform is the rails; (2) premiums are resetting sharply higher post-2023 weather events, growing the royalty base without adding locations; (3) renewal commissions compound — each policy added is an annuity (50-year policy life on average), so the franchise "installed base" is a growing treasury of recurring royalty-generating policies in force (PIF).

Under-coverage stems from the unusual dual-class structure (Jones family controls via voting agreement with 35%+ of votes), the insurance sector mis-classification (most fund mandates don't look here for a franchise compounder), and the post-COVID franchise over-expansion hangover that created a multi-year "show me" narrative. That cloud is now clearing: Q1 2026 delivered 57% adj EBITDA growth and franchise count is stable at ~956 operating locations with stronger per-franchise productivity.

### Moat

The competitive moat is the **renewals flywheel**: policies-in-force (PIF) compound independently of new franchise openings — even if Goosehead stopped franchising tomorrow, the embedded PIF base (~1.97M policies at Q1 2026, +14% YoY) would generate escalating renewal royalties (50% take of renewal commissions) for decades. The platform further embeds via carrier relationships (150+ carriers, vs a captive's 1), technology switching costs, and proprietary client matching. Scale in carrier negotiations becomes self-reinforcing as volume grows.

### Founder / insider ownership

Mark E. Jones (founder / Executive Chairman) controls 35.1% of shares (including family trusts) under a Voting Agreement structure. Jones son Mark Jr. (President & COO) is also a 10%+ owner. Family buying on dips: Jones Jr. bought 2,650 shares in May 2026 even as the stock was depressed. Estimated combined Jones family economic ownership ~30–35% [verified per SEC 13D/A filings cited in StockTitan].

The moat is the **master franchise license** itself — a 20-year agreement nobody else can replicate.
