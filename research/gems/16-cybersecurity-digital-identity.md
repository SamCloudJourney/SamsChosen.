## GEM 1: ADVENICA (ADVE.ST)
- Sweden | Nasdaq Stockholm (First North) | ~SEK 900–1,100M (~€78–96M) | Small-cap, profitability inflection.

**Wave & under-coverage:** EU NIS2, NATO/EU digital sovereignty, surging defense budgets created a structural tailwind for HIGH-ASSURANCE cybersecurity — classified-information-grade hardware encryption (data diodes, VPN encryptors, cross-domain security) certified up to TOP SECRET (HEMLIG), NATO SECRET, EU SECRET. Invisible: ~118 employees, sub-€100M cap, First North micro-cap, no English sell-side; classified products mean customers/use-cases can't be disclosed (hard to analyze) — exactly the information asymmetry to exploit.

**Moat:** One of the highest moats found in any lane. Regulatory certification = 3–7 yr / tens-of-millions barrier (EU SECRET, NATO SECRET, Swedish N3/TOP SECRET, TEMPEST SDIP-27 Level A); the process requires relationships with national security agencies (FRA/NCSA). Maybe 3–4 firms globally compete (Thales, BAE Applied Intelligence, Rohde & Schwarz — all much larger, less focused). Physical unidirectionality (data diode for OT/SCADA in nuclear/ATC) not switchable; near-zero churn. Sovereign-supply requirement (EU Cyber Solidarity Act). Patent on Three Domain Separation (US 9015825).

**Ownership:** Insiders ~24%; net insider buying TTM; no dominant industrial parent (preferable — no squeeze-out overhang, motivated management).

**Economics:** FY2025 revenue SEK ~203M (+25%); 9M 2025 revenue SEK 152.6M (+36%), EBIT SEK 19.5M (vs 0.7M PY); rolling-12mo revenue SEK 202.3M; FY2025 net income SEK ~16.6M; cash SEK 91.4M (Sep 2025); service revenue 63% (9M); product revenue +74%. EBIT margin FY2025 ~8–10% (near-zero 2023, positive 2024–25 — a profitability inflection). ROIC not calculable (asset-light, high once at scale). P/E ~183x TTM (misleadingly high — just turned profitable). EV/Revenue ~4.5x.

**10-yr bull:** Dominant EU-sovereign certified high-assurance security for military networks, critical infrastructure (energy/nuclear/utilities), government cross-domain systems. NIS2/Cyber Resilience Act/NATO expansion = multi-decade demand. Revenue to SEK 600–800M at 20–25% EBIT → SEK 120–200M earnings → 30x → SEK 3.6–6B (~4–7x). The "Bachem of classified cybersecurity."

**Risks:** (1) Small contract concentration (2–3 large defense/government contracts = 30%+ of revenue; lumpy quarters). (2) Larger-player consolidation (Thales/R&S competing harder — or acquiring, a non-zero positive).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Secunet** (the purer, higher-moat-density, more optionality play) or own alongside. **Conviction: 4/5** (below 5 only on size/liquidity, lumpiness, partly-unverified FY2025 profit).

## GEM 3: NETCOMPANY (NETC.CO)
- Denmark | Nasdaq Copenhagen | ~DKK 17B (~€2.28B) | Platform compounder.

"Tyler Technologies of European sovereign government IT" — builds bespoke case-management/benefits/tax platforms that become constitutional infrastructure. Wave: every European government must rebuild 1990s welfare/tax/citizen systems, needing a vendor that understands tech + EU-law sovereignty. Under-covered: Danish "IT services" (the word "consulting" frightens growth investors) but actually platform-led (AMPLIO redeployed across sovereigns). **Moat:** sovereign platform lock-in (HMRC Trader Support Service £294M to 2032; UK gov IT churn <10%/decade once live); cross-country replicability (Danish welfare components adapt to Norwegian tax/UK customs — low marginal cost); €429M+ HMRC wins; SDC banking-IT acquisition adds high-switching-cost infrastructure. **Ownership:** co-founders André Rogaczewski + Claus Jørgensen via AC NC Holding (CEO + COO roles — positive alignment). **Economics (FY2025):** Revenue DKK 7.89B (+20.7%, ~7.9% organic); adj EBITDA margin 16.9%; net income DKK 256.9M (−45% on SDC integration/one-offs — underlying didn't deteriorate). 2026 guidance 15–20% revenue, 15–18% margin. EV/EBITDA ~10–12x (rough); P/E ~66x TTM (depressed) / fwd ~20–25x normalized. **Risks:** SDC integration; UK-government concentration (£429M HMRC). **Conviction: 3/5** — least hidden ($2.3B), fair-not-cheap; replace **India ETF** (passive). Fills "mission-critical government software platform."
