## GEM 1: VICOR (VICR)
- US (Andover, MA) | ~$15.3B | Mid-cap, profitable, re-accelerating.

**Wave & under-coverage:** AI compute has a physics problem — today's GPU racks draw 200–400kW; next-gen Rubin/GB300 racks exceed 1,000kW. Moving 12V→48V cuts I²R losses 16x, and Vicor's Factorized Power Architecture (FPA) / Vertical Power Delivery (VPD) modules go 48V→sub-1V at the package in a 1.5mm slice. Under-covered: lost Nvidia H100 to Monolithic Power (2023) and was written off; founder-controlled, no Wall Street IR; revenue flat-to-down 2022–24 (trough). Re-discovered after Q1 2026 (+20% revenue, backlog $301M +70% sequential, book-to-bill >2.0).

**Moat:** (1) Proprietary CHiP fab process — can't run on standard CMOS lines; own Andover fab ($1B→$1.5B capacity). (2) Patent/IP — second ITC case vs Delta targeting the VPD topology required for Nvidia Rubin; FY2026 guidance excludes new licensing income pending the 2027 ITC determination. (3) 2nd-gen VPD spec gap — 3 A/mm², 40x current multiplication, 1.5mm thickness; no competitor has matched at 2,000A+ for Rubin/B300.

**Ownership:** Dr. Patrizio Vinciarelli (founder/Chair/CEO) ~19.3%; supervoting control. Active selling at $334 = diversification (still holds $3B+) — monitor velocity.

**Economics:** FY2025 revenue $407.7M; FY2026 guidance ~$570M; FY2025 GM 57.3%, op margin ~18%, net margin 26.2%; Q1 2026 $113M (+20%); backlog $301M; net cash; ~15% ROA. 9M-to-Sep-2025 op cash flow $123.8M, capex ~$4M/qtr.

**Valuation:** P/E ~83x TTM / ~47x fwd; ~$15B for $570M revenue — prices a licensing win + Rubin design-win.

**10-yr bull:** By 2030 VPD = standard power layer in every rack >500kW; revenue $2–3B (product + licensing $200–500M/yr if ITC succeeds); 30x EBIT on $600M+ EBIT → $18–20B from licensing alone.

**Risks:** (1) Design-win risk on Rubin/B300 (no confirmed wins disclosed). (2) ITC outcomes. (3) Valuation (was $40 in early 2024).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** — the "early Micron/AMD" pattern (written off, re-emerging as indispensable). **Conviction: 4/5.**

## GEM 2: nVENT ELECTRIC (NVT)
- Ireland-domiciled, NYSE | ~$18B EV / ~$14–15B cap | Accelerating growth.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Every AI rack needs liquid cooling = leak-proof, pressure-rated, isolated manifolds, CDUs, enclosures. nVent (SCHROFF, HOFFMAN, Eldon) is one of ~3 global firms with the scale/certifications/hyperscaler qualification at gigawatt scale. Under-covered: spun from Pentair 2018 as a "boring enclosures" business — the AI reinvention hasn't caught up with institutional memory; Cramer called it "mini Vertiv" Mar 2026. Data-center revenue ~$1B 2025 (~26% of sales → guided >55% in Q1 2026). Ecolab/CoolIT deal at 29x NTM EBITDA (Mar 2026) is an independent comp.

**Moat:** Hyperscaler-qualification lock-in (sole-sourced per generation; re-qual on live clusters impractical); CoolIT partnership + >1GW deployed since 2020 (reference density Eaton/Legrand can't match quickly); US re-shoring leverage (new Minnesota plant 2026); $2.6B backlog (Q1 2026) = 8–12 months; organic orders +40% Q1 2026.

**Ownership:** No founder (CEO Beth Wozniak); institutional. Capital discipline offsets (FCF conversion 90–95% guided; ROIC ex-GW/intangibles 38.4%).

**Economics:** FY2025 revenue $3.89B (+29.5%); adj EPS $3.35 (+35%); FCF ~$561M; Q4 op margin 19.7%; FY2026 guidance +26–28% reported / +21–23% organic; Q1 2026 $1.242B (+53% reported, +34% organic), backlog $2.6B; adj EPS 2026 $4.00–4.15; ROIC ex-GW 38.4% (reported 9.45% — goodwill drag); EV/EBITDA 24.5x; EV/FCF ~55x; ND/EBITDA 1.6x.

**10-yr bull:** Dominant non-captive liquid-cooling supplier; revenue $4B→$8B+ by 2030; margins to 22–25%; FCF/share +20%; 20x EBIT on $1.6–2.0B EBIT → $32–40B EV vs $18B. Acquisition optionality at the 29x CoolIT comp.

**Risks:** (1) Immersion cooling superseding DLC. (2) Hyperscaler vertical integration. (3) M&A integration/goodwill.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **VinaCapital Vietnam** — fills the electrical/mechanical infrastructure layer of the AI supercycle. **Conviction: 4/5** (no founder; size caps upside vs Vicor).

## GEM 3: BE SEMICONDUCTOR (BESI)
- Netherlands | ~€22.5B (~$24B) | Cycle trough-to-recovery.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Every AI chip (HBM4/chiplets) needs hybrid bonding — sub-micron Cu-Cu interconnect replacing solder bumps (doubles bandwidth, halves power). BESI = acknowledged global leader; >150 cumulative system orders, 18 customers; AMAT took 9% stake Apr 2025. Under-covered: Dutch-listed, poor ADR liquidity, sits in "European industrial" bucket; ~15 analysts vs 40+ for ASML.

**Moat:** <10nm alignment accuracy (competitors Hanmi/ASMPT 12–18 months behind); Bernstein sees Samsung/SK Hynix/Micron "all highly likely" to adopt for HBM4; AMAT co-development (full stack); Investor-Day target €1.5–1.9B revenue at 40–55% op margins (vs €173M op income 2025); Lam/AMAT takeover interest = downside floor (~€270–300/share).

**Adversarial:** Already crowded. €284/share = P/E ~151x TTM / ~75x fwd; EV/EBITDA ~96x; EV/Revenue ~36x. On €1.5–1.9B revenue at 40% margins → €680M EBIT at 30x = €20B EV ≈ today. 10-yr bull is priced in unless margins exceed targets.

**Ownership:** Richard Blickman (founder/CEO since 1995) ~1.7%; AMAT 9%; institutions 64%; 95% payout (cuts both ways).

**Economics (FY2025):** Revenue €591.3M (−2.7%); GM 63.3%; EBIT margin 29.3%; net margin 22.3%; net income €131.6M; op cash flow €178.1M; net cash €36M; Q1 2026 revenue €184.9M (+28.3%), orders €269.7M (+104.5%).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Bachem** — adds advanced-packaging exposure, but more cyclical; price matters. **Conviction: 3/5** — buy-on-dips (4/5 at €180–200; balanced at €284).

## GEM 4: FABRINET (FN)
- NYSE (Cayman/Thailand) | ~$23B | High-growth.

**Wave & under-coverage:** AI clusters need photonic interconnects — 400G/800G/1.6T transceivers to move data between GPUs. Fabrinet is the dominant contract manufacturer for optical transceivers (76% of revenue optical comms; DCI ~doubled YoY Q1 FY2026). Misframed as a low-margin "contract manufacturer" — actually a precision micro-assembly monopolist at micron tolerances with 25-yr sole-source ties (Coherent, Ciena, Nvidia).

**Moat:** 99.7% yields at micron tolerances (100–1000x tighter than PCB); re-qualifying an optical partner takes 18–24 months; sole-source on 1.6T modules (JPM sees $1.5B market 2026, Fabrinet majority); asset-light, $0 net debt, $969M cash; new AWS partnership diversifies beyond Nvidia/Cisco.

**Adversarial:** Nvidia ~30% + Cisco ~18% = ~48% from two customers — central bear case. Counter: optical manufacturing more specialized than what Nvidia has in-sourced; Building 10 expansion suggests Nvidia commitment.

**Ownership:** Founder Tom Mitchell retired Oct 2025 (25 yrs); CEO Seamus Grady professional; low insider.

**Economics:** Q1 FY2026 revenue $978M (+22%); FY2025 ~$3.6B (+19%, record); GM ~22.1%; op margin ~18.2%; net margin ~9.8–9.9%; FCF ~$206M; cash $969M; debt $0; ROE 18.8%; Q2 guidance $1.05–1.1B; P/E ~55x TTM / ~41x fwd; EV/EBITDA ~47x.

**10-yr bull:** Every AI DC for Rubin successors needs 1.6T–3.2T interconnects at 10–20x density; Building 10 doubles capacity; revenue $6–8B by 2030; op margins to 12–14%; FCF $700M–$1B; 30x FCF → $21–30B.

**Risks:** (1) Customer concentration/in-sourcing (catastrophic). (2) Thailand single-country FX/tariff (baht −11.8% since 2023). (3) Silicon-photonics co-packaging displacement (5–10 yr).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** or **Vietnam** — precision-manufacturing moat in the data-transmission layer; Thailand base adds indirect EM-Asia exposure with real quality. **Conviction: 3/5** (customer concentration caps it at 4/5).
