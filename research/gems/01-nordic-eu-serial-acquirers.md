# European & Nordic Serial Acquirers / Compounders — Hidden Gems Lane

**Analyst Date: 31 May 2026 | Scope: Beyond the already-owned (Topicus, Vitec) and the ultra-famous (Lifco, Indutrade, Halma)**

## Executive Summary
After screening the full Nordic/European serial-acquirer universe (~30 listed names on Nasdaq Stockholm alone, plus UK and Continental peers), three genuinely compelling ideas stand out at current prices as of late May 2026. A fourth (Lagercrantz) is high quality but not a buy today. The field is more crowded and discovered than in 2020–22; the easy money has been made on the obvious names. The remaining gems require either (a) accepting a turnaround narrative, (b) a complexity discount the market hasn't closed, or (c) structural underpricing vs obvious peers.

## GEM 1: TEQNION AB (TEQ.ST)
- **Ticker:** TEQ.ST (Nasdaq First North Growth Market, Stockholm) | **Country:** Sweden (+ growing UK/Ireland) | **Mkt cap:** SEK ~2.7bn (~€230m) | **Stage:** Post-stumble turnaround compounder, operationally inflecting.

**Structural wave & why under-covered:** Industrial niche consolidation — buying owner-managed Swedish/UK industrial businesses (SEK 25–150m revenue, 15–25%+ EBITA margins) before they go to PE. Decades-long wave: average Nordic owner is 58; succession is perennial; PE ignores sub-€10m EBIT. Severely under-covered because (a) it trades on First North Growth Market (limited index/institutional access), (b) it had a high-profile stumble in 2023–24 when organic growth went negative and margins collapsed, (c) founder-CEO holds only a mid-single-digit stake (no dynasty anchor). International investors have never heard of it.

**Moat:** Decentralised operating model with ~8-person HQ; subsidiaries keep brand/staff/management (critical to win sellers who fear PE integration). Pays ~4–5x EBITA (verified: Cambs Compressor Feb 2026 at 4.1x; Birkett Bogmats, HT Servo all sub-5x). UK/Ireland expansion (TEQ Väst) at 26% EBITA margin. Subsidiaries supply mission-critical industrial consumables/precision components/motion control where switching isn't worth it.

**Ownership:** Johan Steene (CEO since 2009) ~5% (~SEK 133m), bought 100k shares at SEK 202 in 2024 on the dip. Daniel Zhang (Deputy/CXO, ex-McKinsey/Bain) heir-apparent. Woodlock House Family Capital (Chris Mayer, author of *100 Baggers*) a major external holder — quality signal. No dynasty/family control (risk + opportunity).

**Economics (sourced):** Revenue FY2025 SEK 1.80bn (+15%). EBITA margin ~11% FY2025; Q1 2026 14.3% (record). Organic growth Q1 2026 −5.4% (deliberately exiting unprofitable Nordic legacy revenue). TEQ Väst EBITA margin ~26%; TEQ Nord 11.6% in Q1 2026 (recovering from 5%). 9 acquisitions in 2025 adding SEK ~191m sales and ~SEK 50m EBT (~25% EBT margin). EV/EBITDA ~9.1x annualised Q1 / 16.6x TTM. Net debt SEK ~336m (~1.6x, below 2.5x target). Credit facility expanded SEK 575m→1bn in 2026. ROIC implied ~25% on deal price (aggregate unverified). FCF historically weak in transition; Q1 2026 inflecting. EPS FY2025 SEK 5.74.

**Valuation:** Share SEK ~157; mkt cap SEK 2.68bn; EV ~SEK 3.1bn. EV/EBITDA ~9.1x annualised vs 16.6x TTM (gap = margin inflection). Fwd P/E ~28x on depressed earnings; if EBITA recovers to 9%+ on SEK 1.8–2.0bn revenue → EBITA SEK 162–180m → fwd EV/EBITA ~17–19x. Cheap vs Lifco 30x+, Addtech 25x+.

**10-yr bull case:** Doubles EPS every 5 yrs (15% CAGR target). UK platform → 50%+ of revenue at 25%+ EBITA; Nordic legacy improves/rationalised; facility deployed at 4–5x on 20–25% EBITA deals. By 2036: SEK 5–7bn revenue, ~18% EBITA = ~SEK 900m EBITA; at 20x = SEK 18bn vs SEK 2.7bn today = **6–7x** base, **8–10x** at 25x.

**Risks:** (1) FCF/working-capital discipline — 2023–24 stumble was partly self-inflicted (bad inventory mgmt); if turnaround stalls you're paying 16x for stagnant earnings. (2) UK execution — TEQ Väst small (~5 subs). (3) No dynasty anchor; Steene is 52, succession not guaranteed.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **iShares MSCI India** — direct exposure to the same SME-succession wave that drives Constellation/Vitec/Topicus, earlier-stage, less covered, far cheaper. A structural-model bet, not a geographic bet. **Conviction: 4/5** — strongest risk/reward in this lane now.

## GEM 2: ADDNODE GROUP (ANOD-B.ST)
- **Ticker:** ANOD-B.ST (Nasdaq Stockholm Main) | **Country:** Sweden (global ops) | **Mkt cap:** SEK ~6.3bn (~€540m) | **Stage:** Mature mid-cap software compounder at a deep discount to Nordic software peers.

**Structural wave & why under-covered:** Serial acquirer of mission-critical niche software at the intersection of (1) AEC/BIM digitalisation, (2) PLM for industrial manufacturers, (3) Nordic public-sector digital transformation — regulatory/productivity-driven, multi-decade. Awkward classification gap (too industrial for tech analysts, too software for capital-goods desks). ABG upgraded to BUY Feb 2025 noting a 28% discount to Nordic M&A compounder peers vs a 12% 5-yr-avg discount.

**Moat:** #1 global Autodesk Solution Provider (Symetri + Microdesk) — Autodesk deeply embedded in every construction firm; switching requires retraining thousands of engineers. Process Management division serves Nordic municipalities (near-infinite switching costs). 63% recurring revenue (2025). 84 acquisitions since 2003, 90%+ claimed success, 8-person HQ; pipeline of 50+ targets. SolidCAD acquired at implied ~5–7x.

**Ownership:** Aretro Capital (Staffan Hanstorp, co-founder) ~17.5% votes / ~5.4% capital; dual-class anchor. Institutions: Alecta 12.2%, Swedbank Robur 9.8%, SEB 7.1%, AP4 5.4%.

**Economics:** Revenue FY2025 SEK 5.79bn; EBITA SEK 903m (15.6% margin, up from 11.1% in 2024); Q4 2025 EBITA margin 19.1%. Recurring 63%. 10 acquisitions in 2025 (+SEK ~700m sales). EV/EBIT 13.3x; P/E 15.4x; EV/EBITDA 9.3x. Net cash position SEK ~2.23bn (verify treatment of leases/deferred consideration). ROIC 8.79% (goodwill-laden — bear case); ROE 15.0%. New targets: EBITA growth ≥15%, margin ≥17%, ND ≤2.5x EBITDA.

**Valuation:** P/E 15.4x trailing; EV/EBITDA 9.3x — anomalously cheap for a software compounder (Vitec ~25–35x, Topicus ~40–50x, Lagercrantz ~30–35x). Discount drivers: Autodesk dependency, US integration drag, goodwill-suppressed ROIC, low media profile.

**10-yr bull case:** EBITA margin to 17–20% as US matures and recurring mix rises; 12–15% revenue CAGR funded by net cash. By 2035: ~SEK 20bn revenue, 18% EBITA = SEK 3.6bn; at 20x = SEK 72bn vs SEK 6.3bn today = **11x**, more on re-rating.

**Risks:** (1) Autodesk disintermediation (going direct-to-customer). (2) US integration drag 2–3 yrs. (3) Reported ROIC may not recover if deals overpriced.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** or complement **Vitec** — broader, cheaper, more global version of Vitec, plus physical-world digitalisation (BIM/PLM). **Conviction: 3.5/5.**

## GEM 3: MOMENTUM GROUP (MMGR-B.ST)
- **Ticker:** MMGR-B.ST | **Country:** Sweden | **Mkt cap:** SEK ~6.0bn (~€510m) | **Stage:** Independent since 2022 (spun from Alligo); industrial distributor/acquirer, down ~30% TTM.

**Structural wave & why under-covered:** (1) Industrial aftermarket parts consolidation (Momentum ~5% of fragmented Nordic market = decades of runway); (2) predictable MRO demand (non-discretionary). Under-covered: only independent since 2022, recent Nordstjernan mandatory-bid uncertainty, −30% price drop scared momentum capital, "boring distribution" bucket.

**Moat:** 750,000+ SKU inventory (90+ years to build); mission-critical replacement parts where delivery speed > price; **EBITA/Working Capital 58%** (hallmark of distribution pricing power); only ~5% Sweden / ~2% other Nordics share = huge runway; acquires at 5–7x EBITA (6 deals 2025, +SEK ~300m); 3-person HQ (Constellation-style).

**Ownership:** Nordstjernan (Ax:son Johnson family) ~15% at listing, selling down but significant. CEO Ulf Lilius bought 97,470 B shares avg SEK 109.83 (~SEK 10.7m). Hedelius/Börjesson families hold A-shares (voting control).

**Economics:** Revenue FY2025 SEK 3.10bn (+8%); EBITA SEK 337m (+5%); margin 10.9% (target 12–13%+); organic −2% (industrial slowdown); EBITA/WC 58%; EPS SEK 3.80; net debt SEK 344m; equity/assets 36%. Mkt cap SEK ~6.0bn; share ~SEK 121 (−30% TTM). P/E ~32x; EV/EBITA ~18.8x. Doubled EBITA target within 4 yrs of listing, 1 yr early.

**10-yr bull case:** Cycle recovers; organic 3–5%; acquisitions +5–10%/yr; margins to 13%+. By 2035: SEK 8–10bn revenue, 13% margin = SEK 1.0–1.3bn EBITA; at 18x = SEK 18–23bn vs SEK 6bn = **3–4x** base, 4–5x at 22x.

**Risks:** (1) Organic growth deficit if Nordic industry stagnates; rising acquisition multiples. (2) Nordstjernan overhang (selling). (3) E-commerce disruption of distributors (10-yr).

**Portfolio fit:** Blind-spot filler — no Nordic industrial MRO/distribution in the 15. Could take from **VinaCapital Vietnam**. **Conviction: 3/5.**

## WATCH-LIST: LAGERCRANTZ (LAGR-B.ST)
Revenue FY2025/26 SEK 10.6bn (+13%); EBITA margin 18.1%→20%; ROE 29%; 8 acquisitions + 4 post-year-end; EPS +18% to SEK 5.81. P/E sources conflict (30–36x quoted vs derived ~40–50x trailing — **flagged**). Dispersed ownership, no family anchor. Superb but priced for perfection; needs 15%+ EPS growth forever. Not a hidden gem.

## Screened but rejected

- **Judges Scientific (JDG.L):** Pays ~5x EBIT for instrument makers, ~25% margin; but well-covered in UK, US research-funding cuts a headwind.
- **Cicor (CICN.SW):** EMS manufacturer, EBITDA margin only 10.9%; not a pricing-power acquirer.
- **Storskogen:** structural leverage problems, avoid.
