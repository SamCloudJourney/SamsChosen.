## CANDIDATE 1: AURUBIS (NDA.DE)
- Germany | Frankfurt / OTC AIAGF | ~€9.3B (~$10.2B) | Mature, capex inflection.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Simultaneously the world's largest copper recycler and Europe's indispensable copper smelter (~1.2Mt cathode-equivalent/yr from concentrates + complex scrap). Electrification lifts both concentrate and scrap feedstock; the EU Critical Raw Materials Act forces European manufacturers to source domestically recycled copper — which only Aurubis can fill at scale. Under-covered: German mid-cap, 10x earnings, seen as a "toll smelter" not a tech-enabled recycler; Richmond VA greenfield (first new US copper smelter in 40+ yrs) not yet in consensus.

**Moat:** Recovers 20+ metals from a single input (copper, gold, silver, PGMs, selenium, tellurium, bismuth) → implicit "negative cost" on some inputs as by-product credits exceed smelting cost; no Asian competitor can replicate without decades of metallurgy + the permit footprint. Network effects in European scrap sourcing (multi-year contracts); EU state-aid-cleared Complex Recycling Hamburg expansion = decade-long permitting barrier; embedded precious-metals call option; TC/RC hedge (recycling compensates when concentrate TCs tighten).

**Ownership:** Salzgitter AG ~22–25% (post bond conversion, unverified); free-float institutions ~39% (BlackRock, Vanguard); individuals ~25%. (Curiosity: Salzgitter's Aurubis stake reportedly worth more than Salzgitter's whole market cap.)

**Economics (FY2024/25):** Revenue €18.2B; operating EBT €355M; operating EBITDA €589M; FY2025/26 EBT guidance (upgraded May 2026) €425–525M; operating ROCE 8.1% (depressed by capex; target 10–12%); P/E ~10x; EV/EBITDA ~8.4x; div yield ~0.78%; incremental EBITDA ~€260M/yr from new projects from FY2028/29.

**Valuation:** ~€213/share = 10x earnings, 8.4x EV/EBITDA (10-yr median P/E ~9.5x). Market ignores ~€260M/yr incremental EBITDA (worth €1.5–2.5B NPV on current multiples), copper-price upside, recycled-content mandates, and the precious-metals option.

**10-yr bull:** Copper structurally to $5–6/lb; Richmond captures US onshoring premium; EU scrap mandates = captive regulated moat; +€260M EBITDA pushes EBT to €700–900M; re-rate 10x→14x → ~3x.

**Risks:** (1) Copper-price collapse in recession ($3/lb). (2) Richmond execution (limited track record; US trade-policy volatility). (3) Cyberattack/operational disruption (2022 precedent).

**Portfolio fit:** Replace one holdco (**EXOR** or **Investor AB**) — fills the real-asset/commodity-infrastructure blind spot with a quality toll-road on European copper flows and pricing power. **Conviction: 4/5.**

## CANDIDATE 2: LYNAS RARE EARTHS (LYC.AX)
- Australia (ops Malaysia/Australia) | ASX | ~A$19.3B (~US$12.5B) | Growth/scale-up.

**Wave & under-coverage:** Only scaled Western-aligned NdPr (EV-motor/wind/defense magnet material) processor outside China (~90% Chinese processing). China's Apr-2025 rare-earth export restrictions made Lynas a critical national-security asset for multiple Western governments. ASX-listed, no US ADR critical mass; pigeonholed as a volatile commodity stock.

**Moat:** Only scaled Western NdPr producer (LAMP Kuantan processes Mount Weld concentrate); 15+ yrs metallurgical IP; Mount Weld world's highest-grade deposit (20+ yr life); US DoD $258M for Seadrift TX heavy-RE separation; Malaysia renewed LAMP licence 10 yrs (to 2036); DoD offtake floor A$110/kg NdPr.

**Ownership:** CEO Amanda Lacaze (rescued from near-bankruptcy 2015), aligned non-founder; institutional (Australian super funds + global resource funds); effectively co-owned by Western governments via soft governance.

**Economics:** Revenue FY2025 A$556.5M (+20%); TTM A$715.9M (+48%); EBITDA A$101M (18% margin); net profit A$8m (near-zero, heavy reinvestment); NdPr 6,555t (+18%); price US$44–55/kg; cash A$166.5M; P/E ~224x TTM / ~39x fwd (FY2027E); ROIC ~0.25% (capex-depressed). A$750m raise Aug 2025 + A$1.5B+ capex (Kalgoorlie, Texas, Malaysia) suppress GAAP earnings.

**Valuation:** A$19/share, 39x fwd — not cheap; correct framing is optionality + throughput. NdPr at US$80–100/kg → EBITDA A$400–600M on same volumes (4–6x uplift, near-zero incremental cost).

**10-yr bull:** China restrictions persist; OEMs/defense pay a strategic premium; NdPr recovers to US$80–100/kg; Kalgoorlie becomes largest non-Chinese RE hub; Texas heavy-RE; revenue 3–4x; margins back to 40%+; re-rate 8x→15–20x EV/EBITDA = 5–10x.

**Risks:** (1) NdPr price crash (China floods market; entire bull case depends on elevated NdPr). (2) Texas Seadrift looks "unlikely to proceed" under current US politics. (3) Simultaneous-ramp execution complexity.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **Bachem** or **Sartorius** — "indispensable process bottleneck" thesis in a more defensible geopolitical chain, but more commodity/price risk. **Conviction: 3/5.**

## CANDIDATE 3: URANIUM ROYALTY CORP / NEW URC (UROY)
- Canada-domiciled, US-listed | ~$520M pre-deal; post-deal EV ~$2.5–3B | Development-to-royalty inflection.

**Wave:** Nuclear renaissance (AI data centers, defense energy security, net-zero); uranium $85/lb (May 2026) vs $25 in 2020. Only pure-play uranium royalty on NASDAQ. **Moat:** first-mover monopoly in uranium royalties; capital-light (90%+ margins at scale); Apr-2026 Sweetwater combination (Orion + Ontario Teachers, ~$1.9B EV) transforms it into the largest US non-precious-metals royalty company.

**Economics:** Revenue TTM $40.25M; EBITDA $1.84M (pre-scale); cash $102M; negligible debt; Sweetwater EV ~$1.9B; physical uranium held. **Risks:** (1) Massive dilution — current holders → 41%; $813m new shares at $3.64 transfers most value to Orion/Teachers. (2) Sweetwater royalty quality uncertain (idle Wyoming mines). (3) Uranium mean-reversion to $60–70.

**Portfolio fit:** Replace **India ETF** as a uranium call option with royalty protection — but dilution/early-stage risk. **Conviction: 3/5.**

## CANDIDATE 4: ECORA ROYALTIES (ECOR.L)
- UK-listed | ~£100–150M | Producing royalty portfolio, scale-up.

Only LSE-listed royalty company repositioned around electrification minerals (copper ~50% of asset value, cobalt, nickel, uranium, RE). Post-restructuring (sold fossil-fuel royalties 2024–25, renamed from Anglo Pacific). **Moat:** diversified royalties (Mantos, Voisey's Bay, Caserones copper; Voisey's Bay cobalt stream; Denison uranium; Rainbow Rare Earths Phalaborwa — first royalty on a commercial RE-hydroxide producer outside China). Net debt ~$104M (−30% since Q1). **Economics:** H1 2025 portfolio contribution $17.9m (Kestrel coal timing-distorted; H2-weighted); base-metals +81% YoY; EBITDA guided 2x by 2027; ~50% discount to NAV (excl growth projects). **Risks:** Kestrel coal legacy (un-investable for ESG mandates); sub-£200M illiquidity; development-stage royalties (5–8 yr wait). **Conviction: 2/5** — better as a 2–3% starter than a full slot.
