## 1. BACHEM (SIX: BANB)

**FY2025 actuals:** Net sales CHF 695.1m (CHF 605.3m prior year) = +14.8% reported, **+19.2% in local currencies**. EBITDA CHF 214.7m (margin 30.9%). Net profit CHF 148.8m (+24%).

**FY2026 guidance:** "Full-year 2026 sales growth of **35% to 45% in local currencies**, with EBITDA margin in the low-30% range." The 35–45% figure in the second report is accurate, not an error.

**The ">CHF 1bn" question:** The skeptic claimed this is a medium-term ambition, not a 2026 target — this is **partly wrong**. Reputable reporting states Bachem "aims for annual sales of more than CHF 1bn and EBITDA margin over 30% **in 2026**." Mechanically consistent: CHF 695m × 1.45 ≈ CHF 1.0bn, so >CHF 1bn is the top end of the same 35–45% 2026 guidance, driven by the Building K ramp-up (first phase Swissmedic-inspected end-2025). It is an aspirational 2026 figure contingent on the upper end of guidance — not a separate, later "medium-term" target. The skeptic's broader point that 35–45% is extreme for a CDMO is valid context: press flagged single-facility (Building K) execution risk in this target.

**Verdict:** Guidance 35–45% and ~+19% LC growth **CONFIRMED**. ">CHF 1bn as a medium-term-only ambition" **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED** — it is presented as a 2026 ambition (top of guidance).

## 2. SARTORIUS (Sartorius AG / Sartorius Stedim Biotech)

**No profit warning was issued.** Q1 2026 (reported 23 Apr 2026): group sales **€899m, +7.5% constant currency** (+1.8% reported). CEO Michael Grosse **confirmed full-year 2026 guidance**: group sales growth **~5–9% cc**, underlying EBITDA margin slightly above 30%. Divisions: Bioprocess Solutions ~6–10%, Lab Products & Services ~2–6%.

The "profit warning" claim is **REFUTED**: guidance was explicitly confirmed. Press framed it as a solid start, with the soft reported number driven by FX and an underlying-net-profit dip vs prior year — consistent with the counter-claim that any disappointment was a miss-vs-consensus, not a guidance cut.

**The ~+9.6% constant-currency claim is REFUTED/mislabeled.** Q1 2026 group cc growth was **+7.5%**, not 9.6%. (9.6% does not match group, BPS at 8.1%, or LPS at 4.9%; could not source it.)

## 3. ECKERT & ZIEGLER (ETR: EUZ)

**FY2025 actuals (EQS-News):**
- Revenue: **€312.0m** (€295.8m prior year) = **+5%** ✓ matches ~€312m anchor.
- Net income: **€48.8m** vs €33.3m = **+46%** ✓
- EPS: **€0.78** (vs €0.53 split-adjusted) ✓
- Adjusted EBIT: €77.7m, +18%.

**The +46% vs +22% dispute:** Reported **net income** growth is **+46%**. The +22% figure does not match net income; it likely reflects a different metric (e.g. reported/operating EBIT or an adjusted-earnings line). **Net-income +46% CONFIRMED.**

**Segment sum:** Segments do **NOT** sum to the group total. Medical €171.3m + Isotope Products €150.1m = **€321.4m**, exceeding the €312.0m group revenue by ~€9.4m. Expected — group revenue is after intersegment eliminations — but the literal claim "segments sum to the total" is **REFUTED**.

## 4. BIOVENTIX (LON: BVXP)

**Tau/neuro revenue — both figures correct for different periods/metrics:**
- **FY2025 (year ended 30 Jun 2025):** total Tau/neuro SMA sales **~£605k** (up ~4x from £155k), split ~£530k physical antibody sales + ~£75k royalties.
- **H1 FY2026 (six months to 31 Dec 2025):** Tau/neuro **royalties** rose ~5x to **£150k** (vs £30k prior half). Half-year royalty progression: £30k → £45k → £150k.

So £605k is a full-year **total** Tau figure and £150k is a half-year **royalty-only** figure — **CONFIRMED** different periods and revenue lines. (Note: £605k is full-year total, not full-year royalties.)

**Share price / drawdown / yield (late May 2026):**
- Price **~£16.75–17.00 (1,675–1,700p)** ✓ **CONFIRMED**.
- Drawdown: **−44.5%** from a 52-week high of ~3,019p (£30.20) ✓ **CONFIRMED**.
- Dividend yield: total FY2025 dividend 150p; at 1,675p that is **~8.8–9.0%**, not 6%. The **~6% claim is REFUTED for the current price** — the 6%+ yield was accurate earlier (Oct 2025, ~£24). At today's lower price the yield has risen to ~9%.
