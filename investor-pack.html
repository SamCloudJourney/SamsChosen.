03 Yield Model & Worked Deal

The headline: 8%+ is realistically a South Wales story — Cardiff (Cathays/Roath, CF24) and Swansea (SA1) — not Bristol, where flat yields sit around 4.5–6%. And remember gross ≠ net: on a fully-owned freehold block you bear all the communal costs a single-flat landlord never sees, so net typically lands at 55–70% of gross.

How to read a block's yield

Gross yield = annual rent ÷ (price + acquisition costs). Net yield = (annual rent − annual running costs) ÷ (price + acquisition costs). Costs to load on a block: management (8–15%), maintenance (10–15%), sinking/reserve fund (5–10%), voids (~8%), buildings insurance, communal utilities/cleaning/grounds, and statutory compliance (gas, EICR, fire-risk assessment, EPCs).

04 Blocks — Due-Diligence Checklist

Title & tenancies

  • Confirm freehold at Land Registry — title plan, class, restrictions, covenants, rights of way
  • Full tenancy schedule: ASTs (you keep all rent) vs units sold off on long leases (you collect ground rent/service charge but fund communal works)
  • Rent roll, arrears, deposit-protection status

Building & fire safety

  • Building Safety Act: "relevant building" ≥11m or ≥5 storeys; "higher-risk" ≥18m / ≥7 storeys. Most 6–8 unit blocks are <11m — confirm height
  • EWS1 only where combustible cladding/balconies — as freeholder you may own the remediation liability
  • Fire Safety Act 2021: you become the Responsible Person for common parts — current Fire Risk Assessment required (breach = unlimited fine)

Licensing & energy

  • Purpose-built self-contained flats aren't HMOs; converted blocks may be — check council additional/selective licensing (Bristol citywide additional HMO; Cardiff Cathays + Article 4)
  • Wales: mandatory Rent Smart Wales registration/licensing
  • EPC/MEES: minimum E now; EPC C confirmed for PRS by 1 Oct 2030 — price cost-to-C across every unit (could be £60k+ on a block)

Financial & physical

  • 3 years' service-charge accounts & reserve-fund balance
  • Insurance schedule + claims history; managing-agent contract
  • Asbestos survey (pre-2000), structural survey of roof/communal systems, gas & electrical certs for all units

06 The 15-Day Cash Completion

Standard auction completion is 20 working days / 28 days. 15 days is achievable only with cash, a pre-instructed auction-specialist conveyancer, and reliance on search-indemnity insurance instead of fresh local searches (the local-authority search is the usual bottleneck).

The stack

  • Search indemnity (No-Search / Local Authority Search Indemnity) — ~£20–£300. Providers: First Title, Stewart Title, CLS
  • Auction-specialist conveyancers: SAM Conveyancing, JMW, Stephensons, Cunningtons, My Home Move
  • Cash or pre-arranged bridging — no mortgage valuation delay (the usual long pole)
  • AML / source-of-funds done early so funds are ready to send

Fire-specific title traps

  • Live insurance claim: has it settled? Did the insurer pay the previous owner? Any subrogation/retained interest? Don't buy a shell whose payout has already gone
  • Existing charge/mortgagee must be redeemed at completion; if an LPA receiver is selling, confirm their authority
  • Indemnity protects you financially — it does not tell you about planning, road schemes or practical defects
Trade-off to accept: a 15-day, search-indemnity completion means proceeding on less information. Price that risk in, and review the legal pack in advance with your conveyancer before you bid.

07 Fire-Damage Due-Diligence Checklist

  • Structural integrity — independent structural survey; fire can compromise joists, lintels, roof structure
  • Scope of damage — map fire vs smoke vs water (firefighting) damage; the last two spread far beyond the burn
  • Asbestos — survey on any pre-2000 building before works
  • Reinstatement vs purchase — reinstatement = rebuild cost (not market value) and on modest/older stock often exceeds market value. Commission a RICS Reinstatement Cost Assessment
  • Mortgageability/insurability — fire-damaged shells are usually unmortgageable; you'll need unoccupied/renovation insurance from day one
  • Planning & building control — confirm what's needed to reinstate; check for unauthorised prior works
  • Exit decision up front — flip (sell on, refurbished) vs refurb-to-rent. Purchase + full rebuild must leave margin under end value (GDV)

Finding the owner directly (for off-market approaches)

  • Land Registry: property summary (free) → title register £7 (owner name, price paid, charges). Search-of-index-map £8; proprietor-name search (PN1) £15
  • CCOD / OCOD (free, account-gated): bulk data of every UK / overseas company-owned title — filter BS / CF / NP / SA postcodes for company-owned blocks
  • Companies House (free): from the SPV → directors + Persons with Significant Control + registered office; officer search reveals the owner's whole portfolio
  • Compliant outreach: addressed post is the route (legitimate interests; screen against MPS). Avoid cold call/text/email to individuals without consent (PECR)
